📅 TODAY IN SULABHAM | 1 Nov 2026

🗓️ Alfred Wegener — the man who said continents move
Father of the Continental Drift theory and 'Pangaea' — the base of every Geography question on plate tectonics.
⚠️ Exam trap: Wegener was a meteorologist, not a geologist. Continental drift (1912/1915) and plate tectonics (1960s) are not the same theory — Wegener did not give the plate mechanism; he could not explain the moving force.

📰 Ganga Water Treaty with Bangladesh ends on 12 December — what next?
The 30-year Farakka water-sharing treaty of 1996 has no automatic extension; Bangladesh is pressing India for a new deal.
⚠️ Exam trap: Farakka Barrage (1975) is older than the treaty (1996). The 1996 treaty is for 30 years, not 25 or 50; it covers only the Ganga — not the Teesta, whose deal has been pending since 2011.

📰 2026 ED138: a possible sixth dwarf planet beyond Pluto
The new Vera C. Rubin Observatory has found its first dwarf-planet candidate — a good moment to revise 'planet vs dwarf planet'.
⚠️ Exam trap: Being round is not enough to be a planet — a planet must also clear its orbital neighbourhood. Ceres is a dwarf planet in the asteroid belt, not beyond Neptune. 2026 ED138 is only a candidate, not yet the sixth recognised dwarf planet.
