🔑 *Answers — Daily Quiz* | 1 NOV 2026
1-(a) · 2-(c) · 3-(b) · 4-(d) · 5-(a) · 6-(b) · 7-(c) · 8-(b) · 9-(a) · 10-(c)

📖 Explanations:
1. (a) III is wrong: the first linguistic State is Andhra State (1 Oct 1953); Andhra Pradesh (1956) came later. Nehru's announcement (19 Dec 1952) came four days after Sriramulu died (15 Dec 1952).
2. (c) I is wrong: the SRC was chaired by Fazl Ali; H.N. Kunzru and K.M. Panikkar were members. The Gentlemen's Agreement allowed the merger in 1956 instead of 1961.
3. (b) III is wrong: Wegener died in 1930. Plate tectonics was developed in the 1960s by others, building on his ideas.
4. (d) II and III are incorrect: he could not explain the moving force, so most geologists rejected the theory by 1930; and he was a meteorologist and geophysicist, not a geologist. I is correct.
5. (a) II is wrong: the ban was issued by AP's Commissioner of Food Safety under Section 30(2)(a) of the FSS Act, 2006 and applies to AP. FSSAI had issued only a public notice (22 Apr 2026) on not calling cheese analogues 'paneer'.
6. (b) I is wrong: FSSAI comes under the Union Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, not Consumer Affairs.
7. (c) II is wrong: the Farakka Barrage opened in 1975, more than 20 years before the 1996 treaty.
8. (b) II is wrong: the Joint Rivers Commission was set up in 1972, long before the 1996 treaty.
9. (a) II is wrong: it is only a candidate — size and shape need confirmation. IV is wrong: it is a trans-Neptunian object in the Kuiper Belt.
10. (c) II is wrong: Ceres is in the asteroid belt (between Mars and Jupiter). IV is wrong: after 2006 there are 8 planets; Pluto is a dwarf planet.
