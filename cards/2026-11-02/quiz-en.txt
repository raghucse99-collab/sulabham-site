📖 Explanations:
1. (a) III is wrong: it is 2 November; 3 May is World Press Freedom Day.
2. (a) II is wrong: in about 9 out of 10 cases killers go unpunished. IV is wrong: press freedom is read into Article 19(1)(a); the words are not in the text.
3. (c) II is wrong: Shaw got the Nobel in 1925; 1913 is Tagore's Nobel year.
4. (d) Widowers' Houses 1892 → Arms and the Man 1894 → Pygmalion 1913 → Saint Joan 1923.
5. (b) II is wrong: names are not repeated — a used name is retired.
6. (a) Deep depression 50–61 → Cyclonic storm 62–88 → Severe 89–117 → Very severe 118–166 km/h. Murasu became 'severe' on 2 Nov.
7. (c) II is wrong: municipal polls are conducted by the State Election Commission (Art 243ZA), here headed by Anil Chandra Punetha.
8. (d) 243K = SEC (panchayats); 243ZA = SEC (municipalities); 243U = 5-year term; 243Q = nagar panchayat / municipal council / municipal corporation.
9. (b) II is wrong: the Court only issued notice to the Centre (next hearing 17 Nov); it had refused a stay earlier, in February 2026.
10. (c) 8(2) = public-interest override; Section 18 = Data Protection Board; Puttaswamy = privacy under Article 21; 44(3) = amends 8(1)(j).
