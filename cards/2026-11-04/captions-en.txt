📅 TODAY IN SULABHAM | 4 Nov 2026

🗓️ Dr B.R. Ambedkar introduces the Draft Constitution (1948)
On this day in 1948 Ambedkar formally placed the Draft Constitution before the Constituent Assembly — the start of the long debate that gave India its Constitution.
⚠️ Exam trap: Three dates, three events: 21 Feb 1948 = Draft submitted to the Assembly President; 4 Nov 1948 = Draft introduced in the Assembly; 26 Nov 1949 = Constitution adopted. B.N. Rau was the adviser, not a committee member.

📅 TODAY IN SULABHAM | 4 Nov 2026

🗓️ UNESCO turns 80
UNESCO's Constitution entered into force on 4 November 1946 — the UN body for education, science and culture is 80 years old today.
⚠️ Exam trap: Two dates: November 1945 = Constitution signed in London; 4 November 1946 = Constitution came into force (the date counted for the 80th anniversary). First DG = Julian Huxley; El-Enany is the 12th, after Audrey Azoulay.

📅 TODAY IN SULABHAM | 4 Nov 2026

📰 VB-G RAM G: rural job complaints must be settled in 15 days
New grievance rules under the law that replaced MGNREGA give rural workers a time-bound, digital way to complain about denied work or late wages.
⚠️ Exam trap: Two different '15 days': (1) work must be given within 15 days of demand, else unemployment allowance; (2) a grievance must be resolved within 15 days. The enquiry limit is 7 working days. And 125 days is per household, not per person.

📅 TODAY IN SULABHAM | 4 Nov 2026

📰 Census 2027: house-to-house count from 25 January, caste for all
MHA has notified the dates for the Population Enumeration phase; for the first time since Independence, the caste of every community will be recorded.
⚠️ Exam trap: Census is in the Union List (entry 69), not the Concurrent List. The reference date is 1 March 2027 even though door-to-door counting ends on 28 February. 16th Census overall, but only the 8th after Independence. Aadhaar is asked but optional.
