🔑 *Answers — Daily Quiz* | 4 NOV 2026
1-(a) · 2-(c) · 3-(b) · 4-(a) · 5-(b) · 6-(d) · 7-(d) · 8-(a) · 9-(c) · 10-(c)

📖 Explanations:
1. (a) III is wrong: clause-by-clause debate ran from 15 Nov 1948 to 17 Oct 1949; the Constitution was adopted on 26 Nov 1949.
2. (c) III is wrong: B.L. Mitter was replaced by N. Madhava Rau. K.M. Munshi was one of the original seven members.
3. (b) III is wrong: the first DG was Julian Huxley (UK, 1946–48). Audrey Azoulay was the DG before Khaled El-Enany.
4. (a) Only III is incorrect: the General Conference meets every two years. I, II and IV are correct.
5. (b) II is wrong: the 125-day guarantee is per rural household, not per individual.
6. (d) IV is wrong: no FIR is needed — complaints can be oral or written, through any channel. (The FIR clause in the draft was even dropped.)
7. (d) IV is wrong: Census is a Union List subject (entry 69); the Census itself is done by the Union with State/UT governments implementing it.
8. (a) III is wrong: the reference date ('census moment') is 00:00 hours on 1 March 2027, the day after door-to-door counting ends.
9. (c) III is wrong: only two shipyards are involved — GRSE (5) and Goa Shipyard (3). Mazagon Dock has no share.
10. (c) II is wrong: the yards were chosen through competitive bidding — the first such project. III: 460 lakh person-days in ancillary/MSMEs vs 72 lakh in shipyards. IV: CCS cleared it on 28 Oct, contracts on 2 Nov.
