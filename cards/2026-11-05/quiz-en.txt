🔑 *Answers — Daily Quiz* | 5 NOV 2026
1-(b) · 2-(a) · 3-(d) · 4-(a) · 5-(c) · 6-(a) · 7-(c) · 8-(d) · 9-(a) · 10-(b)

📖 Explanations:
1. (b) I is wrong: he went to England for the ICS but did not qualify, and became a barrister instead.
2. (a) Boycott 1921 → Gaya Dec 1922 → Swaraj Party 1923 → Mayor 1924. The trap: the party came AFTER Gaya rejected council entry.
3. (d) II is wrong: Japan proposed it (with 142 countries); 5 November comes from Japan's 1854 'Inamura-no-hi'.
4. (a) II is wrong: waves are low only in deep sea; near the coast they rise high and rush inland within minutes.
5. (c) III is wrong: 77 ZPTC seats were unanimous, so polling is for 582 of 659.
6. (a) Only II is incorrect: 243ZA is the SEC for municipalities (74th Amendment); for panchayats it is 243K.
7. (c) II is wrong: 9.28% is GVA growth of the corporate manufacturing sector, not GDP growth.
8. (d) GVA = 100 − 70 = 30; NVA = GVA − depreciation = 25. III is wrong: depreciation is subtracted, not added.
9. (a) III is wrong: the $300 billion-a-year goal (NCQG) was set at COP29 in Azerbaijan in 2024.
10. (b) II and IV are wrong: India opposes fossil-fuel phase-out language and new top-down mitigation targets.
