2. *Consensus* — ఏకాభిప్రాయం (general agreement of all members, without a vote)
   “The UN General Assembly adopted World Tsunami Awareness Day by consensus in December 2015.”
3. *Unanimous* — ఏకగ్రీవం (agreed by everyone; in elections, won without a contest)
   “In Andhra Pradesh, 2,051 MPTC seats were declared unanimous before polling.”
4. *Depreciation* — తరుగుదల (fall in the value of machines and buildings through use and age)
   “Net Value Added is Gross Value Added minus depreciation.”
5. *Mitigation* — ఉపశమన చర్యలు (ఉద్గారాల తగ్గింపు) (steps to reduce the cause of a problem, e.g. cutting greenhouse gas emissions)
   “India opposes new top-down mitigation targets at COP31.”
