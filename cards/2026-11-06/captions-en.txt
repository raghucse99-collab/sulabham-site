🗓️ Gandhi's Great March in South Africa (1913)
On this day in 1913, Gandhi led over 2,000 Indian workers from Natal into the Transvaal against the £3 tax — the climax of his South African satyagraha.
⚠️ Exam trap: Satyagraha in South Africa ran from 1906 to 1914 — the Great March is 1913, not 1906. The Smuts–Gandhi Agreement is dated 30 June 1914; the Searle judgment (on marriages) came from the Cape Supreme Court in March 1913. Gandhi left South Africa in July 1914 and reached India in January 1915.
