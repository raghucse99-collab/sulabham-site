1-(b) · 2-(c) · 3-(a) · 4-(d) · 5-(c) · 6-(a) · 7-(d) · 8-(b) · 9-(a) · 10-(d)

📖 Explanations:
1. (b) III is wrong: the march began on 6 Nov 1913; 1906 is when satyagraha in South Africa began.
2. (c) Only I is incorrect: the Smuts–Gandhi Agreement is dated 30 June 1914, after the march.
3. (a) III is wrong: it was declared in 2001, during Kofi Annan's tenure.
4. (d) IV is wrong: no official theme has been announced for 2026.
5. (c) III is wrong: CNNS was 2016-18; 2019-21 is NFHS-5.
6. (a) III is wrong: polyphenols (tea, coffee) and calcium (milk) BLOCK iron absorption.
7. (d) III is wrong: India accepts only offset projects started after 1 January 2025.
8. (b) CBAM = EU, definitive Jan 2026; Brazil = absolute cap; Article 6.2 = transfers; summit = New Delhi. Trap: (a) swaps CBAM and Brazil.
9. (a) III is wrong: NE and Himalayan States (incl. J&K) get 90:10; 60:40 is for other States.
10. (d) II is wrong: 'Model' needs both solid and liquid waste management plus visual cleanliness.
