🗓️ Cancer Awareness Day — Marie Curie's birthday & cheaper cancer medicines
Observed since 2014 on Marie Curie's birth anniversary; from 9 Nov 2026 trade margins on 137 anti-cancer drugs are capped at 30%.
⚠️ Exam trap: National Cancer Awareness Day = 7 Nov (India); World Cancer Day = 4 Feb. Marie Curie's 1903 prize was Physics (shared), the 1911 prize was Chemistry (alone). The cap is on the trade MARGIN, not a fixed price.

📰 ₹2,814-crore scheme to process lithium and nickel in India
Cabinet (4 Nov) approved a 30% capital subsidy for four plants — the 'missing link' between mining and EV batteries.
⚠️ Exam trap: The subsidy is 30% of capital cost — not a production-linked incentive (PLI) on sales. Nickel plants are 50 kt and lithium 30 kt (don't swap). Winner = LOWEST support sought, not highest capacity.

📅 TODAY IN SULABHAM | 7 Nov 2026

📰 Internet access is a fundamental right — children included, says Supreme Court
The Court refused blanket age-based bans on children's internet use and asked for safer design instead.
⚠️ Exam trap: The right comes from Article 21 + 19(1)(a) — NOT Article 21A (education). Anuradha Bhasin (2020) did NOT declare internet access itself a fundamental right; the 2026 judgment does. The Court did not ban social media for children — it rejected a blanket ban.
