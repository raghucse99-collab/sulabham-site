📅 TODAY IN SULABHAM | 8 Nov 2026

📰 Article 22(2): the 24-hour clock starts at detention, not at 'formal arrest'
Supreme Court (5 Nov): police cannot keep a person for hours and show the arrest later to stretch the 24-hour limit.
⚠️ Exam trap: The 24 hours exclude ONLY the journey time to the magistrate — not time spent on search, seizure or paperwork. And there is no 'detention before arrest' buffer: the Court said the Constitution does not recognise detention dehors arrest.
