🗓️ Fall of the Berlin Wall (9 November 1989)
The symbol of the Cold War fell; Germany reunited on 3 October 1990 — a turning point of world history.
⚠️ Exam trap: Two different dates: the Wall FELL on 9 Nov 1989; Germany REUNIFIED on 3 Oct 1990. The Wall was built by East Germany (not by the Western Allies) and it went around West Berlin.

📰 High Court judge vacancies: how judges are appointed and why posts stay empty
Fewer judges means longer waits for justice — reports this week say vacancies have come down, but lakhs of cases are still pending.
⚠️ Exam trap: Article 224 is about ADDITIONAL and acting judges, not about the appointment of permanent judges (Article 217). Sanctioned strength ≠ working strength. The largest number of vacancies is at Allahabad (52 of 160).
