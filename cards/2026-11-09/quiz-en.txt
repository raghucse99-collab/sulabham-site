1-(b) · 2-(c) · 3-(c) · 4-(d) · 5-(a) · 6-(d) · 7-(a) · 8-(b) · 9-(d) · 10-(c)

📖 Explanations:
1. (b) I, III and IV are correct. II is wrong: Article 39A is a Directive Principle (Part IV), added by the 42nd Amendment, 1976 — not a Fundamental Right.
2. (c) I and III are correct (NALSA – CJI; DLSA – District Judge). II is wrong: the SLSA's Patron-in-Chief is the Chief Justice of the High Court, so B – 3.
3. (c) II and III are correct. I is wrong: East Germany (GDR) built it in 1961 to stop its own citizens escaping to the West.
4. (d) Correct order: II (1961) → IV (1 Nov 1989) → III (9 Nov 1989) → I (3 Oct 1990). Option (a) wrongly puts the Wall's opening before the Krenz–Gorbachev meeting; (b) puts reunification before the opening.
5. (a) I, III and IV are correct. II is wrong: 92 GWh = 60 GWh pumped storage + 30 GWh battery + 2 GWh emerging technologies.
6. (d) I and II are correct — both are storage (BESS and PSP). III is wrong: more panels add more noon power but do nothing for the evening, when the sun has set.
7. (a) I and II are incorrect. Permanent judges come under Article 217 (Article 224 is for additional judges); working strength is usually lower — on 1 July 2026 it was 781 against 1,122 sanctioned. III is correct.
8. (b) I, II and III are correct (341 < 352). IV is wrong: Meghalaya HC was at full strength; J&K and Ladakh HC had about 52% vacancy.
9. (d) I, II and IV are correct. III is wrong: associated countries contribute financially in return for equal participation.
10. (c) I and III are correct. II is wrong: non-European countries such as Japan, Canada, Korea and New Zealand are already associated.
