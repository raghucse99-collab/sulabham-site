🗓️ Battle of Pratapgad — Shivaji defeats Afzal Khan
A small Maratha force beat a big Bijapur army by using hills and forests — the turning point that made Shivaji a major power.
⚠️ Exam trap: Afzal Khan was a BIJAPUR (Adil Shahi) general, not a Mughal general. The Mughal general who forced the Treaty of Purandar (1665) was Jai Singh I. Do not confuse Pratapgad (1659) with Purandar (1665).

📰 COP31 in Antalya: one host, two presidencies — and India's asks on climate money
The yearly UN climate summit decides who pays and how fast the world cuts emissions — it shapes India's energy, farming and coastal safety, including AP's.
⚠️ Exam trap: COP31's host and COP President is Türkiye; Australia only leads the negotiations — they are NOT co-presidents. And the US$300 bn goal is from COP29 (Baku), not COP30 (Belém).

📅 TODAY IN SULABHAM | 10 Nov 2026

📰 Supreme Court: growing a commercial crop is not a 'forest right' in a tiger reserve
The Forest Rights Act protects genuine forest dwellers — the Court says planned cash-crop farming in a critical tiger habitat does not qualify.
⚠️ Exam trap: OTFDs need 3 generations = 75 years before 13 Dec 2005; forest-dwelling STs do NOT need the 75-year test. The FRA was enacted in 2006 but came into force only on 31 Dec 2007.

📰 Cancer medicines cheaper: trade margins capped, 1,364 formulations get lower MRPs
Cancer treatment pushes many families into debt; capping the middlemen's margin cuts prices without a tax change.
