🔑 *Answers — Daily Quiz* | 10 NOV 2026
1-(c) · 2-(a) · 3-(d) · 4-(b) · 5-(b) · 6-(d) · 7-(a) · 8-(c) · 9-(d) · 10-(b)

📖 Explanations:
1. (c) I and III are correct. II is wrong twice: it was proclaimed by UNESCO, and in 2001; 2002 was the first celebration.
2. (a) World Science Day = 10 Nov; National Science Day (Raman Effect) = 28 Feb; UNESCO Constitution in force = 4 Nov 1946 (it was signed in Nov 1945 — the trap).
3. (d) Only I is incorrect: Afzal Khan was a general of Ali Adil Shah II of Bijapur, not of the Mughals. II, III and IV are correct.
4. (b) Pratapgad (10 Nov 1659) → Panhala (28 Nov 1659, just 18 days later) → Purandar (1665) → Raigad coronation (1674). Trap: Panhala is placed after Purandar in option (a).
5. (b) I and II are correct. III is wrong: India withdrew its COP33 bid by a letter to the UNFCCC dated 2 April 2026.
6. (d) I, III and IV are India's positions. II is the opposite: India wants the Global Goal on Adaptation indicators to stay voluntary and NOT become conditions for finance.
7. (a) I and III are correct. II is wrong: the Court said claims must be decided strictly under Section 6 (Gram Sabha first), 'not merely on the basis of present occupation'.
8. (c) II and III are correct. 1960 to 2005 is only 45 years — less than the 75 years (3 generations) required for OTFDs, so I is wrong. Under Section 6, the Gram Sabha is the first tier.
9. (d) I, II and IV are correct. III is wrong: the 137 are NON-scheduled anti-cancer drugs; prices fell through trade-margin capping (DPCO 2013, Para 19), not NLEM ceiling prices.
10. (b) I, II and IV are correct. III is wrong: NPPA was set up on 29 August 1997; 2013 is the year of the DPCO it enforces.
