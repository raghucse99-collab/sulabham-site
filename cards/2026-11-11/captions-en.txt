🗓️ Maulana Abul Kalam Azad — National Education Day
India's first Education Minister. His birthday, 11 November, is observed as National Education Day since 2008.
⚠️ Exam trap: Do not mix National Education Day (11 Nov, Azad, India) with the UN's International Day of Education (24 Jan) or Teachers' Day (5 Sep). Bharat Ratna to Azad came in 1992 — not in his lifetime. 'India Wins Freedom' came out in 1959, after his death.

🗓️ Armistice Day — the guns of World War I fall silent
At the 11th hour of the 11th day of the 11th month of 1918, fighting in World War I stopped.
⚠️ Exam trap: Armistice (11 Nov 1918, Compiègne) ≠ Treaty of Versailles (28 June 1919, peace treaty). Armistice only stopped the fighting. Also: the armistice was signed at 5 a.m.; 11 a.m. is when it came into effect.

📅 TODAY IN SULABHAM | 11 Nov 2026

📰 Census on hold in Manipur till 30 April 2027: what the High Court said
The Census is a Union subject run under the Census Act, 1948. In Manipur it is paused after protests linked to an NRC demand.
⚠️ Exam trap: Census is a Union subject, not State or Concurrent. Census law = Census Act, 1948 (not 1951 or 1955). NRC comes under the Citizenship Act, 1955 — it is not part of the Census Act. The hold is till 30 April 2027, not 'cancelled'.

📰 Villages without 4G down 75% in four years: the BSNL 4G saturation project
Villages without 4G fell from 45,180 in 2022 to 11,372 now — the gaps that remain are in hilly, forest and tribal areas.
