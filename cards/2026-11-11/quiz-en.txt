🔑 *Answers — Daily Quiz* | 11 NOV 2026
1-(b) · 2-(a) · 3-(c) · 4-(d) · 5-(c) · 6-(c) · 7-(d) · 8-(b) · 9-(d) · 10-(a)

📖 Explanations:
1. (b) III is wrong: India Wins Freedom was published posthumously in 1959.
2. (a) II is wrong: the UN's International Day of Education is 24 January; 5 September is Teachers' Day.
3. (c) I is wrong: it was signed at 5 a.m.; 11 a.m. is when the fighting stopped.
4. (d) Armistice = 11 Nov 1918; Peace Conference opened 18 Jan 1919; Versailles signed 28 June 1919. Option (a) swaps the armistice and the treaty.
5. (c) I is wrong: the ₹31,387-crore 512 MW centre is at Bheemunipatnam (Visakhapatnam); Vizianagaram land is for Reliance.
6. (c) III is incorrect: nuclear energy is a Union subject; a State can only study it, plants need Centre's approval.
7. (d) II is wrong: the order was passed on consent; the notifications continue with no rollback — only the exercise is on hold.
8. (b) II is wrong: Census is in the Union List (Entry 69); only Parliament can make Census law.
9. (d) II is wrong: Odisha has the most (1,789); Arunachal Pradesh is second (1,518).
10. (a) II is wrong: BharatNet lays optical fibre to gram panchayats; 4G saturation puts mobile towers in villages.
