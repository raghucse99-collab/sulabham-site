1. *Armistice* — యుద్ధ విరమణ ఒప్పందం (శాంతి ఒప్పందానికి ముందు) (an agreement to stop fighting (before a peace treaty))
   “The Armistice signed at Compiègne stopped the fighting of World War I at 11 a.m. on 11 November 1918.”
2. *Abeyance* — తాత్కాలిక నిలిపివేత (a state of temporary pause)
   “The Manipur High Court said the Census will stay in abeyance till 30 April 2027.”
3. *Posthumously* — మరణానంతరం (after a person's death)
   “Maulana Azad was posthumously awarded the Bharat Ratna in 1992.”
4. *Saturation* — పూర్తి కవరేజ్ (చివరి వరకు చేరడం) (full coverage, reaching every last unit)
   “BSNL's 4G saturation project aims to take 4G to every uncovered village.”
5. *Feasibility* — సాధ్యాసాధ్యత (whether a plan can practically be done)
   “The AP Cabinet approved examining the feasibility of Small Modular Reactors.”
