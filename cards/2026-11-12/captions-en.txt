🗓️ Pandit Madan Mohan Malaviya — 'Mahamana'
Founder of Banaras Hindu University, four-time Congress president and the man who made 'Satyameva Jayate' a national slogan.
⚠️ Exam trap: BHU (1916) ≠ Central Hindu College (1898, Annie Besant). Malaviya died in 1946 — he did not see Independence; Bharat Ratna was posthumous (2014). Mundaka, not Mandukya, Upanishad.
