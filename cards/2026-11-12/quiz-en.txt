📖 Explanations:
1. (b) II is wrong: 'The Book of Indian Birds' (1941) is his own; the 10-volume 'Handbook' was with Ripley.
2. (a) II is wrong: Silent Valley National Park is in Kerala, not Karnataka.
3. (a) III is wrong — it is from the Mundaka Upanishad. IV is wrong — the Bharat Ratna (2014) was posthumous; he died in 1946.
4. (c) Only III is incorrect: Malaviya was Vice-Chancellor from 1919, and he died on 12 November 1946.
5. (d) II is wrong: the organiser is CII (an industry body) in partnership with DPIIT and the AP Government — not NITI Aayog.
6. (b) III is wrong: ₹13.35 lakh crore was an investment commitment through 600+ MoUs, not money already spent.
7. (a) II is wrong — the 2004 letter cannot override the 1993 OM. IV is wrong — the income limit stays; salary alone is just not decisive.
8. (c) II is wrong: the limit was ₹1 lakh in 1993; it rose to ₹8 lakh only in 2017.
9. (b) III is wrong: the Rajatarangini manuscript is at the Oriental Research Library, Srinagar.
10. (c) I is wrong — they are on the regional (Asia-Pacific) register. IV is wrong — MoW brings recognition, not binding obligations or funds.
