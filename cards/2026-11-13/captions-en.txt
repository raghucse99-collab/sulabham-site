🗓️ Tagore's Nobel Prize announced — 113 years ago

⚠️ Exam trap: Gitanjali (Bengali) = 1910; English Song Offerings = 1912; Nobel = 1913; knighthood = 1915. Tagore was NOT present at the 1913 Stockholm ceremony.

📰 AP seeks WIPO partnership: an IP academy, GI tags and a top-100 S&T cluster goal
At Visakhapatnam, the AP Chief Minister asked WIPO to help train innovators in IPR and to get GI tags for AP handlooms and handicrafts.
⚠️ Exam trap: A GI tag protects a product's link to a PLACE (e.g. a handloom from a region); a patent protects an INVENTION. The academy proposed is a WIPO–AP academy, not a new WIPO headquarters.

📰 Export of lithium-ion battery 'black mass' banned: keeping critical minerals at home
DGFT moved black mass from 'Free' to 'Prohibited' so Indian recyclers get enough feedstock to recover lithium, cobalt, nickel and manganese.
⚠️ Exam trap: It is an EXPORT ban (Free → Prohibited), not an import ban, and it was issued by DGFT (Commerce Ministry), not the Ministry of Mines. Lead, cadmium and mercury batteries are outside this code.
