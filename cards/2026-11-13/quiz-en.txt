🔑 *Answers — Daily Quiz* | 13 NOV 2026
1-(a) · 2-(c) · 3-(d) · 4-(b) · 5-(b) · 6-(d) · 7-(b) · 8-(a) · 9-(b) · 10-(c)

📖 Explanations:
1. (a) II is wrong: Lahore 1799, but proclaimed Maharaja on Baisakhi day, 12 April 1801.
2. (c) II and IV are incorrect: the Koh-i-noor came from the Afghan ex-ruler Shah Shuja, and he died at Lahore (27 June 1839).
3. (d) III is wrong: the introduction was by the Irish poet W.B. Yeats.
4. (b) II is wrong: knighthood was GIVEN in 1915 and renounced in 1919 after Jallianwala Bagh.
5. (b) III is wrong: the WIPO Andhra Pradesh Academy is meant for students, researchers, startup founders and MSMEs.
6. (d) Only a GI tag ties the product to its region — that is why AP asked WIPO for GI support for handlooms. A patent is for inventions; a design protects only the look.
7. (b) III is the trap: a platform that acts promptly on notice is exactly the case safe harbour protects.
8. (a) I is wrong: it is MeitY. IV is wrong: talks are only exploratory, and a change needs Parliament to amend the IT Act.
9. (b) I is wrong: it went from 'Free' to 'Prohibited'. III is wrong: the code covers batteries WITHOUT lead, cadmium or mercury.
10. (c) IV is wrong: GTRI says India's exports of these goods were nil last fiscal year — the ban is preventive.
