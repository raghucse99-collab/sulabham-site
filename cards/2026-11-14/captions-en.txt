📰 Retail inflation 6.09%: above RBI's 6% upper limit — and AP at 7.25%
October 2026 CPI inflation rose to 6.09% (provisional), the first month above 6% in the new 2024-base series; food inflation 8.94%.
⚠️ Exam trap: One month above 6% is NOT a 'failure' of the target — failure = outside 2–6% for three consecutive quarters. Also: in the new series the weight of food has gone DOWN (36.75), not up. Section 45ZA, not 45ZN, is the target section.

📰 Climate Change Performance Index 2027: India slips to 28th
