🔑 *Answers — Daily Quiz* | 14 NOV 2026
1-(b) · 2-(c) · 3-(a) · 4-(d) · 5-(d) · 6-(b) · 7-(a) · 8-(c) · 9-(a) · 10-(b)

📖 Explanations:
1. (b) III is wrong: Nehru received the Bharat Ratna in 1955, while he was alive. I and II are correct.
2. (c) IV is wrong: Panchsheel (five principles of coexistence, 1954) was with China. I, II, III are correct (1889 + 137 = 2026).
3. (a) III is the trap: 14 November is the birthday of Frederick Banting (1891), not of his assistant Charles Best.
4. (d) Only III is incorrect: the statin target is 60% (not 80%) of people with diabetes aged 40 or older. I, II and IV are correct.
5. (d) II is wrong: failure means inflation stays outside 2–6% for three consecutive quarters; one month is not enough. I and III are correct (rural 6.63% > urban 5.41%).
6. (b) III is wrong: the share of food and beverages has declined in the new series (weight 36.75), though it is still the largest component.
7. (a) III is the trap: India is 'low' in the renewable energy category; only its renewable energy trend indicator is rated 'high'.
8. (c) I is wrong: installed capacity is not generation — the 50% share (which includes nuclear) is of capacity; coal still dominates actual generation.
9. (a) Only II is incorrect: the facility is at Amaravati; Visakhapatnam was only the summit venue. I, III and IV are correct.
10. (b) III is wrong: ₹9.98 lakh crore is the reported whole-summit total; Day 1 alone was ₹5.84 lakh crore. 1 lakh crore = 1 trillion, so II is correct.
