1. *Tolerance band* — లక్ష్యం చుట్టూ అనుమతించిన పరిధి (సహన పరిధి) (the allowed range around a target)
   “October's 6.09% inflation crossed the upper end of the RBI's 2–6% tolerance band.”
2. *Provisional* — తాత్కాలికం; తర్వాత సవరించవచ్చు (for now; may be revised later)
   “MoSPI said the October CPI figure of 6.09% is provisional.”
3. *Curtailment* — తగ్గించడం, నిలిపివేయడం (cutting down or holding back)
   “Weak grids led to the curtailment of 2.3 TWh of solar power in 2025.”
4. *Stigma* — కళంకం, చిన్నచూపు (a mark of shame or disapproval)
   “WHO says diabetes stigma is widespread, harmful and preventable.”
5. *Posthumous* — మరణానంతరం (given or happening after death)
   “Nehru's Bharat Ratna (1955) was not posthumous — he received it while alive.”
