🗓️ Acharya Vinoba Bhave — Bhoodan began in Telangana
Gandhi's first individual satyagrahi (1940); started the Bhoodan (land-gift) movement at Pochampally, Nalgonda, on 18 April 1951.
⚠️ Exam trap: Bhoodan began at Pochampally (Nalgonda, Telangana) in 1951 — not in Bihar or UP. The first GRAMDAN village was Mangrath in UP (1952). Bhoodan was voluntary; it was not a law. Bharat Ratna (1983) was posthumous.

📰 AP plans an Investment Protection Act: can one government bind the next?
CM N. Chandrababu Naidu announced at the 31st CII Partnership Summit a law to keep investment commitments binding beyond one government's term; the Bill is likely in the next Assembly session.
⚠️ Exam trap: It is only a proposal — no Act exists yet. A state law can be amended or repealed by a later Assembly, so no Act can make promises 'permanent'. ₹22.6 lakh crore = commitments, not money actually invested.
