1-(b) · 2-(c) · 3-(a) · 4-(d) · 5-(b) · 6-(a) · 7-(c) · 8-(d) · 9-(c) · 10-(b)

📖 Explanations:
1. (b) III is wrong: the Santhal Hul (1855–56, Sidhu–Kanhu) is a different revolt; Ulgulan = Munda, 1899–1900.
2. (c) III and IV are wrong: the Cabinet decided on Janjatiya Gaurav Divas on 10 Nov 2021; Birsa died on 9 June 1900, eight years before the 1908 Act.
3. (a) III is wrong: he died on 15 Nov 1982; the Bharat Ratna was given posthumously in 1983.
4. (d) A-2, B-1, C-4, D-3: Bhoodan at Pochampally; first Gramdan Mangrath; born at Gagode; Brahma Vidya Mandir at Paunar. Trap: swapping Pochampally and Mangrath.
5. (b) II is wrong: the Bill is still being prepared and is likely to come in the next Assembly session.
6. (a) III is wrong: ₹22.6 lakh crore is investment COMMITMENTS since June 2024; ₹17.2 lakh crore have approvals/work orders.
7. (c) III is wrong: incentives paid were ₹35,354 crore — only 18.51% of the outlay. 76% is the investment figure.
8. (d) II and IV are incorrect: ACC batteries reached only 1.4 GWh of 50 GWh, and jobs only 28% of target.
9. (c) II is wrong: Constitution Benches fell from about 134 cases a year (early 1960s) to fewer than 10 a year in the last decade.
10. (b) 124 = establishment, 130 = seat, 136 = SLP, 145(3) = five-judge bench. Trap: swapping 130 (seat) and 136 (SLP).
