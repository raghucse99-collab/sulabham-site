🗓️ National Press Day — the Press Council of India turns 60
16 November marks the day in 1966 when the Press Council of India began work as the 'moral watchdog' of a free and responsible press.
⚠️ Exam trap: 1956 = the year the First Press Commission recommended the Council; 1966 = the year the PCI began work. Examiners swap them. Also, 16 Nov National Press Day is Indian; 3 May is the UN's World Press Freedom Day.

📅 TODAY IN SULABHAM | 16 Nov 2026

📰 Third cyclone in three weeks? 'Akvan' and IMD's four-stage warning
IMD issued a 'pre-cyclone watch' for the AP and south Odisha coasts on 15 Nov; the depression is forecast to become a cyclonic storm today and a severe cyclonic storm on 17 Nov.
⚠️ Exam trap: A storm gets its name only at the cyclonic-storm stage (62 km/h), not as a depression. 'Pre-cyclone watch' has no colour; yellow = alert, orange = warning, red = post-landfall outlook. Akvan is Iran's name, not India's (India's was Murasu).

📅 TODAY IN SULABHAM | 16 Nov 2026

📰 PM in Manila for the 23rd ASEAN-India Summit and 21st East Asia Summit
Both summits are on 17 Nov in Manila; 2026 is the ASEAN-India Year of Maritime Cooperation and the summit will review the Plan of Action 2026–2030.
⚠️ Exam trap: ASEAN-India Summit (23rd) and East Asia Summit (21st) have different numbers and members — India is a partner, not a member of ASEAN. ASEAN now has 11 members (Timor-Leste), not 10. Official sources give India's full dialogue partnership as 1995 (many books write 1996).

📅 TODAY IN SULABHAM | 16 Nov 2026

📰 National Health Accounts 2023-24: government spending triples, families pay a smaller share
Out-of-pocket spending fell from 64.2% of total health spending in 2013-14 to 42.7% in 2023-24, while government health spending rose about three times.
⚠️ Exam trap: OOPE fell as a SHARE of total health spending — not necessarily in rupees. 'Percentage points' (64.2 → 42.7 = 21.5 points) is not the same as 'per cent'. NHA is prepared by NHSRC's NHA Technical Secretariat, not by NSO.
