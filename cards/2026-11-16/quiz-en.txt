🔑 *Answers — Daily Quiz* | 16 NOV 2026
1-(b) · 2-(d) · 3-(a) · 4-(c) · 5-(d) · 6-(b) · 7-(c) · 8-(a) · 9-(b) · 10-(d)

📖 Explanations:
1. (b) II is the trap: 1956 = First Press Commission's recommendation; the Council began work on 16 Nov 1966.
2. (d) National Press Day = 16 Nov (India, PCI); World Press Freedom Day = 3 May (UNESCO); End Impunity Day = 2 Nov. Option (a) swaps the two press days.
3. (a) USA 1912 → 'Ghadar' paper 1 Nov 1913 → revolt leaked Feb 1915 → hanged 16 Nov 1915. Option (b) wrongly puts the paper before his going abroad.
4. (c) Only I is incorrect: the party was formed in America (California); Sohan Singh Bhakna was president and Har Dayal secretary.
5. (d) Deep depression 50–61; cyclonic storm 62–88 (the stage at which 'Akvan' gets its name); severe 89–117; very severe 118–166.
6. (b) III is wrong: a name is used only once and never repeated. I, II and IV are correct.
7. (c) III is wrong: the Philippines chairs ASEAN in 2026 (Singapore is next). IV is wrong: EAS = ASEAN plus eight partners such as India, China, Japan, the US and Russia.
8. (a) The MEA release itself calls ASEAN the central pillar of Act East — that is why India gives the summits priority. Trap: thinking Act East is only about trade (it covers security and sea routes too).
9. (b) A direct payment by a household is OOPE and is part of THE. II is wrong: who pays decides the category, not who licenses the hospital.
10. (d) II is wrong: the fall is 21.5 percentage POINTS (64.2% → 42.7%), not 21.5 per cent. IV is wrong: public health is in the State List (Entry 6, List II).
