🗓️ Suez Canal opens — the short sea route between Europe and India
Opened 157 years ago today, the canal joined the Mediterranean to the Red Sea — ships no longer had to go round Africa to reach India.

📰 Data protection law, phase 2: companies can now register as 'Consent Managers'
From 13 November 2026, Indian companies can apply to the Data Protection Board to become Consent Managers — one place where you give, check and withdraw consent for use of your personal data.
⚠️ Exam trap: Data PRINCIPAL = you (the person); Data FIDUCIARY = the company using your data; Consent MANAGER = the go-between platform. A Consent Manager is registered with the Data Protection Board, not with RBI or MeitY, and must be an Indian company.

📰 India votes against the UN call for a moratorium on the death penalty
The UN General Assembly adopted Mexico's resolution 133–33–16; India voted against, keeping the death penalty for the 'rarest of rare' cases under its own law.
⚠️ Exam trap: Moratorium = temporary halt of executions, NOT abolition. A UNGA resolution is not binding. Bachan Singh (1980) gave 'rarest of rare'; Machhi Singh (1983) gave the five tests — don't swap them.
