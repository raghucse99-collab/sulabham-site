🔑 *Answers — Daily Quiz* | 17 NOV 2026
1-(b) · 2-(d) · 3-(a) · 4-(c) · 5-(c) · 6-(d) · 7-(c) · 8-(a) · 9-(d) · 10-(b)

📖 Explanations:
1. (b) DAV School 1886 → Home Rule League of America 1917 → Calcutta Special Session 1920 → Servants of the People Society 1921. Trap (a): 1920 before 1917.
2. (d) III is wrong: by mistaken identity they shot Assistant Superintendent John P. Saunders, not Scott.
3. (a) 1869 opened, 1875 shares to Britain, 1888 neutral (Constantinople), 1956 nationalised. Trap (d): the 1888 Convention did not nationalise the canal.
4. (c) Only II is incorrect: both seas are at the same level, so the Suez Canal has no locks.
5. (c) A is true (data must stay unreadable to it). R is false: the Consent Manager is a go-between platform registered with the Board; the Data Fiduciary is the company that uses the data.
6. (d) IV is wrong: only companies incorporated in India (net worth ≥ ₹2 crore) can apply. I, II, III are correct.
7. (c) Bachan Singh = 'rarest of rare'; Machhi Singh = five tests; BNSS 407 = High Court confirmation; BNS Sec 4 = list of punishments. Trap (a): swapping the two judgments.
8. (a) II is wrong: a moratorium is a temporary halt, not abolition. The resolution asks for a moratorium 'with a view to abolishing' and for removing mandatory death sentences.
9. (d) III is wrong: the 'Adornment' gallery is expected in North Block in the latter part of 2027.
10. (b) III is wrong: they were built in the colonial period (1912–1931) as secretariat buildings for ministries, not for Parliament.
