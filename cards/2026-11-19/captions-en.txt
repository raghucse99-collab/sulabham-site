🗓️ Rani Lakshmibai of Jhansi — 'Meri Jhansi nahin dungi'
The queen who lost her state to the Doctrine of Lapse and died fighting in the Revolt of 1857.
⚠️ Exam trap: Jhansi was annexed in 1854 (before the Revolt) by DALHOUSIE — not by Canning, and not in 1857. Her ally at Gwalior was TANTIA TOPE; the British commander was HUGH ROSE. She died near GWALIOR (Kotah-ki-Serai), not inside Jhansi fort.

📰 Census 2027 Phase II begins: self-enumeration opens in four states
Residents of UP, Punjab, Goa and Uttarakhand can fill their own Census form online till 30 Nov; a '287 other religions' list was removed the same day.
⚠️ Exam trap: Self-enumeration is OPTIONAL — enumerators still visit every block. Reference date is 1 March 2027 for most states, but 5 January 2027 for the four early states. 'Others' is still an open field; the 287 names were NOT new official religions. Census Act is 1948 (not 1951 — that is the Representation of the People Act).

📰 Why India needs a space-security doctrine
Satellites can now be knocked out without being destroyed — by jamming, spoofing and cyberattacks. India needs a clear plan to protect them.
⚠️ Exam trap: Jamming = BLOCKING a signal; spoofing = sending a FALSE signal. Mission Shakti was by DRDO (not ISRO) and was launched from Odisha (not Sriharikota). ISSAR is ISRO's space-situational-awareness report, not a policy.

📰 Solar at 175 GW: India's renewable journey and the new floating-solar scheme
Solar grew from 2.8 GW in 2014 to about 175 GW; non-fossil sources are now 55% of India's power capacity.
⚠️ Exam trap: 55% is the non-fossil share of installed CAPACITY, not of electricity actually generated. PM-Surya Ghar = rooftop (2024); PM Surya Sarovar = floating solar (2026). India is 3rd in renewable capacity, but was the 2nd-largest solar GROWTH market in 2025.
