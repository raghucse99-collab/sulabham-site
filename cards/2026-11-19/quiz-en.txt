🔑 *Answers — Daily Quiz* | 19 NOV 2026
1-(b) · 2-(c) · 3-(a) · 4-(d) · 5-(c) · 6-(c) · 7-(a) · 8-(b) · 9-(d) · 10-(b)

📖 Explanations:
1. (b) Adoption came first (just before the Maharaja died), then Dalhousie refused the heir and annexed Jhansi (1854), then Meerut (1857), then Gwalior (1858). Option (a) wrongly puts annexation before adoption — the annexation happened BECAUSE the adopted heir was refused.
2. (c) (c) is NOT correct: she escaped from Jhansi fort and was killed at Kotah-ki-Serai near Gwalior on 17/18 June 1858. (a), (b), (d) are correct.
3. (a) II is wrong: resolution 67/291 was adopted by consensus (24 July 2013), no vote. IV is wrong: World Toilet Day is 19 November; 22 March is World Water Day.
4. (d) All three are correct: theme on sanitation + gender; 3.4 billion = 4 in 10 lack safe toilets (2025); SDG 6.2. The trap is to think sanitation falls under SDG 3 (health) or SDG 5 (gender) and reject III.
5. (c) A-3 (1 Mar 2027 for most states), B-1 (5 Jan 2027 for the four early states), C-2 (40 questions), D-4 (16 languages). (a) swaps the two reference dates; (b) swaps 40 and 16.
6. (c) I is wrong: the drop-down was removed the same day; the names were not new official religions — the seven categories (Hindu, Muslim, Christian, Sikh, Buddhist, Jain, Others) remain. II, III, IV are correct; (b) misses the true statement III.
7. (a) Both true, and R explains A: because these attacks leave no debris and attribution is uncertain, deciding whether it is an 'attack' and how to respond is harder. (b) is the usual trap — here R is the very reason for A.
8. (b) IV is wrong: the fourth pillar is institutional PARTNERSHIP with private operators (along with government, forces and ISRO), not a ban. (a) misses III.
9. (d) Solar ~175 GW > Wind 59.20 GW > Hydro 57.49 GW > Bio 11.75 GW. The trap is (a): wind and hydro are close, but wind is slightly larger.
10. (b) III is wrong: PM-Surya Ghar's outlay is ₹75,021 crore, far more than PM Surya Sarovar's ₹5,070 crore. (a) misses the true statement II.
