1-(b) · 2-(d) · 3-(a) · 4-(b) · 5-(a) · 6-(c) · 7-(a) · 8-(c) · 9-(a) · 10-(d)

📖 Explanations:
1. (b) II is wrong: Hyder Ali was the ruler in the 1st war; Tipu became ruler only in December 1782.
2. (d) Madras (1769) ended the 1st war, Mangalore (1784) the 2nd, Seringapatam (1792) the 3rd. Option a swaps Mangalore and Seringapatam — the common trap.
3. (a) II is wrong: the 1954 resolution only recommended a Universal Children's Day; the Convention came on 20 Nov 1989.
4. (b) Declaration 1959 → CRC adopted 1989 → in force 1990 → India 1992. Option a puts 'in force' before adoption — impossible.
5. (a) Legacy packaging gets 50% (not 70%) and compound fabs get 70% (not 50%) — III and IV swap the figures.
6. (c) I is wrong: the park is planned at Tirupati, not Visakhapatnam.
7. (a) Only II is incorrect: Akvan is a Bay of Bengal storm. I and III are correct as per IMD updates on AIR.
8. (c) IV is wrong: IMD told fishermen not to go into the west-central and north-west Bay of Bengal till 21 Nov — the whole sea area is risky.
9. (a) Both true and linked: money went mostly to road dust while more harmful PM2.5 sources like industry and household fuel got <1% — that is why the writers call for outcome-based financing.
10. (d) III is wrong: the Lancet Planetary Health estimate is 1.36% of GDP (2019), not 13.6% — a decimal trap.
