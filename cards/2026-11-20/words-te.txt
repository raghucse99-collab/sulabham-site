📚 *సులభం · నేటి 5 కొత్త పదాలు* | శుక్రవారం, 20 నవంబర్ 2026

1. *Indemnity* — యుద్ధ పరిహారం (ఓడినవారు చెల్లించే నష్టపరిహారం) (money a defeated side is forced to pay for war losses)
   “Under the Treaty of Seringapatam (1792), Tipu Sultan had to pay a war indemnity of three crore rupees.”
2. *Accession* — చేరిక (ఇతరులు ఇప్పటికే కుదుర్చుకున్న ఒప్పందంలో చేరడం) (joining a treaty that has already been negotiated and signed by others)
   “India became a party to the Convention on the Rights of the Child by accession on 11 December 1992.”
3. *Tranche* — విడత (దశలవారీగా విడుదల చేసే చెల్లింపులో ఒక భాగం) (one part of a payment released in instalments)
   “Andhra Pradesh will release its share of chip-fab support after each tranche of Central assistance.”
4. *Landfall* — తుపాను తీరం దాటడం (the moment a storm's centre crosses from sea onto land)
   “IMD expected Cyclone Akvan to make landfall between Narsapur and Puri on 20 November.”
5. *Surveillance* — నిఘా / నిరంతర పర్యవేక్షణ (close, continuous watching and recording of data)
   “The writers want an environmental-health surveillance system that links air-quality data with hospital admissions.”
