🗓️ Sir C.V. Raman — the scientist of light

⚠️ Exam trap: National Science Day (28 Feb) marks the DISCOVERY of the Raman effect (1928), not Raman's birthday (7 Nov). The discovery was in 1928 but the Nobel came in 1930 — and it was in Physics, not Chemistry.

🗓️ World Fisheries Day — India No. 2 in the world, Andhra Pradesh No. 1 in India
The day began in New Delhi in 1997. India produces about 8% of the world's fish, and AP leads the country in fish and shrimp production.

📅 TODAY IN SULABHAM | 21 Nov 2026

📰 One year of the four Labour Codes: what changed, what is still pending
29 central labour laws were merged into 4 Codes from 21 Nov 2025. One year on, many States are still finishing their rules — Andhra Pradesh is among the few that have notified final rules under all four.
⚠️ Exam trap: The Codes were PASSED in 2019–2020 but came INTO FORCE only on 21 Nov 2025. They merged 29 laws (not 44). The retrenchment-permission threshold is 300 workers (raised from 100). The gig-fund cap is 5% of payments to gig workers, not 5% of turnover.

📰 SVAMITVA: drones map 98% of target villages, but only two-thirds have property cards
The Centre and States have agreed to finish the scheme by 31 March 2027. The weak link is ground verification and card distribution, which States must do.
⚠️ Exam trap: SVAMITVA maps the inhabited (abadi) house plots of villages — NOT agricultural land. It is a Central Sector scheme of the Ministry of Panchayati Raj (not the Rural Development Ministry). 98% is the DRONE SURVEY figure; property cards cover only 66% of villages.
