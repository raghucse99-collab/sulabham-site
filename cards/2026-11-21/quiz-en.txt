🔑 *Answers — Daily Quiz* | 21 NOV 2026
1-(c) · 2-(a) · 3-(d) · 4-(b) · 5-(c) · 6-(a) · 7-(d) · 8-(b) · 9-(a) · 10-(d)

📖 Explanations:
1. (c) III is wrong: the Raman Research Institute was founded in 1948, long after the 1930 Nobel Prize.
2. (a) 1917 Palit Professor, 1926 Indian Journal of Physics, 1954 Bharat Ratna, 1957 Lenin Peace Prize. Option (c) swaps 1917/1926 and (d) swaps the two prizes — common traps.
3. (d) III is wrong: PMMSY was launched on 10 September 2020. 2018 is the year of the Fisheries and Aquaculture Infrastructure Development Fund (FIDF).
4. (b) II is wrong: PMMSY's outlay is ₹20,312 crore. ₹6,000 crore is the outlay of PM-MKSSY.
5. (c) IV is wrong: the contribution is 1–2% of annual turnover, and the CAP is 5% of the amount paid to gig and platform workers — not 5% of turnover.
6. (a) (a) is wrong: under the Industrial Relations Code the threshold was raised from 100 to 300 workers. (b), (c) and (d) are correct.
7. (d) II is wrong: SVAMITVA maps the inhabited (abadi) house plots of villages, not agricultural land, and has nothing to do with crop insurance.
8. (b) II is the reverse of the truth: the drone survey is 98% done, but property cards cover only 66% of villages.
9. (a) IV is wrong: the Mission will intensify exploration within the country AND in its offshore areas.
10. (d) IV is the opposite of the argument: auction counts are weak indicators — processed output is the real measure.
