🔑 *Answers — Daily Quiz* | 22 NOV 2026
1-(b) · 2-(c) · 3-(a) · 4-(d) · 5-(c) · 6-(a) · 7-(b) · 8-(a) · 9-(d) · 10-(b)

📖 Explanations:
1. (b) IV is wrong: her decoy act was at Jhansi; the Rani herself later died fighting near Gwalior, and Jhalkari's death date is disputed.
2. (c) III is incorrect: 4 April 1858 is the community account; some historians give 1881 or say she died very old.
3. (a) Johnson sworn in 22 Nov (2:38 pm) → Oswald shot two days later → Commission set up 29 Nov 1963 → report 24 Sep 1964.
4. (d) Warren = Chief Justice (3); Ruby = nightclub owner who shot Oswald (4); Oswald = accused (2); Connally = wounded Governor (1). Option (a) swaps Ruby and Oswald — the usual trap.
5. (c) I is wrong: AP was 3rd in Overall Performance; its 1st rank was in Special Campaigns (and 2nd in Loan Disbursement).
6. (a) IV is wrong: the credit card has a limit of up to ₹30,000. Interest subsidy (7%), next tranche (₹25,000) and ₹50–100 monthly cashback are all part of the scheme.
7. (b) III wrong: fertilisers (−12.2%), natural gas (−7.6%) and crude oil (−4.1%) fell. IV wrong: April–October was 6.0%, lower than October's 10.9%.
8. (a) Both true and linked: a very low base (0.8%) makes this year's percentage look large; ICRA called it partly optical. (b) is the trap — R does explain A.
9. (d) II is wrong: 1.3 TMC/day for 10 days was Tamil Nadu's DEMAND; the Court dismissed the applications.
10. (b) III is wrong: the CWMA was set up in 2018, after the Supreme Court's 2018 judgment, under the 1956 Act — not by the 2007 award.
