📅 TODAY IN SULABHAM | 23 Nov 2026

🗓️ Henry Moseley — the man who gave the periodic table its 'atomic number'
His X-ray work (1913–14) showed that an element's properties depend on its atomic number, not its atomic weight — the basis of the modern periodic table.
⚠️ Exam trap: Mendeleev = atomic WEIGHT (1869); Moseley = atomic NUMBER (1913–14). Moseley did not win a Nobel Prize — he died in 1915. Atomic number = protons, not protons + neutrons (that is mass number).
