1-(b) · 2-(d) · 3-(a) · 4-(c) · 5-(c) · 6-(d) · 7-(a) · 8-(a) · 9-(b) · 10-(d)

📖 Explanations:
1. (b) II is the trap: Bose chose NOT to commercialise his invention; Marconi held the patented system.
2. (d) J.C. Bose = Crescograph (3); S.N. Bose = boson statistics (4); Raman = light scattering (1); Moseley = atomic number (2). Option (a) swaps the two Boses — the usual trap.
3. (a) Only II is incorrect: Moseley never won a Nobel; he died in 1915 aged 27. Siegbahn, who used his method, won in 1924.
4. (c) II is wrong: 35 = protons + neutrons = mass number. Atomic number = protons = 17, which fixes the place.
5. (c) Nov 2019 filing (II) → Jan 2020 provisional measures (IV) → Jul 2022 jurisdiction (I) → Nov 2026 merits (III). Option (a) puts jurisdiction before provisional measures — the trap.
6. (d) II is wrong: trying individuals is the ICC's job; the ICJ decides disputes between states. III is wrong: the ICJ cannot enforce its rulings — the UN Security Council can be approached.
7. (a) II is wrong: MAHASAGAR (like IPOI) is India's; Japan's vision is 'Free and Open Indo-Pacific' (FOIP).
8. (a) Both true, and R explains what the 2+2 format is. Trap: thinking 2+2 means two Defence Ministers + two service chiefs.
9. (b) Fuel first (RAPP-8 loaded fuel in Sep 2026), then criticality (AERB nod 19 Nov 2026), then grid, then commercial operation. RAPP-7 followed the same path: criticality Sep 2024 → grid Mar 2025.
10. (d) II is wrong: PHWRs are Stage 1. Stage 2 = fast breeder reactors, Stage 3 = thorium.
