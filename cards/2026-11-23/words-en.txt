📚 *Sulabham · Today's 5 new words* | Monday, 23 November 2026

1. *Propagation* — తరంగాలు ఒక చోటు నుంచి మరో చోటుకు ప్రయాణించడం (ప్రసారం) (the spreading or travelling of waves through space)
   “J.C. Bose was one of the first to demonstrate the propagation of very short radio waves.”
2. *Restitution* — కోల్పోయినదాన్ని తిరిగి ఇవ్వడం, పూర్వ స్థితిని పునరుద్ధరించడం (giving back what was lost, or putting things back as they were)
   “The ICJ said Myanmar must provide restitution, including the safe return of the Rohingya.”
3. *Intervene* — ఒక కేసులో/పరిస్థితిలో జోక్యం చేసుకోవడం (to join a case or situation to protect an interest)
   “Eleven states chose to intervene in The Gambia v. Myanmar.”
4. *Coercion* — ఒత్తిడి, బెదిరింపులతో బలవంతం చేయడం (forcing someone to act by using pressure or threats)
   “India and Japan expressed grave concern over economic coercion.”
5. *Criticality* — అణు గొలుసు చర్య తనంతట తానే కొనసాగే స్థితి (the state in which a nuclear chain reaction keeps going on its own)
   “AERB allowed RAPP-8 to make its first approach to criticality.”
