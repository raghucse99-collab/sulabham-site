🗓️ Darwin's 'On the Origin of Species' is published
The book that explained HOW new species form — by natural selection — and changed biology for ever.
⚠️ Exam trap: Darwin did NOT invent the idea of evolution — he explained its mechanism. Human evolution is in 'The Descent of Man' (1871), not in the Origin (1859). Wallace, not Lamarck, independently reached natural selection.

AP is grouping its districts into three economic regions, each with its own growth plan up to 2047 — so that growth spreads to north Andhra, the central coast and Rayalaseema.

📰 SAMPADA survey: anaemia lower than before, but over half of women still anaemic
ICMR's new survey, using venous blood, finds 55.9% of women (15–49) and 41.8% of young children anaemic — and Andhra Pradesh is among the worst-affected states.

NSO's quarterly bulletin (QBUSE) shows non-farm unincorporated enterprises grew 18.81% in a year — the informal sector is where most Indians find non-farm work.
