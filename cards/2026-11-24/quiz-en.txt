🔑 *Answers — Daily Quiz* | 24 NOV 2026
1-(b) · 2-(d) · 3-(a) · 4-(a) · 5-(c) · 6-(b) · 7-(d) · 8-(c) · 9-(a) · 10-(b)

📖 Explanations:
1. (b) II is the trap: Guru Gobind Singh was born at Patna (1666), where Tegh Bahadur had left his family during his eastern travels.
2. (d) Amritsar = birth; Anandpur Sahib = 1665 centre; Dhubri Sahib = Ahom truce; Chandni Chowk = martyrdom (Gurdwara Sis Ganj). Option (a) swaps Dhubri and Chandni Chowk.
3. (a) Guwahati retaken Nov 1667 → Alaboi 1669 → Saraighat March 1671 → statue 9 March 2024. Trap (b): Alaboi came after the recapture of Guwahati.
4. (a) Both are true and R explains A: the river tactics were the reason a smaller force won. Statements I and II are supporting facts. Trap: confusing Ram Singh I with Mir Jumla, whose invasion came earlier.
5. (c) I is wrong — human descent is in 'The Descent of Man' (1871). IV is wrong — the co-discoverer presented at the Linnean Society (1858) was Alfred Russel Wallace; Lamarck was an earlier evolutionist.
6. (b) Nellore and Kurnool → Tirupati region; Prakasam → Amaravati region; Kakinada → Visakhapatnam region. Trap: Nellore is coastal but grouped with Tirupati, not Amaravati.
7. (d) III is wrong: the AER is a planning and investment framework — the nine district collectors are members of its committee, not replaced by it.
8. (c) Only I questions the comparison: a change in measuring method can change the numbers by itself. II and III are true facts but do not make the comparison unreliable.
9. (a) I is the trap: for women 15–49 West Bengal is highest (72.4%); AP (68.5%) is high but tops the list only for women aged 60+ (81.7%).
10. (b) II is wrong: urban led in establishments (+22.05% vs +16.05%), but rural led in employment (+17.52% vs +16.30%). I: +18.81% vs +16.86%.
