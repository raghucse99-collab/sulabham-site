🔑 *Answers — Daily Quiz* | 25 NOV 2026
1-(b) · 2-(d) · 3-(a) · 4-(c) · 5-(b) · 6-(b) · 7-(d) · 8-(c) · 9-(a) · 10-(d)

📖 Explanations:
1. (b) II is the trap: he called civil disobedience, non-cooperation and satyagraha the 'Grammar of Anarchy' once constitutional methods are open, and asked that they be abandoned.
2. (d) A = J.S. Mill (hero-worship warning), B = Daniel O'Connell (gratitude vs liberty), C = Abraham Lincoln ('a House divided'). Option (c) wrongly swaps O'Connell and Lincoln.
3. (a) Order: Mirabal sisters 1960 → NCW 1992 → Declaration 1993 → Day designated 1999. Option (b) places the 1993 Declaration before the 1992 NCW.
4. (c) II is incorrect — the 16 days end on 10 December, Human Rights Day. III is incorrect — the colour is orange ('Orange the World'). I is the correct 2026 theme.
5. (b) III is the reverse of the plea: it wants majority tested only on the floor of the House after members take oath under Article 188, not by letters of support.
6. (b) Both are true. But A exists because of the merger clause (Paragraph 4); the removal of the split clause in 2003 does not explain why the merger exception exists.
7. (d) II is wrong: the Bill was introduced under Article 3 on the President's recommendation; Article 4 says such a law is not an amendment under Article 368, even though it changes the Schedules.
8. (c) I is wrong — only Parliament can change a State's name (Article 3). IV is wrong — ratification by States applies to some Article 368 amendments, and Article 4 says a renaming law is not such an amendment.
9. (a) NTR Award = Indian cinema, ₹5 lakh; Raghupathi Venkaiah = Telugu cinema (since 1980); Nagi Reddy–Chakrapani = an eminent producer/writer/technician. Option (b) swaps the NTR and Raghupathi Venkaiah awards.
10. (d) IV is wrong: the Nandi Awards are State awards of the Government of Andhra Pradesh, organised by the AP State Film, Television and Theatre Development Corporation.
