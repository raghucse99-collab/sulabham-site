🗓️ Constitution Day — 77 years since India adopted its Constitution

⚠️ Exam trap: Constitution Day was notified by the Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment — not the Law Ministry. And 26 Nov = adoption; 26 Jan = commencement. Do not swap them.

🗓️ Dr Verghese Kurien — Father of the White Revolution
National Milk Day is held on his birthday. His cooperative model made India the world's largest milk producer.
⚠️ Exam trap: NDDB (1965) and Operation Flood (1970) are different years. And India has been the No. 1 milk producer since 1998 — not since 2014. National Milk Day marks his BIRTH anniversary.

📰 Who pays for climate disasters? A question of federalism
States face floods and landslides first, but the Constitution keeps most laws and money with the Union.
⚠️ Exam trap: 'Disaster management' is NOT an entry in any of the three Lists — the DM Act 2005 was made using Concurrent List entries. And Ranjitsinh (2024) found the climate right in Articles 14 and 21 without a separate climate law.

📰 Monthly jobs data: is it news or just noise?
