🔑 *Answers — Daily Quiz* | 26 NOV 2026
1-(b) · 2-(c) · 3-(d) · 4-(a) · 5-(a) · 6-(b) · 7-(c) · 8-(a) · 9-(c) · 10-(d)

📖 Explanations:
1. (b) I and III are correct. II is the trap: the Gazette notification (19 Nov 2015) was issued by the Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment, not the Law Ministry.
2. (c) Drafting Committee 29 Aug 1947 → Draft introduced 4 Nov 1948 → Constitution Day notified 19 Nov 2015 → campaign launched 24 Jan 2024. Option (a) wrongly puts the 2024 campaign before the 2015 notification.
3. (d) NDDB 1965, Operation Flood 1970, GCMMF 1973, Magsaysay 1963. Option (a) swaps NDDB and Operation Flood — the most common confusion.
4. (a) (a) is wrong: NDDB was created in 1965 but Operation Flood began in 1970. (b), (c) and (d) match PIB figures and facts.
5. (a) I and III are correct. II is wrong: disaster is in none of the three Lists; the DM Act 2005 was made using Concurrent List entries.
6. (b) M.C. Mehta (1987) = absolute liability; Vellore (1996) = polluter pays + precautionary; Ranjitsinh (2024) = climate right under Arts 14 and 21. Option (c) wrongly swaps the two older cases.
7. (c) I and II are correct. III is wrong: the method was modified from January 2025 to give monthly and quarterly estimates; October 2026 is only the 19th monthly bulletin.
8. (a) (a): when confidence intervals overlap, the change is not statistically significant — that is exactly why the April–May rise is not a real change. The trap is to think a bigger sample makes every monthly change meaningful.
9. (c) I, II and IV are correct. III is wrong: Section 12 needs an officer not below Deputy Superintendent of Police or Assistant Commissioner of Police.
10. (d) (d) is wrong: the second report (23 Nov 2026) is under government consideration; an amendment Bill is expected in the Winter Session. (a), (b) and (c) are the panel's proposals.
