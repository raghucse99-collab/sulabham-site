India's PM (1989–90) who announced 27% reservation for OBCs in central government jobs.
⚠️ Exam trap: The Mandal Commission was set up by Morarji Desai's government (1979), NOT by V.P. Singh — he only implemented it (1990). And the 50% ceiling and creamy layer came from the Supreme Court (1992), not from the Mandal report.

Each harmful nutrient gets its own red hexagon on the front of the pack — so buyers see the risk at a glance.
⚠️ Exam trap: The court asked for SEPARATE hexagons (one per nutrient), not a single combined symbol — and for TOTAL sugars and SATURATED fat, not 'added' sugar/fat. The 2022 FSSAI idea was a star rating, which is different from a warning label.

📅 TODAY IN SULABHAM | 27 Nov 2026

📰 A legislator's conviction can't be stayed just to save his seat: Supreme Court
Stay of conviction is for rare cases with a 'palpable mistake' — possible disqualification cannot be the main reason.
⚠️ Exam trap: Suspension of SENTENCE ≠ stay of CONVICTION. Only a stay of conviction removes disqualification, and it needs a much stricter test. Also: Section 8(4) is still printed in the Act but was struck down in Lily Thomas (2013).

📰 US proposes up to 25% 'excess capacity' tariff on India under Section 301
Only a proposal for now — it falls to 17% if India and the US sign a reciprocal trade agreement.
⚠️ Exam trap: These tariffs are only PROPOSED (hearings in Jan 2027), not in force. And it is Section 301 of the Trade ACT of 1974 — Section 232 belongs to the Trade EXPANSION Act of 1962. India's 25% is second-highest, after Vietnam's 35%.
