🔑 *Answers — Daily Quiz* | 27 NOV 2026
1-(b) · 2-(a) · 3-(d) · 4-(c) · 5-(c) · 6-(a) · 7-(d) · 8-(a) · 9-(b) · 10-(c)

📖 Explanations:
1. (b) I and III are correct. II is the trap: the Mandal Commission was set up in 1979 by the Morarji Desai government; V.P. Singh implemented its report in 1990.
2. (a) Report 31 Dec 1980 → announcement 7 Aug 1990 → Indra Sawhney 16 Nov 1992 → education quota 2006. Option (b) wrongly puts the 1992 judgment before the 1990 announcement.
3. (d) Physics – Swedish Academy of Sciences; Medicine – Karolinska Institute; Literature – the Academy in Stockholm; Peace – Norwegian Storting's committee. Option (a) wrongly gives Literature to Norway.
4. (c) (c) is NOT correct: the Economics prize was added in 1968 by Sveriges Riksbank; the will named Physics, Chemistry, Medicine, Literature and Peace. (a), (b), (d) are correct.
5. (c) II and III are correct. I is wrong: the court said the DGI 2024 Table 15.1 values should NOT be adopted as they stand, because they were based on added sugar and added fat.
6. (a) (a): a separate hexagon for each nutrient that crosses the limit. (b) is the rejected single-hexagon idea; (c) was the 2022 draft idea; (d) is wrong because salt is within limits.
7. (d) I and III are correct. II is the trap: Section 8(4) was declared unconstitutional in Lily Thomas (10 July 2013), so the seat is lost with immediate effect.
8. (a) (a): the court said disqualification is a statutory consequence that cannot be avoided 'on mere showing of an arguable case'; so it cannot be the primary ground for a stay. A stay needs a 'palpable mistake' in rare cases.
9. (b) II and III are correct. I is wrong — the rates are only proposals. IV is wrong — India said it 'does not have structural excess capacity in any sector'.
10. (c) A-3 (Section 301 is the basis), B-1 (Section 232 = national security), C-2 (deal → 17%), D-4 (July 2026). Option (a) swaps 301 and 232 — the common trap.
