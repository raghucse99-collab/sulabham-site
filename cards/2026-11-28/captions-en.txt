📰 Delimitation explained: why Lok Sabha seats are still based on the 1971 Census
Parliament meets in a special sitting from 30 Nov on a revised plan to raise every State's Lok Sabha seats by 50% and keep State shares frozen till 2051 (as reported).
⚠️ Exam trap: The 42nd Amendment (1976) first froze seats on the 1971 Census; the 84th Amendment (2001) extended the freeze to after 2026. The 2002 Commission did NOT change the number of seats per State — only boundaries inside States.
