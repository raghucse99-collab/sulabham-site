🔑 *Answers — Daily Quiz* | 28 NOV 2026
1-(b) · 2-(c) · 3-(a) · 4-(c) · 5-(c) · 6-(a) · 7-(d) · 8-(d) · 9-(b) · 10-(c)

📖 Explanations:
1. (b) Girls' school 1848 → night schools 1855 → Satyashodhak Samaj 1873 → 'Mahatma' 1888. Trap: night schools (1855) came before the Samaj (1873).
2. (c) (c) is wrong: the Satyashodhak Samaj was founded in Pune on 24 Sept 1873. 1867, Bombay is the Prarthana Samaj — a classic trap.
3. (a) Working Class in England 1845, Manifesto 1848, Anti-Dühring 1878, Origin of the Family 1884. Trap: (c) swaps 1878 and 1884.
4. (c) A is true: Engels edited Vol 2 (1885) and completed Vol 3 (1894) after Marx's death. R is false: he was born at Barmen, Prussia; in Manchester he worked at the Ermen & Engels mill in which his father was a partner.
5. (c) I and II are correct. III is wrong: 298 votes in favour (230 against) was short of the two-thirds special majority (352) — the Bill failed on 17 April 2026.
6. (a) I and III are correct. II is wrong: the plan reportedly freezes State shares (based on 1971) till 2051 with a uniform 50% rise — no population-based reallocation between States.
7. (d) I and III are correct. II is wrong: cash transfers are revenue expenditure — they create no physical asset; more of them can squeeze capital spending.
8. (d) Lakshmir Bhandar — West Bengal; Gruha Lakshmi — Karnataka; Subhadra — Odisha; Annadata Sukhibhava — AP (farmers, ₹6,600 crore). Trap: Annadata Sukhibhava is a farmers' scheme, not a women's scheme.
9. (b) Hormuz — Persian Gulf/Gulf of Oman; Bab el-Mandeb — Red Sea/Gulf of Aden (south gate); Suez — Red Sea/Mediterranean (north end); Cape of Good Hope — the detour. Trap: (a) swaps the two ends of the Red Sea.
10. (c) II, III and IV are correct. I is wrong: growth slowed from 3.9% (2024) to 1.9% (2025).
