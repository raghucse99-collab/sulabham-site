📰 *SULABHAM · DAILY CURRENT AFFAIRS* | 1 NOV 2026
Important for APPSC Group 1 & 2

🏛️ *Cyclone 'Murasu' heads for south Andhra coast; landfall between Nellore and Narsapur on the night of 2–3 November*
🎯 Exam point: Naming trap: cyclones of the North Indian Ocean are named by RSMC New Delhi (IMD) from the WMO/ESCAP Panel list of 13 countries; a storm gets a name only when it reaches 'cyclonic storm' stage — not as a depression.

🏛️ *AP releases ₹9.86 crore to modernise 16 de-addiction centres; 3 to become centres of excellence*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: Article 47 (Part IV, DPSP) asks the State to raise nutrition and public health and to try to prohibit intoxicating drinks and harmful drugs except for medicinal use; DPSPs are not enforceable in courts. Trap: IMH is in Kadapa, KGH in Visakhapatnam.

📋 *Centre sets up two National Social Security Boards — for unorganised workers and for gig & platform workers; AP on the gig board*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: the Code on Social Security, 2020 is the first central law to define 'gig worker' and 'platform worker'; it is one of the four Labour Codes. Trap: the 5% cap is on payouts to workers, not on turnover.

💰 *Gross GST collection in October 2026: ₹2.31 lakh crore, up 22.9%; import GST up 42.2%*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: GST came on 1 July 2017 through the 101st Constitutional Amendment (2016); the GST Council is under Article 279A and is chaired by the Union Finance Minister. IGST is levied on inter-State supplies and imports. Trap: 'gross' is before refunds, 'net' is after refunds.

🌏 *PM Modi to visit Brussels in December to sign the India–EU Free Trade Agreement*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: European Council President (António Costa) ≠ European Commission President (Ursula von der Leyen). EU HQ institutions sit in Brussels; the EU has 27 members. In goods, India has a trade SURPLUS with the EU.

🛡️ *Air Chief Marshal Ashutosh Dixit takes charge as the 29th Chief of the Air Staff*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: the IAF was founded on 8 October 1932 (Air Force Day); the President is the Supreme Commander of the armed forces. Trap: Dixit is the 29th CAS — Amar Preet Singh was the 28th.

🏅 *Manisha Bhanwala becomes the first Indian woman to win gold at the Senior World Wrestling Championships*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: the venue is Astana, the capital of Kazakhstan (not Almaty). This is the Senior Worlds — not the Asian Championships, where she had won gold earlier.

🗓️ *50 years of Indian women's international cricket: first Test was on 31 October 1976 at Bengaluru*
🎯 Exam point: Chronology question likely: WCAI 1973 → first Test 1976 → first ODI 1978 → first World Cup final 2005 (lost to Australia) → merger with BCCI 2006.
