📰 *SULABHAM · DAILY CURRENT AFFAIRS* | 2 NOV 2026
Important for APPSC Group 1 & 2

🏛️ *Severe Cyclone Murasu crosses the coast tonight south of Ongole; IMD warns of a 1–1.5 m storm surge*
🎯 Exam point: Concept trap: a storm surge is caused by a cyclone's wind and low pressure — not by an earthquake (that is a tsunami). NDRF was created under the Disaster Management Act, 2005.

🏛️ *AP rural local body polls on 5 November: 9,829 MPTC and 659 ZPTC seats; counting on 7 November*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: panchayat and municipal elections are held by the State Election Commission (Art. 243K / 243ZA), NOT by the Election Commission of India (Art. 324). The SEC is appointed by the Governor.

💰 *Bengaluru–Mumbai Vande Bharat Sleeper starts (stops at Anantapur, Guntakal); design of India's own bullet train 'B28' ready*
🎯 Exam point: Don't mix up: Vande Bharat Sleeper = overnight semi-high-speed train on existing tracks; B28 = Swadeshi high-speed (bullet) train for the separate Mumbai–Ahmedabad HSR corridor.

💰 *Manufacturing PMI eases to 54.7 in October; export orders grow at a six-month high*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: a fall from 55.1 to 54.7 is slower growth, NOT contraction — only a reading below 50 means contraction. PMI is a private survey (S&P Global), unlike IIP, which is released by NSO (MoSPI).

📋 *EPFO marks its 74th Foundation Day: over 8 crore members and a ₹32 lakh crore corpus*
🎯 Exam point: Order trap: EPF 1952 → EDLI 1976 → EPS 1995 → Code on Social Security 2020. EPFO is under the Labour Ministry, not the Finance Ministry; ESIC (health insurance, 1948 Act) is a different body.

⚖️ *Kerala High Court turns 70; CJI Surya Kant flags rising 'urgent listing' requests to the Supreme Court*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: Anna Chandy = first woman High Court judge in India (Kerala); Fathima Beevi = first woman Supreme Court judge (1989). The Chief Justice is the 'master of the roster' in each court.

🔬 *Digi Yatra goes international: e-passport based trials at seven airports for flights abroad*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: the data is stored in the passenger's own phone wallet, not in a central government database; the trial is only for Indian e-passport holders, not foreign nationals. Hyderabad is the only Telugu-state airport in the trial.

🌏 *India hosts the first-ever BIMSTEC Maritime Security Exercise at Sri Vijaya Puram (30 Oct – 3 Nov)*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: Pakistan, Afghanistan and Maldives are NOT BIMSTEC members (they are in SAARC); Myanmar and Thailand are BIMSTEC members but not SAARC members.
