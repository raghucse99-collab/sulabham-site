🔑 *Answers — Daily Quiz* | 2 NOV 2026
1-(b) · 2-(d) · 3-(a) · 4-(c) · 5-(c) · 6-(a) · 7-(d) · 8-(b) · 9-(a) · 10-(c)

📖 Explanations:
1. (b) I and III are correct. II is the trap: a tsunami comes from an undersea earthquake/landslide; a storm surge comes from a cyclone's wind and low pressure. IMD named Bapatla, Prakasam and Krishna as most at risk.
2. (d) All three are correct. Trap to avoid: thinking the Election Commission of India (Art. 324) holds panchayat polls — it is the SEC (Art. 243K), under Part IX added by the 73rd Amendment.
3. (a) Guntakal (and Anantapur) are in Andhra Pradesh, Raichur is in Karnataka and Solapur in Maharashtra. Trap: Raichur and Guntakal are close to each other across the AP–Karnataka border.
4. (c) EPF Act 1952 → EDLI 1976 → EPS 1995 → Code on Social Security 2020. Trap: putting EPS (pension) before EDLI (insurance).
5. (c) II and III are correct. I is wrong: any reading above 50 means expansion; 54.7 is only slower growth than 55.1. IIP (not PMI) comes from NSO.
6. (a) Fathima Beevi became the first woman SC judge (1989); Anna Chandy was India's first woman HC judge (Kerala). CJI Surya Kant inaugurated the jubilee on 1 Nov 2026. Trap: swapping the two pioneers — both began at the Kerala HC.
7. (d) (d) is incorrect: only Indian nationals with e-passports on select Air India and IndiGo international flights can join. (a), (b) and (c) are correct as per the Civil Aviation Ministry.
8. (b) I and III are correct. II is the trap: Maldives and Pakistan are SAARC members, not BIMSTEC members. BIMSTEC = Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Myanmar, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Thailand.
9. (a) The SEC said counting of urban local body votes is on 3 November and police and election staff were needed for it (order issued on 28 October). (b) is a tempting trap — the cyclone came later; (c) is wrong because the ECI has no role in local body polls.
10. (c) I and III are correct. II mixes it up with the Vande Bharat Sleeper, which started on the Bengaluru–Mumbai route on 1 Nov. B28's design is ready and the first prototype may come in early 2027.
