📰 *SULABHAM · DAILY CURRENT AFFAIRS* | 3 NOV 2026
Important for APPSC Group 1 & 2

🏛️ *Severe Cyclone Murasu crosses the AP coast near Ongole early on 3 November; about 10,000 people moved to shelters*
🎯 Exam point: IMD rain scale (24 h): heavy 64.5–115.5 mm, very heavy 115.6–204.4 mm, extremely heavy ≥204.5 mm. Trap: 198 mm is 'very heavy', not 'extremely heavy'.

🏛️ *Counting for 92 urban local bodies in AP; cyclone delays counting in Prakasam and Bapatla*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: Article 243K = SEC for panchayats; 243ZA = SEC for municipalities. Both from the 73rd/74th Amendments (1992).

🌿 *'Nadi Sanjeevani': Centre and Bihar sign MoU to desilt rivers and use the silt to build national highways*
🎯 Exam point: Concept + AP link: silt on river beds raises flood risk (Bihar: Kosi, Gandak; AP: Godavari, Krishna deltas). Gandak and Kosi enter Bihar from Nepal — a common map question.

💰 *SEBI eases KYC review for foreign portfolio investors: review cycle up to 8 years from 1 December*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: FPI ≠ FDI. FPI = portfolio investment (below 10% of a company, no control); FDI = 10% or more, with lasting interest. SEBI regulates FPIs.

🔬 *Dinesh V. Ganvir takes charge as the 55th Director General of the Geological Survey of India*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: GSI is under the Ministry of Mines, not Earth Sciences; HQ Kolkata (not Hyderabad — Hyderabad is one of its six regional offices).

🛡️ *Air Marshal Mehtab Singh Deswal takes over the IAF's Eastern Air Command at Shillong*
🎯 Exam point: Static: IAF has 7 commands — 5 operational (Western–Delhi, South Western–Gandhinagar, Central–Prayagraj, Eastern–Shillong, Southern–Thiruvananthapuram) + Training (Bengaluru) + Maintenance (Nagpur).

🌏 *Iliana Iotova wins Bulgaria's presidential runoff — the country's first woman elected President*
🎯 Exam point: Map trap: Bulgaria borders the Black Sea (east) and Romania across the Danube (north) — not the Adriatic. Use it for 'match the country with sea' questions.

🏅 *India win both men's and women's titles at the first Commonwealth Kabaddi Championship in Ahmedabad*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: women's final opponent was Uganda (not Sri Lanka); men's was Sri Lanka. Pairs are a favourite 'match the following' item.

🗓️ *2 November: International Day to End Impunity for Crimes against Journalists*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: World Press Freedom Day is 3 May; National Press Day (India) is 16 November; 2 November is about impunity for crimes against journalists.
