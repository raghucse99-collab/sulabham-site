📰 *SULABHAM · DAILY CURRENT AFFAIRS* | 4 NOV 2026
Important for APPSC Group 1 & 2

🏛️ *Cyclones Arnab and Murasu: AP seeks central aid; PM Modi calls CM Naidu*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: SDRF = Sec 48(1)(a) and National Disaster Response Fund = Sec 46 of the Disaster Management Act, 2005. Extra NDRF money is approved by a high-level committee chaired by the Union HOME Minister (trap: not the PM), after an Inter-Ministerial Central Team (IMCT) visit.

🏛️ *AP urban local body results: ruling alliance wins 12 corporations and 83 municipalities*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: municipalities are in Part IXA (Articles 243P–243ZG), added by the 74th Amendment (1992); 12th Schedule lists 18 municipal functions. Trap: 11th Schedule (29 subjects) is for panchayats.

🛡️ *Tiger Triumph 2026: India–US tri-service disaster-relief exercise opens in Visakhapatnam*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: Tiger Triumph = India–US tri-service HADR (not navy-only). Visakhapatnam is HQ of the Eastern Naval Command.

🔬 *IndiaAI Mission releases home-grown AI models EKA-1, Dhīr 2B and Dhīr 5B*
🎯 Exam point: Link: 22 scheduled languages = Eighth Schedule of the Constitution. 'B' in a model name = billion parameters (bigger number = bigger model).

💰 *RBI eases FEMA reporting for small service exporters and importers (₹10 lakh limit)*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: FEMA, 1999 replaced FERA, 1973; FEMA violations are civil (penalty), FERA's were criminal. RBI makes FEMA regulations.

🌿 *COP31 at Antalya (9–20 Nov): India to press for climate finance and equity*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: COP31 is in Türkiye, but Australia holds the President of Negotiations post and co-hosted the Pre-COP with Fiji and Tuvalu. Paris Agreement Art 9.1 = finance duty of developed countries.

🌏 *Ganga Water Treaty with Bangladesh expires on 12 December; MEA says talks go through Joint Rivers Commission*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: the treaty was signed by Deve Gowda (1996), not I.K. Gujral or Vajpayee. Don't mix with the Indus Waters Treaty (1960, Pakistan, World Bank).

🌏 *US midterm elections: Democrats projected to win control of both House and Senate*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: in a midterm, ALL House seats but only about ONE-THIRD of Senate seats are contested (like India's Rajya Sabha, one-third retire every 2 years).

🩺 *Draft rule: every medicine batch to be released only by an authorised qualified person*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: the Act is of 1940, the Rules are of 1945. Drug quality is regulated centrally (CDSCO under the Health Ministry) along with state drug controllers.
