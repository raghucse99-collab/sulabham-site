📖 Explanations:
1. (a) I and III are correct. II is the trap: the high-level committee is chaired by the Union HOME Minister, not the PM. SDRF share is 75:25 for general states, 90:10 for NE & Himalayan states.
2. (b) I and III are correct. II is wrong: YSRCP won one corporation — Anantapur — and three municipalities.
3. (d) I, II and IV are correct. III is the trap: Tiger Triumph is a TRI-SERVICE HADR exercise (Navy, Army, Air Force, Special Forces).
4. (a) I and II are correct. III is wrong: EKA-1 has 26.3 billion parameters; Dhīr 5B has only 5 billion ('B' = billion parameters).
5. (b) I and II are correct. III is the trap: IDPMS reporting is needed only for NON-individual importers above ₹10 lakh; resident individuals are left out.
6. (d) A-3 (Antalya, Türkiye), B-4 (Murat Kurum), C-1 (Chris Bowen of Australia), D-2 (Pre-COP in the Pacific by Fiji, Tuvalu, Australia). Trap: Australia is not the host but holds the negotiations post.
7. (c) (c) is NOT India's position: India wants VOLUNTARY adaptation indicators that are NOT tied to access to climate finance. (a), (b) and (d) are India's stated positions.
8. (b) I, II and III are correct. IV is the trap: the treaty was signed by PM H.D. Deve Gowda with PM Sheikh Hasina. It expires on 12 Dec 2026 unless renewed.
9. (c) I and II are correct. III is wrong: senators have 6-year terms, so only about one-third of Senate seats are contested every two years; all 435 House seats are contested.
10. (c) All three are correct. Trap to remember: the Rules are of 1945, but the parent Drugs and Cosmetics Act is of 1940.
