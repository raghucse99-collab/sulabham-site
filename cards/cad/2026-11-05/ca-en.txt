📰 *SULABHAM · DAILY CURRENT AFFAIRS* | 5 NOV 2026
Important for APPSC Group 1 & 2

🏛️ *Drought in 412 mandals: CM Naidu asks Centre for 200 days of rural work and 'drought cards'*
🎯 Exam point: Concept: drought is declared by the STATE (as per the Centre's Manual for Drought Management); the Centre's IMCT then assesses damage for extra help. Anantapur is among India's driest districts — Rayalaseema = rain-shadow region.

🏛️ *ASI finds another 16th-century Tamil inscription of Achyutadevaraya at Tirumala temple*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: Achyuta Deva Raya (r. 1529–1542) of the Tuluva dynasty succeeded his half-brother Krishnadevaraya. Trap: Chandragiri was a later Vijayanagara capital (Aravidu period) — do not confuse with Hampi.

🏛️ *AP DSC-2026 (APTRT-2026) detailed notification: 2,721 teacher posts, applications from 11 November*
🎯 Exam point: For aspirants: SA = School Assistant (high school), SGT = Secondary Grade Teacher (primary). Special-education posts come from the RPwD Act, 2016 focus on inclusive education.

⚖️ *Supreme Court: laws punishing 'more than two children' have lost relevance; Javed (2003) sent to larger bench*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: the SC did NOT strike down the law — it referred Javed (2003) to a larger bench. Link: Article 243F lets State laws fix disqualifications for panchayat members. TFR 2.1 = replacement level.

⚖️ *SC seeks Centre and ECI reply on plea against lifelong legal immunity for CEC and Election Commissioners*
🎯 Exam point: Static: Article 324 creates the ECI. The 2023 Act set up a selection committee of PM + a Union Minister + Leader of Opposition (CJI not included). Trap: a notice is not a stay — Section 16 is still in force.

🛡️ *Cabinet allows two-year extension for Agniveers: service up to 6 years, path to regular service*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: the 25% retention is NOT changed; only the remaining 75% get the optional 2-year extension. Agniveers get a tax-free exit package (Seva Nidhi) but no pension.

📋 *Fast Track Special Courts for rape and POCSO cases extended till March 2031; courts to rise from 779 to 850*
🎯 Exam point: Static: POCSO Act, 2012 protects children under 18. Nirbhaya Fund was set up in 2013 (after the Dec 2012 Delhi case). Trap: FTSC is centrally SPONSORED (shared with States), not a central sector scheme.

💰 *VISTAR: ₹4,371 crore scheme to modernise India's statistical system; 8th Economic Census included*
🎯 Exam point: Static: Economic Census counts non-farm establishments; the 7th was in 2019. Trap: Economic Census ≠ Population Census (Census 2027 is by the Registrar General, MHA). Prof. P.C. Mahalanobis — father of Indian statistics.

🌿 *₹14,500 crore Viability Gap Funding for 92 GWh of grid-scale energy storage*
🎯 Exam point: Concept: VGF = one-time government grant that makes a useful but not-yet-profitable project viable. Note GWh (energy stored) vs GW (power). AP link: Pinnapuram (Nandyal) is a large pumped-storage project.

💰 *₹2,814 crore capex support for lithium and nickel processing plants*
🎯 Exam point: Link: National Critical Mineral Mission (2025) and KABIL (joint venture for overseas lithium assets, e.g. Argentina). Trap: this scheme is for PROCESSING, not mining. Lithium symbol Li, nickel Ni.

⚖️ *Bharatiya Vayuyan Niyam, 2026: new aviation rulebook replaces Aircraft Rules, 1937 from 1 January 2027*
🎯 Exam point: Trap pair: ADHINIYAM (2024) = the Act passed by Parliament, which replaced the Aircraft Act, 1934; NIYAM (2026) = the rules made under it. Regulator: DGCA.
