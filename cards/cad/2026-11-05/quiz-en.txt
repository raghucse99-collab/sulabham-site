📖 Explanations:
1. (b) I and III are correct. II is wrong: the IMCT visited Kurnool, Nandyal, Anantapur and Sri Sathya Sai — all Rayalaseema districts, not coastal.
2. (b) (b) is incorrect: the inscription is of Achyutadevaraya, Krishnadevaraya's successor (Tuluva dynasty). The trap is the more famous name. The other three are true.
3. (c) I and II are correct (2,721 posts; 150 SA + 200 SGT special education = 350). III is wrong: it is a computer-based test (CBT).
4. (d) II and III are correct. I is the trap: a two-judge bench cannot overrule a three-judge ruling, so Javed was REFERRED to a larger bench.
5. (a) Article 324 = ECI; Section 16 of the CEC & ECs Act, 2023 = immunity now challenged in the SC; Article 243F = disqualifications for panchayat membership (linked to the two-child norm case).
6. (a) II and III are correct. I is wrong: the 25% retention rule is unchanged; the remaining 75% get the optional extension (4 + 2 = 6 years).
7. (d) II and III are correct. I is wrong: FTSC is a Centrally SPONSORED scheme — Centre ₹2,353.15 cr + States/UTs ₹1,434.89 cr.
8. (b) I and II are correct. III is the trap: the population Census is done by the Registrar General & Census Commissioner (MHA); VISTAR covers the ECONOMIC Census.
9. (c) PSP 60 GWh > BESS 30 GWh > other 2 GWh. Trap: batteries are in the news more, but pumped storage gets twice the capacity.
10. (a) I and II are correct. III is wrong: the rules take effect from 1 January 2027, not on notification.
