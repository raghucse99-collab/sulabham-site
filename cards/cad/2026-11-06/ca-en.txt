Important for APPSC Group 1 & 2

🏛️ *Cyclone Murasu: AP puts first loss estimate at ₹1,919 crore*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: NDRF was created under the Disaster Management Act, 2005 (Centre); SDRF belongs to the state. Trap: Murasu made landfall near Ongole (Prakasam) — but most deaths were in Bapatla district.

🏛️ *AP MPTC/ZPTC elections: 67.24% polling; postponed seats vote on 6 November*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: panchayat elections are held by the State Election Commission (Art. 243K), NOT the Election Commission of India. 73rd Amendment (1992) → three tiers: Gram Panchayat – Mandal Parishad (MPTC) – Zilla Parishad (ZPTC).

🏛️ *TTD: three-officer 'Specified Authority' takes charge after Board's term ends*
🎯 Exam point: Remember the shape: 3 officers, headed by the EO, for an interim period. Trap: a Specified Authority is made of officials — not a new elected or nominated Board.

🛡️ *DRDO test-fires 'Pralay' quasi-ballistic missile in user trials; induction 'imminent'*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: Pralay is a SURFACE-to-surface tactical missile (150–500 km) — not a cruise missile and not an ICBM like Agni-V. Abdul Kalam Island was earlier called Wheeler Island.

🌏 *India–Guyana: three agreements and a new US$500 million Line of Credit*
🎯 Exam point: Connect: Guyana is in South America but a CARICOM (Caribbean) member; it has a large Indian-origin population. India exports its UPI/DPI model to Global South partners — a frequent exam theme.

🌏 *CHOGM 2026 ends in Antigua and Barbuda — the smallest host ever*
🎯 Exam point: Static: CHOGM meets every two years in rotating member countries. Trap: the Commonwealth Secretary-General (Shirley Ayorkor Botchwey) is different from the Head of the Commonwealth (King Charles III).

🏅 *India beat Japan 4–1 to win a record sixth Asian Champions Trophy in hockey*
🎯 Exam point: Remember: 6th title, beat JAPAN (not Korea — Korea was the semi-final, 8–1), venue Mohali. Trap: Asian Champions Trophy (Asian Hockey Federation) ≠ Asia Cup.

📊 *NITI Aayog's 'India S&T Scoreboard': states' research and innovation on one platform*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: the Global Innovation Index is published by WIPO — NITI Aayog only uses it; the new Scoreboard is a data platform, NOT a ranking of states.
