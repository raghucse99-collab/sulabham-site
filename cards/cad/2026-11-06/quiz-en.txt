📖 Explanations:
1. (b) I and III are correct. II is the trap: landfall was near Ongole (Prakasam), but most deaths were in Bapatla (5), then Guntur (2) and Palnadu (1).
2. (d) 2 (₹1,919 cr preliminary loss) > 3 (₹92 cr for 74 damaged rural roads) > 1 (₹37 cr released for immediate relief). Trap: the relief released is not the loss figure.
3. (c) (c) is incorrect: panchayat elections are held by the State Election Commission (Art. 243K), not the ECI (Art. 324). The others are true: 67.24%, highest Sri Sathya Sai 82.60%, lowest ASR 59%.
4. (a) ZPTC = Zilla Parishad Territorial Constituency member; MPTC = Mandal Parishad Territorial Constituency member; village level = ward members and Sarpanch. Trap (b) swaps ZPTC and MPTC.
5. (a) I and II are correct (EO Ravichandra is chairman; members Vinayachand and Kannababu). III is wrong: it works only until a new Board is formed.
6. (b) I and II are correct. III is the trap: the test was from Dr APJ Abdul Kalam Island off Odisha; Sriharikota (AP) is ISRO's satellite launch centre, not a missile range.
7. (c) I and II are correct. III is wrong: EAM Jaishankar and President Irfaan Ali signed them in St John's, Antigua, on the sidelines of CHOGM.
8. (d) I and III are correct. II is the trap: the CHOGM chair is the host's head of government — PM Gaston Browne. King Charles III is Head of the Commonwealth.
9. (b) II and III are correct. I is the trap: the final was against JAPAN (4–1); South Korea lost the semi-final 8–1.
10. (a) I and II are correct. III is wrong: the Global Innovation Index is published by WIPO (India 38th); NITI Aayog only refers to it.
