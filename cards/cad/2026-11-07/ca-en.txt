📰 *SULABHAM · DAILY CURRENT AFFAIRS* | 7 NOV 2026
Important for APPSC Group 1 & 2

🏛️ *Visakhapatnam to host the 31st CII Partnership Summit on 12–13 November*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: the summit is organised by CII — an industry body, not a government ministry. Remember the theme's three Ts: Trade, Technology, Trust; host city Visakhapatnam.

🏛️ *AP MPTC/ZPTC results: NDA alliance wins most seats; ZP chairs to be chosen next*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: Art. 243K (Part IX, 73rd Amendment) — the State Election Commission, not the ECI, conducts panchayat polls. ZPTC/MPTC members are elected directly; ZP chairpersons and MPPs indirectly. *Figures as of early 8 Nov.

💰 *Centre orders weekly rice & paddy stock declaration; AP and 4 southern states may fix stock limits*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: stock limits are imposed under the Essential Commodities Act, 1955. OMSS = government (through FCI) sells foodgrains in the open market to cool prices. Trap: Odisha is NOT among the five states.

🌏 *India and EU conclude talks for India to join 'Horizon Europe', the €93.5-billion research programme*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: talks are 'concluded', not yet in force — signing and ratification remain. India will be an 'associated country', not an EU member; funding comes directly from the European Commission.

🔬 *Semicon 2.0 application portal opens; 12 more electronics-component projects worth ₹7,503 crore cleared*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: Semicon 2.0 (chips: fabs, ATMP, design) and ECMS (passive parts like PCBs, copper foil, cathode material) are two different schemes — both under MeitY. '12' appears twice: 12 chip projects (phase one) and 12 new ECMS projects.

⚖️ *Uttarakhand Police receives the President's Police Colour — 16th state to get it*
🎯 Exam point: The 'Colour' is a flag given to a whole force in the President's name — it honours the unit, not one officer. Link: 'Police' and 'public order' are State List subjects (7th Schedule). Trap: 16th state, not 16th force overall.

🌿 *Delhi-NCR: CAQM brings in GRAP Stage-3 as AQI touches 380*
🎯 Exam point: GRAP has 4 stages (Stage I 'poor' AQI 201–300 up to Stage IV above 450). Trap: GRAP is run by CAQM — not the CPCB or the Delhi government; vehicle curb = BS-III petrol & BS-IV diesel (not BS-VI).

🛡️ *IAF's IL-76MD lands at Nyoma in Ladakh — its highest-altitude landing so far*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: Daulat Beg Oldie is still the highest advanced landing ground, but it cannot take an IL-76 — the record here is for the IL-76 type. LAC = India–China border line; Ladakh is a Union Territory.

🏅 *Mohammed Shami retires from Test cricket after 13 years*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: Shami quit only Test cricket — not ODIs/T20Is. Among Indian pacers in Tests he is 6th; Kapil Dev is first.
