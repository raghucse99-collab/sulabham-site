📰 *SULABHAM · DAILY CURRENT AFFAIRS* | 8 NOV 2026
Important for APPSC Group 1 & 2

🏛️ *Andhra Pradesh wins six Swachh Survekshan awards; President to present them on 11 November*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: Kuppam is a MUNICIPALITY, not a municipal corporation. Swachh Survekshan is run by the Housing & Urban Affairs Ministry (urban) — not the Jal Shakti Ministry, which handles SBM-Grameen.

⚖️ *Supreme Court: commercial crop cultivation is not a 'forest right' in a tiger reserve*
🎯 Exam point: FRA 2006 recognises rights of forest-dwelling STs and Other Traditional Forest Dwellers (OTFD = 3 generations/75 years before 13 Dec 2005). Trap: FRA protects naturally occurring minor forest produce — not planned commercial plantations.

💰 *Petroleum Ministry: E20 petrol meets BIS standard IS 17021, including in Karnataka*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: the 2022 amendment to the National Policy on Biofuels advanced the 20% blending target from 2030 to 2025-26. Trap: IS standards are set by BIS (under the Consumer Affairs Ministry), not by the Petroleum Ministry.

📋 *Kashi Tamil Sangamam 5.0: 1,000 institutions invited; students to study Thirukkural*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: Kashi Tamil Sangamam is organised by the Ministry of EDUCATION (not Culture). Thirukkural = Thiruvalluvar; 1,330 couplets in 3 parts — Aram (virtue), Porul (wealth), Inbam (love).

📋 *Piprahwa relics of the Buddha move to Bomdila (8–13 Nov) on their Arunachal Pradesh tour*
🎯 Exam point: Order trap: Tawang → Bomdila → Namsai (west to east). Piprahwa is in Uttar Pradesh near the Nepal border — not in Bihar.

🛡️ *'Rahat Mitra': first India–Myanmar tri-services disaster-relief exercise held in Chennai*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: Rahat Mitra was held in CHENNAI (India), not in Myanmar. MAHASAGAR (2025) builds on SAGAR (2015). Four Indian states border Myanmar.

🌏 *Sudhakar Dalela takes up post as India's Ambassador to Nepal*
🎯 Exam point: Five Indian states border Nepal: Uttarakhand, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, West Bengal, Sikkim. Trap: Himachal Pradesh does NOT border Nepal.

🏅 *India's V. Pranav wins the World Junior Blitz Chess Championship in Al Ain*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: Pranav won BLITZ gold but RAPID silver. Formats by time: classical (slowest) > rapid > blitz (fastest).
