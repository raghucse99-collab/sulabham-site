🔑 *Answers — Daily Quiz* | 8 NOV 2026
1-(a) · 2-(c) · 3-(b) · 4-(d) · 5-(b) · 6-(c) · 7-(c) · 8-(d) · 9-(a) · 10-(b)

📖 Explanations:
1. (a) Only I. II is wrong: President Droupadi Murmu presents them (11 Nov, New Delhi). III is the trap: Swachh Survekshan (urban) is run by the Housing & Urban Affairs Ministry; Jal Shakti handles SBM-Grameen.
2. (c) Kuppam is a municipality. Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada, Guntur, Rajahmundry and Tirupati are the five municipal corporations that won.
3. (b) I and III are correct. II is the trap: the Court said those living outside the forest (and temporary residents) cannot become forest dwellers just by cultivating.
4. (d) OTFD = at least 3 generations (one generation = 25 years, so 75 years) before the cut-off date 13 December 2005. Option (b) has the right period but the wrong cut-off date.
5. (b) I and II are correct. III is wrong: the Ministry said all 56 Karnataka samples conformed, with ethanol at 19–20%; it rejected the state's excess-ethanol claim.
6. (c) II and III are correct. I is the trap: the Ministry of EDUCATION organises Kashi Tamil Sangamam.
7. (c) Tawang (1–7 Nov) → Bomdila (8–13 Nov) → Namsai (14–20 Nov), west to east; the relics return to the National Museum, Delhi, on 21 Nov.
8. (d) All three are correct. Trap to avoid: thinking it was held in Myanmar or that it was a combat exercise — it was a disaster-relief (HADR) exercise in Chennai.
9. (a) Nepal borders five Indian states: Uttarakhand, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, West Bengal and Sikkim. Himachal Pradesh borders China (Tibet), not Nepal.
10. (b) Pranav (India) won open blitz gold with 10.5/13; Kleymenova (Russia) women's blitz gold; Orozbaev (Kyrgyzstan) silver. Trap: Pranav's medal in RAPID was silver, not gold.
