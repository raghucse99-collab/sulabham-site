📰 *SULABHAM · DAILY CURRENT AFFAIRS* | 9 NOV 2026
Important for APPSC Group 1 & 2

🏛️ *AP gram panchayat polls in three phases — 26 Nov, 29 Nov and 2 Dec; schedule on 13 November*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: panchayat polls are run by the State Election Commission under Article 243K (73rd Amendment) — NOT the Election Commission of India (Art. 324).

🏛️ *Air quality worsens in AP after Deepavali: Visakhapatnam (AQI 264) and Rajamahendravaram (220) 'poor'*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: PM2.5 (fine particles, ≤2.5 micrometres) — not PM10 — was the main pollutant in Visakhapatnam. AQI 201–300 = 'poor', 101–200 = 'moderate'.

🩺 *Lower MRPs for 1,364 anti-cancer medicines come into force; patients to save ₹3,540 crore a year*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: NPPA is under the Ministry of Chemicals & Fertilisers (Dept of Pharmaceuticals), not the Health Ministry; prices are controlled under the Drugs (Prices Control) Order, 2013.

🌿 *COP31 climate summit opens in Antalya, Türkiye (9–20 Nov); Australia leads the negotiations*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: COP = Conference of the Parties to the UNFCCC (1992, Rio). Article 9.1 of the Paris Agreement (2015) says developed countries shall provide finance to developing countries.

🛡️ *Navy's largest-ever Agniveer batch — over 3,300 trainees — to pass out at INS Chilka on 13 November*
🎯 Exam point: Link: Agnipath scheme (2022) recruits Agniveers into the armed forces. Trap: INS Chilka is in Odisha, under the Southern Naval Command — not in Kerala.

📋 *Delhi gets its first recreational Yamuna river cruise (Sonia Vihar–Jagatpur) with electric vessels*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: the National Waterways Act, 2016 declared 111 national waterways (incl. 5 earlier ones). NW-1 = Ganga (Haldia–Prayagraj); NW-4 includes Krishna & Godavari in AP. Yamuna = NW-110.

🏅 *Anuj Shrivatri becomes India's 100th chess Grandmaster — the first from Madhya Pradesh*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: the 100th GM is Anuj Shrivatri (MP), not V. Pranav (World Junior Blitz champion, 7 Nov). Titles (GM, IM) are awarded by FIDE.

🗓️ *9 November: Uttarakhand Statehood Day — the state turns 26*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: Bhararisain (Gairsain) is the SUMMER capital, Dehradun the WINTER capital. Old name Uttaranchal changed in 2007.
