🏛️ *AP Cabinet clears AVGC-XR and Robotics & Physical AI policies; 512 MW AI data centre near Visakhapatnam*
🎯 Exam point: AVGC-XR = Animation, Visual Effects, Gaming, Comics, Extended Reality. Trap: the 512 MW AI data centre is at Bheemunipatnam (Visakhapatnam dist.); Bhogapuram (Vizianagaram) is the Reliance land.

🏛️ *AP to study Small Modular Reactors (SMRs) as an alternative to thermal power; biogas and wind projects cleared*
🎯 Exam point: An SMR is still nuclear FISSION — 'small' refers to size and factory-built modules. Trap: the lead BSMR-200/SMR-55 units are proposed at Tarapur; BARC Vizag is for the 5 MWt hydrogen reactor.

🏛️ *AP Cabinet: new Directorate for Traffic Management and Road Safety at Mangalagiri; land for ITI and Kendriya Vidyalaya*
🎯 Exam point: Static: 'Public order' (Entry 1) and 'Police' (Entry 2) are in the STATE List (List II, Seventh Schedule) — so a state can create a new police directorate. Trap: not the Union or Concurrent List.

🏛️ *APPSC Group 2: vacancies raised to 720 in 30 categories; comprehensive notification due on 10 November*
🎯 Exam point: Static: State Public Service Commission — Art. 315; Chairman and members are appointed by the Governor (Art. 316) but can be removed only by the President (Art. 317). Trap: the Governor cannot remove them.

⚖️ *Ladakh: election process begins for hill councils in all 8 districts — 100 constituencies*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: Ladakh is a Union Territory WITHOUT a legislature (J&K is a UT WITH one) — so its elected hill councils are the main elected local bodies.

⚖️ *Delhi notifies Right of Citizen to Time-Bound and Ease of Delivery of Services Act, 2026*
🎯 Exam point: Right to services is a STATUTORY right (made by a state law), not a Fundamental Right in Part III. Madhya Pradesh passed the first such state law in 2010.

🌏 *India and Lebanon hold their first-ever Foreign Office Consultations in New Delhi*
🎯 Exam point: FOCs are official-level (Secretary) talks, not ministerial summits. Static link: Indian troops serve in UNIFIL, the UN peacekeeping force in south Lebanon.

🌏 *PM Modi speaks to Ukraine's President Zelenskyy; Russia–Ukraine talks open in Abu Dhabi*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: Abu Dhabi is the capital of the UAE (not Saudi Arabia; Saudi capital is Riyadh). India's line on the war — dialogue and diplomacy.

🔬 *Mines Minister calls for faster exploration of critical minerals and rare-earth elements; new GSI facilities in Hyderabad*
🎯 Exam point: Critical minerals (e.g. lithium, cobalt, rare earths) power batteries, electronics and defence. Trap: GSI is under the Ministry of Mines (not Earth Sciences) and its HQ is Kolkata; GSITI is in Hyderabad.
