1-(b) · 2-(d) · 3-(a) · 4-(c) · 5-(b) · 6-(c) · 7-(a) · 8-(d) · 9-(d) · 10-(b)

📖 Explanations:
1. (b) I and II are correct. III is the trap: Reliance's land (about 25 acres) is at Bhogapuram (Vizianagaram); Rambilli (Anakapalli) is the Epack unit.
2. (d) A-3 (Naidupeta), B-4 (Rambilli), C-1 (Tirupati), D-2 (Nakkapalli Bulk Drug Park, 173.82 acres). Options swapping Naidupeta and Tirupati are the trap — both are in Tirupati district.
3. (a) I and II are correct. III is wrong: the lead BSMR-200 and SMR-55 units are proposed at Tarapur (Maharashtra); BARC Vizag is for a 5 MWt high-temperature gas-cooled reactor for hydrogen.
4. (c) (c) is incorrect: Public order (Entry 1) and Police (Entry 2) are in the State List (List II), which is why AP can create a police directorate. (a), (b), (d) are cabinet decisions of 10 Nov.
5. (b) I and II are correct. III is the trap: the Governor appoints SPSC members (Art. 316) but only the President can remove them (Art. 317).
6. (c) I and III are correct. II is wrong: Leh has the most seats (22); Kargil is second (21). Ladakh became a UT without a legislature on 31 Oct 2019.
7. (a) I and II are correct. III is the trap: right to services is a statutory right created by state law (first: Madhya Pradesh, 2010), not a Fundamental Right.
8. (d) I and III are correct. II is the trap: India's side was led by Sripriya Ranganathan, Secretary (CPV & OIA), not the Foreign Secretary.
9. (d) I and III are correct. II is wrong: Abu Dhabi is the capital of the UAE; Saudi Arabia's capital is Riyadh.
10. (b) I and II are correct. III is wrong: GSITI is in Hyderabad, where the Mines Minister opened new facilities on 10 November.
