📰 *SULABHAM · DAILY CURRENT AFFAIRS* | 11 NOV 2026
Important for APPSC Group 1 & 2

🏛️ *31st CII Partnership Summit opens in Visakhapatnam on 12–13 November*
🎯 Exam point: CII = Confederation of Indian Industry (a non-government industry body). Trap: the summit is a Commerce Ministry–CII–State event, not a NITI Aayog event; the series began in 1995.

🏛️ *AP SEC notifies indirect elections: MPP presidents on 19 Nov, Zilla Parishad chairpersons on 20 Nov*
🎯 Exam point: 73rd Amendment link: Art. 243K — SEC conducts panchayat polls; Art. 243C(5) — chairpersons at intermediate and district level are elected indirectly. Trap: ZP chairperson is NOT directly elected.

🏛️ *C.P. Brown's birth anniversary (10 Nov): the Englishman who saved Telugu literature*
🎯 Exam point: AP culture link: Brown = 'saviour of Telugu literature'. Trap: he was born in Calcutta (not England) and his Telugu work began at Kadapa.

⚖️ *Supreme Court asks WhatsApp and other intermediaries for steps to curb 'digital arrest' scams*
🎯 Exam point: Suo motu = the court acts on its own. Trap: 'digital arrest' is not a legal procedure; I4C is under the Home Ministry, not MeitY.

🌏 *India and France hold first Economic Security and Critical Technologies Dialogue in Paris*
🎯 Exam point: Remember the roadmaps: Horizon 2047 (strategic partnership) and Innovation Roadmap 2030. Trap: the dialogue was held in Paris, not New Delhi.

🌏 *India–Saudi Arabia Haj Agreement 2027: India's quota is 1,75,025 pilgrims*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: Haj is handled by the Ministry of Minority Affairs, not the Ministry of External Affairs.

💰 *Goyal: India on track for $1 trillion exports this year; 48% of target achieved*
🎯 Exam point: 1 trillion = 1 lakh crore dollars. Link: Foreign Trade Policy is issued by the Commerce Ministry (DGFT).

💰 *ADB approves $98 million loan for Sri Lanka's digital economy*
🎯 Exam point: Static GK: ADB 1966, HQ Manila. Trap: AIIB (HQ Beijing) and NDB (HQ Shanghai) are different banks.

🗓️ *Ropuiliani Tribal Freedom Fighters Museum near Aizawl to be inaugurated on 15 November*
🎯 Exam point: 15 November = Janjatiya Gaurav Divas (Birsa Munda's birth anniversary). Trap: the museum is under the Ministry of Tribal Affairs, not the Ministry of Culture.

🗓️ *Manipur celebrates Ningol Chakouba — festival of married women and their brothers*
🎯 Exam point: Match-the-festival questions: Ningol Chakouba – Manipur. Do not confuse with Hornbill (Nagaland) or Chapchar Kut (Mizoram).
