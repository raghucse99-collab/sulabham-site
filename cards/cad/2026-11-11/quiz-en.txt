1-(c) · 2-(a) · 3-(d) · 4-(b) · 5-(c) · 6-(a) · 7-(b) · 8-(d) · 9-(a) · 10-(c)

📖 Explanations:
1. (c) I and II are correct. III is the trap: the summit series began in 1995, not 2005. (a)/(b) leave out a correct statement; (d) includes the wrong year.
2. (a) I and II are correct. III is wrong: ZP chairpersons are elected indirectly by ZPTC members (here by show of hands on 20 Nov), as Art. 243C(5) allows. So (c) and (d) fail; (b) misses I.
3. (d) Results 7 Nov → MPP presidents 19 Nov → ZP chairpersons 20 Nov → gram panchayat phase 1 on 26 Nov. Trap in (a): ZP comes after MPP, not before.
4. (b) (b) is incorrect: in 1820 he served as Deputy Collector in Kadapa, not Visakhapatnam. (a), (c) and (d) are correct facts about Brown.
5. (c) I, II and III are correct. IV is the trap: 'digital arrest' is a fraud — no law allows arrest over a video call. (b) and (d) include IV; (a) misses III.
6. (a) Haj agreement – Jeddah; India–France dialogue – Paris; CII summit – Visakhapatnam; Ropuiliani museum – Kelsih near Aizawl. (b) swaps Jeddah/Paris, (c) swaps Vizag/Kelsih, (d) swaps Jeddah/Vizag.
7. (b) I and III are correct. II is wrong: the inaugural meeting was in Paris on 10 Nov. (c) and (d) include II; (a) misses III.
8. (d) All three are correct: quota 1,75,025; signed in Jeddah by Kiren Rijiju (Minority Affairs) and Tawfiq bin Fawzan Al-Rabiah (Hajj and Umrah). Trap: many think Haj is under External Affairs — it is not.
9. (a) I, II and III are correct. IV is the trap: ADB's HQ is Manila; Beijing hosts the AIIB. (c) and (d) include IV; (b) misses II.
10. (c) I, II and IV are correct. III is wrong: the museum was built under the Ministry of Tribal Affairs. (b) and (d) include III; (a) misses IV.
