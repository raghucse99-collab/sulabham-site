📰 *SULABHAM · DAILY CURRENT AFFAIRS* | 12 NOV 2026
Important for APPSC Group 1 & 2

🏛️ *Swachh Survekshan 2025-26: Visakhapatnam cleanest million-plus city; Vijayawada 3rd in Super Swachh League*
🎯 Exam point: Super Swachh League = cities that have topped earlier years, ranked separately. Trap: Visakhapatnam won the million-plus category; Vijayawada is in the Super Swachh League (3rd). Puri (Odisha) is the first Swachh Coastal Town.

🏛️ *AP SEC: polls to the remaining 23 urban local bodies on 26 November*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: State Election Commission — Article 243K (panchayats) and 243ZA (municipalities), added by the 73rd/74th Amendments. Trap: the SEC, not the Election Commission of India, conducts local body polls.

🏛️ *CII Partnership Summit day 1: Naidu seeks an Investment Protection Act, announces Hydrogen Valley*
🎯 Exam point: Escrow account = money kept with a third party (bank) and released only for a fixed purpose — here, assured payment of incentives to investors. Link: Green Hydrogen Mission (2023).

📋 *Cabinet extends Credit Guarantee Fund for Micro Units (Mudra) and Stand-Up India till March 2031*
🎯 Exam point: PMMY (2015) gives collateral-free loans to non-farm micro units. Trap: Stand-Up India loans are ₹10 lakh–₹1 crore for women/SC/ST — not the small Mudra loans.

💰 *Nutrient Based Subsidy rates for Rabi 2026-27: about ₹62,370 crore for P&K fertilisers*
🎯 Exam point: NBS began in 2010 for P&K fertilisers. Trap: urea is NOT under NBS — its retail price is fixed by the government separately.

💰 *Moody's raises India's GDP growth forecast for this fiscal year to 7% from 6.5%*
🎯 Exam point: Moody's, S&P and Fitch are the 'Big Three' credit rating agencies (all US-based). Trap: a growth forecast is not a sovereign credit rating change.

📋 *Cabinet decision: North & South Block to become the 'Yuge Yugeen Bharat' National Museum*
🎯 Exam point: North & South Block (designed by Herbert Baker) housed key ministries; they moved out under the Central Vista project. 'Yuga' = age/era — the name means 'India through the ages'.

🌿 *Maharashtra gets its first Blue Flag beaches: Ladghar, Guhagar and Shrivardhan*
🎯 Exam point: Blue Flag is given by the Foundation for Environmental Education (FEE), Denmark. AP link: Rushikonda beach, Visakhapatnam is a Blue Flag beach. Trap: it is not a UNEP award.
