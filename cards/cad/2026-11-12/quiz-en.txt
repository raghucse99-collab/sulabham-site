🔑 *Answers — Daily Quiz* | 12 NOV 2026
1-(c) · 2-(a) · 3-(d) · 4-(b) · 5-(a) · 6-(b) · 7-(c) · 8-(d) · 9-(d) · 10-(b)

📖 Explanations:
1. (c) I and III are correct (Tirupati–Kuppam came 3rd in Swachh Shehar Jodi). II is the trap: Navi Mumbai is first in the Super Swachh League; Vijayawada is 3rd.
2. (a) Puri (Odisha) = Coastal Town; Rewa (MP) = River Front Town; Rudrapur (Uttarakhand) = Himalayan City. Trap: Rewa is not a coastal town.
3. (d) I and II are correct. III is the trap: local body polls are conducted by the State Election Commission (Art. 243K/243ZA), not the ECI.
4. (b) The four are Srikakulam, Kakinada, Rajamahendravaram and Mangalagiri-Tadepalli. Nellore is not in the list.
5. (a) I and II are correct. III is wrong: the venue is the AU Engineering College grounds, Visakhapatnam.
6. (b) I and II are correct. III is the trap: Stand-Up India began in April 2016; PM Mudra Yojana began in 2015.
7. (c) (c) is incorrect: urea is outside NBS; its price is fixed separately. The other three are correct.
8. (d) I and II are correct. III is wrong: Moody's is a US-based global agency (one of the 'Big Three' with S&P and Fitch).
9. (d) All three are correct. Common trap: thinking Blue Flag is a UNEP or Indian government award — it is given by FEE, Denmark.
10. (b) It will come up in the historic North and South Block, freed after ministries moved under the Central Vista project. Trap: the old Parliament House is a different building (Samvidhan Sadan).
