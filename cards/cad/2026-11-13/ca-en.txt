📰 *SULABHAM · DAILY CURRENT AFFAIRS* | 13 NOV 2026
Important for APPSC Group 1 & 2

🏛️ *CII Partnership Summit ends in Visakhapatnam: about ₹5.84 lakh crore commitments on day 1, 11 more MoUs on day 2*
🎯 Exam point: Match company ↔ district is a likely question. Trap: Kia's plant is at Penukonda in Sri Sathya Sai district (not Anantapur after reorganisation); an MoU is only a promise — G.O.s are the approved projects.

🏛️ *AP gram panchayat elections: first-phase notification issued; polling on 26 Nov, 29 Nov and 2 Dec*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: SEC is in Article 243K — the State Election Commissioner is appointed by the GOVERNOR (trap: not the President, not ECI). Panchayats = Part IX (73rd Amendment); municipalities = Part IXA (74th).

🌿 *Third cyclone of the season likely over the Bay of Bengal around 18 Nov; it would be named 'Akvan' (given by Iran)*
🎯 Exam point: Static GK: North Indian Ocean cyclone names come from a list of the WMO/ESCAP Panel (13 members); names are used in order and IMD New Delhi announces them. Trap: a 'low' → 'depression' → 'cyclone' — a name is given only when it becomes a cyclonic storm.

💰 *Retail inflation rises to 6.09% in October 2026 (CPI base 2024=100); Andhra Pradesh at 7.25%*
🎯 Exam point: CPI is compiled by NSO (MoSPI), not RBI or the Labour Bureau; the new base year is 2024. RBI's flexible inflation target (4% ± 2%) is set by the Centre under the RBI Act. Trap: in Oct 2026 rural inflation was HIGHER than urban.

🩺 *WHO Global TB Report 2026: India's TB deaths down 38% since 2015; treatment success 94%*
🎯 Exam point: India's TB elimination target is 2025 (SDG target 2030). Trap: the Global TB Report is published by WHO, not by the Health Ministry; 94% is the treatment SUCCESS rate, 38% is the fall in MORTALITY.

📊 *NITI Aayog report on PLI schemes: nearly 70% of targeted segments on track*
🎯 Exam point: PLI = incentive on INCREMENTAL sales of goods made in India (Atmanirbhar Bharat). Trap: the partner is IIM Ahmedabad; drones are a success story, while ACC batteries lag.

🌏 *PM to visit Manila on 16–17 Nov for the 23rd ASEAN-India Summit and the 21st East Asia Summit*
🎯 Exam point: India became a sectoral partner of ASEAN in 1992 (hence 35 years in 2027); Look East (1991) became Act East (2014); relations were raised to Comprehensive Strategic Partnership in 2022. Trap: EAS has 18 members incl. USA & Russia — it is not the same as ASEAN.

🛡️ *Exercise Mitra Shakti 2026 with Sri Lanka begins; INS Sahyadri ends Japan visit after Malabar 2026*
🎯 Exam point: Exercise–country pairs are a favourite match question: Mitra Shakti (Sri Lanka, army), SLINEX (Sri Lanka, navy), Malabar (Quad navies), Yudh Abhyas (USA, army), Ekuverin (Maldives). Trap: Mitra Shakti ≠ Maitree (Thailand).

🩺 *Cheaper cancer medicines: NPPA orders strict monitoring of trade-margin cap on 137 anti-cancer drugs*
🎯 Exam point: NPPA (set up 1997) fixes ceiling prices of SCHEDULED drugs (NLEM) and monitors non-scheduled ones; TMR is a tool for non-scheduled drugs. Trap: NPPA is under the Chemicals & Fertilizers Ministry, not the Health Ministry.
