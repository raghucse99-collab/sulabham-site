1-(b) · 2-(d) · 3-(c) · 4-(a) · 5-(c) · 6-(b) · 7-(d) · 8-(a) · 9-(b) · 10-(d)

📖 Explanations:
1. (b) I and II are correct (73 MoUs + 15 G.O.s on day 1). III is the trap: Penukonda is in Sri Sathya Sai district; Kadapa got the JSW investment.
2. (d) Reliance → East Godavari, Rashmi Metallurgical → Srikakulam (with GAIL), Kia → Penukonda (Sri Sathya Sai), Megha Engineering → Krishna. Option (b) swaps Rashmi and Kia — the common trap.
3. (c) I and II are correct. III is wrong: under Article 243K the State Election Commissioner is appointed by the Governor; the Election Commission of India does not hold panchayat polls.
4. (a) (a) is incorrect: Arnab and Murasu struck the Andhra Pradesh coast (Bay of Bengal side). (b), (c), (d) are correct — a low or depression gets no name.
5. (c) I, III and IV are correct (AP 7.25% vs India 6.09%). II is the trap: rural inflation (6.63%) was higher than urban (5.41%).
6. (b) I, II and IV are correct (global fall 35%). III is wrong: the treatment success rate is 94%, not 84% — a number-swap trap.
7. (d) All three are correct. Traps to avoid: the partner is IIM Ahmedabad (not IIM Bangalore), and the slow sectors are ACC batteries, specialty steel and technical textiles — not drones.
8. (a) Mitra Shakti = India–Sri Lanka army (12th edition, Maduru Oya); Malabar = naval exercise with Japan, USA, Australia; Yudh Abhyas = India–USA army; Ekuverin = India–Maldives. (b) wrongly swaps Malabar and Yudh Abhyas.
9. (b) I and II are correct. III is wrong: dialogue relations began in 1992, so 2027 marks 35 years (and 5 years of the Comprehensive Strategic Partnership).
10. (d) (d) is incorrect: NPPA is under the Department of Pharmaceuticals, Ministry of Chemicals & Fertilizers. (a), (b), (c) are correct as per the NPPA order (revised prices from 9 Nov 2026).
