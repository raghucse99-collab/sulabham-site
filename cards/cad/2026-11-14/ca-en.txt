📰 *SULABHAM · DAILY CURRENT AFFAIRS* | 14 NOV 2026
Important for APPSC Group 1 & 2

🏛️ *IMD: low over Bay of Bengal to become Cyclone 'Akvan' by 16 Nov evening; north AP and south Odisha likely in its path*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: cyclone names come from the WMO/ESCAP Panel list of 13 countries, used in order. Trap: a 'depression' is NOT yet a cyclone — the name is given only at the cyclonic-storm stage.

🏛️ *AP's proposed Investment Protection Act: promises to investors to stay binding even if the government changes*
🎯 Exam point: Polity link: a State law on a Concurrent-List matter that conflicts with a central law needs the President's assent (Art. 254(2)) — hence 'send it to the Centre'. Trap: it is a proposal, not an enacted Act.

🔬 *ISRO tests the redesigned PSLV third stage (PS3) at Sriharikota after two mission failures*
🎯 Exam point: Static GK: PSLV has 4 stages — PS1 solid, PS2 liquid, PS3 solid, PS4 liquid (alternate). Trap: PS3 is solid, not liquid or cryogenic. SDSC SHAR is in Tirupati district of AP.

🗓️ *15 November — Janjatiya Gaurav Diwas: Birsa Munda's 151st birth anniversary; PM's programme at Gadchiroli*
🎯 Exam point: Constitution link: Scheduled Tribes are notified by the President under Article 342; Fifth Schedule = Scheduled Areas. Trap: DAJGUA = Dharti Aaba Janjatiya Gram Utkarsh Abhiyan, not a Birsa Munda scholarship.

🏆 *President presents National Sports Awards 2025: 17 Arjuna awards, no Khel Ratna this time*
🎯 Exam point: Match award ↔ year started and award ↔ type (player / coach / organisation). Trap: Dronacharya is for coaches, Rashtriya Khel Protsahan Puraskar is for organisations, not players.

🏅 *Youth Olympics, Dakar 2026: India's first-ever boxing golds — Gunjan and Ambekar Meetei; 10 medals in all*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: Dakar's 10 medals are India's SECOND-best, not best (2018 had 13). Gunjan — 48 kg women; Ambekar Meetei — 50 kg men.

🛡️ *INS Betwa to get diesel engines: ₹498.58 crore refit contract with Cochin Shipyard*
🎯 Exam point: Indian Navy ships are often named after rivers (Betwa is a tributary of the Yamuna). Trap: the contract is with Cochin Shipyard (Kerala), not Mazagon Dock or GRSE.

💰 *45th India International Trade Fair opens at Bharat Mandapam (13–27 Nov); theme 'Vikas aur Virasat'*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: ITPO is under the Ministry of Commerce & Industry; Bharat Mandapam is at Pragati Maidan (G20 Summit venue, 2023). Trap: Partner vs Focus States are different lists.
