1-(b) · 2-(c) · 3-(a) · 4-(d) · 5-(b) · 6-(a) · 7-(c) · 8-(d) · 9-(a) · 10-(c)

📖 Explanations:
1. (b) I and II are correct. III is the trap: Akvan would be the THIRD cyclone, after Arnab and Murasu.
2. (c) I and II are correct. III is wrong: the bill is in its final stage and is likely in the next Assembly session — it is not yet law.
3. (a) All three are correct. Trap: many think PS3 is liquid — PSLV alternates solid (PS1, PS3) and liquid (PS2, PS4).
4. (d) (d) is incorrect: Gadchiroli is in Maharashtra, not Jharkhand. Jharkhand is linked to Birsa Munda, which makes it a tempting trap.
5. (b) I and II are correct. III is wrong: the Lifetime Arjuna went to I. Arumainayagam, a footballer from the 1962 Asian Games gold team.
6. (a) Arjuna 1961 (oldest), Dronacharya 1985, Khel Ratna 1991-92, RKPP 2009. Trap: Khel Ratna is the highest award but NOT the oldest.
7. (c) I and III are correct. II is the trap: Dakar is India's second-best; the best was 13 medals in 2018.
8. (d) All three are correct (₹498.58 crore refit and re-powering, 50+ MSMEs). Trap: 're-powering' means a new engine type (steam → diesel), not nuclear propulsion.
9. (a) I, II, III are correct. IV is wrong: Partner States are Bihar, Maharashtra, Rajasthan and Uttar Pradesh.
10. (c) Betwa contract 11 Nov → PS3 test 12 Nov → Sports Awards 13 Nov → Janjatiya Gaurav Diwas 15 Nov.
