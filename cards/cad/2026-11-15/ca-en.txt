📰 *SULABHAM · DAILY CURRENT AFFAIRS* | 15 NOV 2026
Important for APPSC Group 1 & 2

🏛️ *Bay of Bengal system becomes a depression; IMD: cyclonic storm on 16 Nov, severe cyclonic storm on 17 Nov, heading towards north Andhra*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: IMD scale — depression → deep depression → cyclonic storm (62–88 km/h) → severe (89–117) → very severe (118–165). Trap: IMD bulletins now say 'Sri Vijaya Puram' — that is the new name of Port Blair.

🛡️ *IMNEX-26: 8th India–Myanmar naval exercise held at Visakhapatnam (10–14 Nov)*
🎯 Exam point: AP link: Visakhapatnam is HQ of the Eastern Naval Command. Static: Myanmar shares a 1,643 km border with India (Arunachal, Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram). Trap: MAHASAGAR is the vision; SAGAR was the older 2015 doctrine.

📊 *Access Equality Index 2026: Andhra Pradesh 2nd among 28 States, 1st in socio-economic security*
🎯 Exam point: Concept: access to services is not the same as income — a poorer State can still give more equal access. Trap: Goa (not AP or Kerala) is 1st; AP is 1st only in the socio-economic security pillar.

💰 *October 2026 trade data: total exports US$ 89.99 billion (+29.9%); overall trade deficit narrows to US$ 20.09 billion*
🎯 Exam point: Concept: trade balance = exports − imports. India has a goods deficit but a services surplus. Trap: 'deficit narrowed' is true for the OVERALL (goods + services) figure, not a goods surplus — goods still show a large deficit.

💰 *RBI (Domestic Money Transfer) Directions, 2026: walk-in customers without a bank account can send up to ₹50,000 by NEFT at a branch*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: payment systems are regulated by RBI under the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007. NEFT is run by RBI; IMPS and UPI by NPCI. Trap: ₹50,000 is the branch NEFT limit — BC/ATM limit is only ₹5,000 per transaction.

🌿 *PIB factsheet: India's installed power capacity about 560 GW; non-fossil sources 55%; solar about 175 GW*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: India's Paris NDC target of 50% non-fossil capacity by 2030 has already been crossed. Trap: 55% is the share of installed CAPACITY, not of electricity actually generated.

🌏 *49th ASEAN Summit opens in Manila (theme 'Navigating Our Future, Together'); PM Modi in the Philippines 16–18 Nov for ASEAN-India and East Asia Summits*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: ASEAN was founded on 8 Aug 1967 by the Bangkok Declaration; Secretariat in Jakarta. India's 'Act East' policy (2014) centres on ASEAN. Trap: 49th = ASEAN's own summit; India's summit with ASEAN is the 23rd.

🏅 *Badminton: Lakshya Sen wins Kumamoto Masters Japan (BWF Super 500) — first Indian champion of the event*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: BWF World Tour levels (high → low): Finals, Super 1000, 750, 500, 300, 100. Trap: Kumamoto Masters Japan is a Super 500 — not the Japan Open (Super 750).

🏅 *Cricket: India beat Pakistan by one wicket off the last ball to win a record 9th Under-19 Asia Cup; football team enters SAFF final*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: SAFF = South Asian Football Federation; India is the most successful team. Trap: the U-19 final was in Oman, while the SAFF Championship is in Dhaka (Bangladesh).
