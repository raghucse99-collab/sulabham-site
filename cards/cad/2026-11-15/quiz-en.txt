🔑 *Answers — Daily Quiz* | 15 NOV 2026
1-(c) · 2-(d) · 3-(a) · 4-(d) · 5-(b) · 6-(c) · 7-(a) · 8-(d) · 9-(b) · 10-(c)

📖 Explanations:
1. (c) I and II are correct. III is the trap: a name is given only when it becomes a cyclonic storm (expected 16 Nov), not at the depression stage.
2. (d) Depression → cyclonic storm (62–88 km/h) → severe (89–117) → very severe (118–165). Trap: 'severe' comes BEFORE 'very severe'; and a depression is weaker than any cyclonic storm.
3. (a) I and II are correct. III is wrong: there was a harbour phase (expert exchanges, cross-deck visits, sports) followed by a sea phase.
4. (d) I and II are NOT correct: it was made by CNES, O.P. Jindal Global University (not NITI Aayog), and Goa is 1st (AP 2nd). III is correct (AP 0.69).
5. (b) Only I is correct (US$ 89.99 bn). II is wrong: services 40.51 < goods 49.47. III is wrong: goods imports (89.35) exceeded goods exports — a deficit of 39.88 bn; only the OVERALL deficit narrowed.
6. (c) I and III are correct. II is wrong: cash pay-out is limited to ₹10,000 per transaction and ₹25,000 a month per beneficiary — ₹50,000 is the branch NEFT limit.
7. (a) Both true and R explains A: 55% > 50%. Trap: the 55% is a share of installed CAPACITY, not of electricity generated — but the target is also about capacity, so R explains A.
8. (d) 49th ASEAN Summit, 23rd ASEAN-India Summit, 21st East Asia Summit. Trap: option (a) swaps ASEAN's own summit with India's summit with ASEAN.
9. (b) I and III are correct. II is the trap: he LOST the first game 13-21 and won the next two (21-18, 21-14).
10. (c) I and II are correct. III is wrong: IMNEX-26 was held at Visakhapatnam, HQ of the Eastern Naval Command (Kochi is Southern Naval Command).
