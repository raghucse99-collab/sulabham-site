Important for APPSC Group 1 & 2

🏛️ *AP gram panchayat polls: SEC issues Phase-2 notification; polling on 29 November*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: Panchayat elections are conducted by the State Election Commission (Art 243K), NOT by the Election Commission of India (Art 324). Trap: the Upa-Sarpanch is chosen on polling day after results, not on a separate later date.

🏛️ *AP forms State and district committees to trace missing children and stop human trafficking, after Supreme Court directions*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: Article 23 bans traffic in human beings and forced labour; Article 24 bans child labour in factories/mines. Trap: the district committee is chaired by the Collector, the SP is only co-chair.

🏛️ *AP project status report: 526 of 714 investment proposals (₹17.1 lakh crore) became projects; orders issued for 3 tech policies*
🎯 Exam point: AVGC-XR = Animation, Visual Effects, Gaming, Comics + Extended Reality. Trap: the 200-acre park is for AVGC (Amaravati); 5,000 acres is for the semiconductor park. 'Grounded' means work has started, not just signed.

🏛️ *Evening update: deep depression to become Cyclone Akvan; landfall likely between Nellore and Tuni around 20 Nov*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: November cyclones often hit the Andhra coast (IMD seasonal pattern); a name is given only at the cyclonic-storm stage (62–88 km/h). Arnab was only the 4th very severe cyclone to hit AP since 1970 (after 1999, Hudhud 2014, Titli 2018).

💰 *Wholesale inflation rises to 12.29% in October 2026; fuel & power inflation at 32.84%*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: WPI is released by the Office of the Economic Adviser (DPIIT); CPI by NSO (MoSPI). WPI has no services. Trap: manufactured-goods inflation FELL in October — only fuel & power and primary articles rose.

🌏 *Belarus Foreign Minister Maxim Ryzhenkov in India; 12th India–Belarus Intergovernmental Commission meets in New Delhi*
🎯 Exam point: Belarus became the 10th full member of the SCO in July 2024 (Astana summit) — so India and Belarus are both SCO members. Trap: the Indian co-chair of the Commission is the Commerce MoS, not the External Affairs Minister.

🌏 *7th India–Egypt Joint Trade Committee in Cairo: work to begin on a bilateral trade agreement; trade in local currencies*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: Egypt joined BRICS in January 2024; Egypt's President el-Sisi was the Republic Day 2023 chief guest; the Suez Canal links the Mediterranean and the Red Sea. Trap: ToR only starts the talks — no trade agreement has been signed yet.

🛡️ *'Sagar Suraksha 2026': national coastal security seminar in Kolkata (16–17 Nov)*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: the Indian Coast Guard works under the Ministry of Defence, but border management (including coastal security schemes) is under the Ministry of Home Affairs. Trap: the seminar was organised with MHA's Department of Border Management, not the Navy.

🔬 *SpaceX catches Starship's upper stage with tower arms for the first time — a step towards fully reusable rockets*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: ISRO's reusable effort is the RLV-TD; its landing experiments (RLV-LEX) used the winged vehicle 'Pushpak' at Chitradurga. Trap: this time the SHIP was caught and the BOOSTER was lost — not the other way round.

🏅 *ICC fixes windows: India to host 2029 Champions Trophy; India–Bangladesh to co-host 2031 ODI World Cup*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: India won the 2025 Champions Trophy (final in Dubai; Pakistan was the official host). Trap: the 2031 ODI World Cup co-host is Bangladesh, not Sri Lanka.
