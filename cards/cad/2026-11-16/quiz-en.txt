1-(c) · 2-(a) · 3-(d) · 4-(b) · 5-(d) · 6-(a) · 7-(c) · 8-(b) · 9-(c) · 10-(a)

📖 Explanations:
1. (c) I and II are correct. III is the trap: the Upa-Sarpanch is elected on polling day itself, after the results. Option (d) fails because of III; (a)/(b) leave out a correct statement.
2. (a) Nominations close 20 Nov → scrutiny 21 Nov → withdrawal till 24 Nov → re-poll 30 Nov (after polling on 29 Nov). Trap: scrutiny always comes before withdrawal.
3. (d) State: chair = Principal Secretary/Secretary, co-chair = DGP/ADGP (CID). District: chair = Collector, convener = DCPO (the SP is co-chair). (a) swaps the State posts; (b) swaps district chair and convener; (c) puts the Collector in the State committee.
4. (b) I is correct. III is correct: NREDCAP ₹8.5 lakh crore vs Industries ₹6.1 lakh crore. II is the trap: grounded projects are ₹7.3 lakh crore out of ₹22.6 lakh crore — about one-third, not more than half.
5. (d) (d) is incorrect: the AVGC park in Amaravati is 200 acres; 5,000 acres is for the semiconductor park. (a), (b) and (c) are correct facts.
6. (a) I and II are correct (IMD via AIR). III is the trap: reports put the likely landfall between Nellore and Tuni in Andhra Pradesh around 20 Nov, not near Chennai.
7. (c) I, II, IV are correct (fuel & power 32.84%). III is the trap: manufactured-products inflation eased to 9.54% from 9.76%. (a) leaves out IV; (b) and (d) include the wrong III.
8. (b) Belarus FM came to New Delhi for the IGC; Goyal went to Cairo for the Joint Trade Committee; Sagar Suraksha is in Kolkata; the Starship catch was at Starbase, Texas. (a) swaps Delhi and Cairo; (c) swaps Kolkata and Texas; (d) swaps Delhi and Kolkata.
9. (c) I is correct (Belarus became the 10th SCO member in July 2024). II is correct. III is the trap: the Indian co-chair was Jitin Prasada, MoS for Commerce and Industry.
10. (a) I and III are correct. II is the trap: the 2031 ODI World Cup co-host is Bangladesh, not Sri Lanka. (b) leaves out III; (c) and (d) include the wrong II.
