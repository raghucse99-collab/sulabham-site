📰 *SULABHAM · DAILY CURRENT AFFAIRS* | 17 NOV 2026
Important for APPSC Group 1 & 2

🏛️ *Cyclone Akvan (named by Iran) to cross AP coast between Nellore and Tuni on 20 Nov as a very severe cyclonic storm; AP moves to protect crops and fishermen*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: IMD (New Delhi) is the RSMC that names cyclones in the North Indian Ocean from a list given by 13 member countries; names are used in order and not repeated. Trap: 'Very Severe' is above 'Severe' — the scale is Depression → Deep Depression → Cyclonic Storm → Severe → Very Severe → Extremely Severe → Super Cyclone.

🏛️ *AP to build a 'Media City' in Amaravati under its AVGC-XR Policy 2026–31*
🎯 Exam point: Know the expansion of AVGC-XR (the 'C' is Comics, not Content/Cinema). Static link: SGST is the State's share of GST — so only the State can reimburse it. Trap: 25% (park) vs 50% (infrastructure) subsidy.

🌏 *23rd ASEAN-India Summit in Manila: joint statement on disaster management; PM proposes free e-visas for ASEAN tourists*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: ASEAN-India dialogue began in 1992 (hence 35 years in 2027); relations were raised to a Comprehensive Strategic Partnership in 2022. Trap: the ASEAN-India Summit (23rd) and East Asia Summit (21st) have different edition numbers.

🛡️ *Rajnath Singh and S. Jaishankar in Tokyo for the 4th India–Japan 2+2 Ministerial Dialogue*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: India and Japan are both Quad members (with the USA and Australia). Trap: a '2+2' is Foreign + Defence ministers — not the PM-level Annual Summit.

🌏 *UNGA: India votes against resolution on UN–OIC cooperation, objects to being excluded from consultations*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: OIC has 57 members, HQ in Jeddah (Saudi Arabia); India is not a member. Trap: a UNGA resolution is passed by a simple majority of members present and voting — abstentions are not counted as votes.

🔬 *India re-elected to the ITU Council for 2027–2030 at the Plenipotentiary Conference in Doha*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: ITU is the oldest UN specialised agency (1865) and allocates radio spectrum and satellite orbits. Trap: the ITU Council (48) is not the whole ITU membership, and the Plenipotentiary Conference — not the Council — is ITU's supreme body.

🩺 *ICMR-NIMR golden jubilee: President says malaria cases and deaths fell nearly 80% in 10 years; high-burden districts down from 155 to 33*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: malaria is caused by the Plasmodium parasite (a protozoan, not a virus or bacterium) and spread by the bite of an infected female Anopheles mosquito. Trap: Aedes spreads dengue/chikungunya, not malaria.

💰 *Horticulture output 2025-26 (3rd Advance Estimates): about 3,869 lakh tonnes, up 4.36%*
🎯 Exam point: Concept: output = area × yield; here area fell but output rose, so yield rose. Trap: fruits grew faster (4.60%) than vegetables (4.35%), but vegetables are the bigger share. Among vegetables listed, potato > onion > tomato.
