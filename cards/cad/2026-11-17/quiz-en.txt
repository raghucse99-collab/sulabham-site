🔑 *Answers — Daily Quiz* | 17 NOV 2026
1-(c) · 2-(a) · 3-(c) · 4-(a) · 5-(b) · 6-(d) · 7-(b) · 8-(b) · 9-(a) · 10-(d)

📖 Explanations:
1. (c) I and II are correct. III is the trap: Akvan comes after Arnab (late October) and Murasu (November). (d) fails because of III; (a)/(b) miss a correct statement.
2. (a) (a) Deep Depression → Cyclonic Storm → Severe CS → Very Severe CS. Akvan follows this ladder: CS on 16 Nov, Severe on 17 Nov, Very Severe by 19 Nov. Trap: 'Very Severe' is above 'Severe' (option d reverses them).
3. (c) (c) is NOT correct: the park gets 25% (up to ₹50 crore); 50% (up to ₹2 crore) is for AVGC-XR infrastructure. (a), (b), (d) are correct as per the policy.
4. (a) (a): SGST goes to the State, so the State alone can give it back as an incentive — R directly explains A. Trap: CGST belongs to the Centre, so a State cannot reimburse it.
5. (b) I, II and IV are correct. III is the trap: it was the 21st East Asia Summit; only the ASEAN-India Summit is the 23rd. Dialogue began in 1992, so 2027 = 35 years.
6. (d) I and III are correct. II is the trap: a 2+2 is Defence + External Affairs ministers (Rajnath Singh and S. Jaishankar), not the Prime Minister.
7. (b) II and III are correct. I is the trap: India voted AGAINST (one of the 6 'no' votes); 39 countries abstained.
8. (b) (b): Council = 48 members; HQ = Geneva; 2026 conference = Doha (Qatar); India's term = 2027–2030. Trap (a): Doha is only the conference venue, not the HQ.
9. (a) II, III, IV are correct. I is the trap: malaria is caused by the Plasmodium protozoan spread by the female Anopheles; Aedes spreads dengue.
10. (d) Only I is correct (area 301.36 → ~297.21 lakh ha, output up 4.36% — so yield rose). II is wrong: fruits 4.60% > vegetables 4.35%. III is wrong: potato 655.70 > onion 288.96 lakh tonnes.
