Important for APPSC Group 1 & 2

🏛️ *AP amends its Semiconductor & Display Fab Policy 4.0 to match ISM Semicon 2.0; plans a 5,000-acre Semiconductor Park at Tirupati*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: India Semiconductor Mission (ISM) works under MeitY. Know the terms: fab = makes chips on silicon wafers; ATMP/OSAT = assembly, testing and packaging of chips. Trap: the 80% is Centre + State together, not the State alone.

🏛️ *Cyclone Akvan becomes a Severe Cyclonic Storm; AIR (2:37 pm): likely to cross North AP–Yanam–South Odisha coast between Narsapur and Puri by Friday (20 Nov) noon*
🎯 Exam point: Concept: a forecast track changes as a cyclone moves — always quote the latest IMD bulletin. Colour codes: Green (no action) → Yellow (watch) → Orange (be prepared) → Red (take action). Trap: Narsapur is in West Godavari (AP), Puri is in Odisha — the stretch covers three areas: North AP, Yanam (Puducherry UT) and South Odisha.

📋 *PM Surya Ghar: Andhra Pradesh 4th in India with 7,39,260 rooftop solar systems*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana (launched Feb 2024, Ministry of New & Renewable Energy) — free electricity up to 300 units/month through rooftop solar. Trap: AP is 4th, not 1st; and ULA ≠ CAPEX.

🌏 *21st East Asia Summit, Manila: PM calls for a free, open, rules-based Indo-Pacific and proposes four initiatives*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: EAS began in 2005 (Kuala Lumpur); India is a founding member; 18 members = 10 ASEAN + India, China, Japan, South Korea, Australia, New Zealand, USA, Russia. Lothal (Gujarat) = Harappan dockyard; Nalanda (Bihar) = ancient university. Trap: EAS ≠ ASEAN-India Summit (23rd, held the same day).

⚖️ *'Samaveshi Niti Sankalp': two-day Social Justice Ministry summit with States and UTs begins in New Delhi*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: Article 41 (DPSP) — public assistance in disability; Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016 recognises 21 disabilities. 3 December = International Day of Persons with Disabilities. Trap: disability welfare is under this Ministry's Department of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities, not the Health Ministry.

🔬 *NASA's Voyager 1 becomes the first human-made object to reach one light-day (about 25.9 billion km) from Earth*
🎯 Exam point: Units: 1 AU = Earth–Sun distance (~15 crore km); light-year = distance light travels in a year (a unit of DISTANCE, not time). Heliopause = boundary where the Sun's solar wind ends. Trap: Voyager 1 visited only Jupiter and Saturn; Voyager 2 did the 'Grand Tour' (Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune).

🔬 *68th TOP500 list (SC26, Chicago): China's LineShine stays No. 1; India's best is Yotta's Shakti Cloud at No. 35*
🎯 Exam point: Basics: FLOPS = floating-point operations per second; peta = 10^15, exa = 10^18. India's own: PARAM (C-DAC, 1991), National Supercomputing Mission (2015, MeitY + DST). Trap: the No. 1 is not a US system this time.

🛡️ *India–Malaysia Army exercise 'Harimau Shakti 2026' runs 18–30 November*
🎯 Exam point: Match exercises with countries: Harimau Shakti – Malaysia; Mitra Shakti – Sri Lanka; Yudh Abhyas – USA; Maitree – Thailand; Garuda Shakti – Indonesia. Trap: Samudra Laksamana is the NAVAL exercise with Malaysia, Harimau Shakti is the ARMY one.

🗓️ *9th National Naturopathy Day (18 Nov): theme 'Prakritik Chikitsa, Swasth Bharat'; main event at CRIYN, Nagamangala*
🎯 Exam point: AYUSH = Ayurveda, Yoga & Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, Sowa-Rigpa, Homoeopathy. Ayush became a separate Ministry in Nov 2014. Trap: National Naturopathy Day (18 Nov, Gandhi link) ≠ Ayurveda Day (on Dhanteras) ≠ International Yoga Day (21 June).
