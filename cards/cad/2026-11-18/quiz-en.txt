🔑 *Answers — Daily Quiz* | 18 NOV 2026
1-(c) · 2-(a) · 3-(d) · 4-(b) · 5-(a) · 6-(c) · 7-(d) · 8-(b) · 9-(c) · 10-(b)

📖 Explanations:
1. (c) I and III are correct. II is the trap: 80% = 40% Centre + 40% AP; the State matches the Centre's share, it does not give 80% alone.
2. (a) AP 80% > Tamil Nadu 65% > UP 60% > Gujarat 56%. Trap: Gujarat, a chip-investment leader, actually offers the lowest share of the four.
3. (d) (d) is incorrect: 'Akvan' was suggested by Iran. (a), (b), (c) match the AIR report of 18 Nov. Remember: IMD New Delhi (RSMC) only applies names from the list given by 13 member countries.
4. (b) I and III are correct. II is wrong: Puri is in Odisha; Narsapur (West Godavari) is the AP end. Red = take action; Yellow = be watchful.
5. (a) I and III are correct. II is wrong: about 69% (5,09,004) came through the Utility-led Aggregation (ULA) model for SC/ST families; CAPEX is about 31%.
6. (c) Lothal (Harappan port-dockyard, Gujarat) suits maritime heritage; Nalanda (ancient university, Bihar) suits the university conclave; cyber crime goes to NFSU. Logic helps: link each place to its history.
7. (d) All three are correct. India joined at the first EAS (2005, Kuala Lumpur); the USA and Russia joined in 2011, making 18 members. Trap: many think EAS is only for East Asian countries.
8. (b) II and III are correct. I is the trap: a light-day (like a light-year) measures DISTANCE — how far light goes in 24 hours (~25.9 billion km).
9. (c) I and III are correct. II is wrong: India's best (Shakti Cloud, Yotta) is at rank 35. El Capitan (USA) is No. 2.
10. (b) Harimau Shakti = India–Malaysia Army (18–30 Nov 2026); Mitra Shakti = India–Sri Lanka Army; Samudra Laksamana = India–Malaysia Navy. Trap: both Malaysian exercises — tell Army from Navy.
