📰 *SULABHAM · DAILY CURRENT AFFAIRS* | 19 NOV 2026
Important for APPSC Group 1 & 2

🏛️ *Cyclone Akvan: IMD now expects landfall near Gopalpur (Odisha), between Narsapur and Puri, by noon/afternoon of 20 Nov*
🎯 Exam point: Concept: the forecast track shifted north (earlier Nellore–Visakhapatnam, now Narsapur–Puri) — always quote the latest IMD bulletin. Static link: RSMC New Delhi (IMD) names North Indian Ocean cyclones; Cyclone Phailin (2013) also crossed near Gopalpur. Trap: Gopalpur is in Odisha (Ganjam), not AP.

🏛️ *AP power demand up 18.23%: CM orders preparation for a 14,852 MW peak in the next six months*
🎯 Exam point: Concepts: MW measures peak load (capacity at one moment); units (kWh/MU) measure consumption. Pumped storage works like a giant battery — pump water up when power is surplus, generate at peak. El Niño = warming of the central/east Pacific, usually linked to a weaker Indian monsoon and hotter conditions.

🌏 *4th India–Japan 2+2 in Tokyo: push for UNICORN stealth-mast deal, maritime security and defence industry ties*
🎯 Exam point: 2+2 = foreign + defence ministers of both sides. Static link: India–Japan exercises — JIMEX (navy), Dharma Guardian (army), Veer Guardian (air force); both are in Quad and the Malabar naval exercise. Trap: 2027 (not 2026) is the 75th year of ties.

🌏 *India hosts the first SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum in New Delhi (20–22 Nov); PM to inaugurate*
🎯 Exam point: Static SCO: founded 2001; India and Pakistan became full members in 2017, Iran in 2023, Belarus in 2024. Trap: not every participant is a full member — Nepal, Armenia, Cambodia and Saudi Arabia are dialogue partners, not members.

🛡️ *MoD signs ₹1,976.40 crore contract with Israel Aerospace Industries to upgrade the Navy's Barak point-defence missile system*
🎯 Exam point: Concept: point defence protects the ship itself against close aerial threats; it is one layer of the Navy's layered air defence. Trap: this is an upgrade of the older Barak-1, not a purchase of Barak-8 (MR-SAM/LR-SAM, co-developed by DRDO and IAI).

🔬 *India's first indigenous H5N1 (clade 2.3.2.1a) bird-flu vaccine for chickens, made by ICAR-NIHSAD Bhopal, to be dedicated on 20 Nov*
🎯 Exam point: Concepts: H and N = haemagglutinin and neuraminidase (virus surface proteins); bird flu is zoonotic (animal → human). Inactivated = killed virus; live-attenuated = weakened live virus — a common trap. ICAR works under the Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare (DARE).

📋 *11 States/UTs now declared 'fully literate' under ULLAS; Madhya Pradesh has the most adult learners*
🎯 Exam point: Five ULLAS components: foundational literacy & numeracy, critical life skills, basic education, vocational skills, continuing education. Trap: Andhra Pradesh and Kerala are NOT in this ULLAS list of 11 (Kerala's 1991 'total literacy' declaration was under an earlier campaign).
