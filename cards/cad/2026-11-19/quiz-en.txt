1-(c) · 2-(a) · 3-(d) · 4-(b) · 5-(d) · 6-(a) · 7-(b) · 8-(b) · 9-(c) · 10-(d)

📖 Explanations:
1. (c) I and III are correct. II is the trap: Gopalpur is in Ganjam district of ODISHA; Narsapur (West Godavari) is the AP end of the stretch.
2. (a) Narsapur (~16.4°N, AP) → Kalingapatnam (~18.3°N, Srikakulam, AP) → Gopalpur (~19.3°N, Odisha) → Puri (~19.8°N, Odisha). Trap: Gopalpur is south of Puri.
3. (d) I, III, IV are correct. II is the trap: officials blamed El Niño (hotter, drier), not La Niña (which usually brings a stronger monsoon).
4. (b) Solar and wind vary with sun and wind; storing surplus power and releasing it at peak (R) is exactly why pumped storage helps such a grid (A). It works like a giant battery.
5. (d) I, II, III are correct. IV is the trap: the 75th year of diplomatic ties is 2027 (relations from 1952).
6. (a) JIMEX = Japan–India Maritime Exercise (navies); Dharma Guardian = armies; Veer Guardian = air forces; Malabar = India, USA, Japan, Australia navies. Trap: Dharma (army) vs Veer (air).
7. (b) I, II, IV are correct. III is wrong: participants such as Nepal, Armenia, Cambodia and Saudi Arabia are dialogue partners, not full members.
8. (b) I, III, IV are correct. II is the trap: the contract is for upgrade and obsolescence management of the existing Barak point-defence system, not a new Barak-8 purchase.
9. (c) I, III, IV are correct. II is the trap: it is an INACTIVATED (killed-virus) vaccine, not live-attenuated (weakened live virus).
10. (d) (d) is incorrect: AP is not in the list (Ladakh, Mizoram, Goa, Tripura, HP, Chandigarh, A&N, Sikkim, Uttarakhand, J&K, Lakshadweep). (a), (b), (c) are correct ULLAS features.
