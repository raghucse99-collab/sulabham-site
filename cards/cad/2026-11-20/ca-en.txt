📰 *SULABHAM · DAILY CURRENT AFFAIRS* | 20 NOV 2026
Important for APPSC Group 1 & 2

🏛️ *Cyclone Akvan crossed the North AP–South Odisha coast near Gopalpur on 20 Nov morning, after weakening to a Cyclonic Storm*
🎯 Exam point: IMD scale: Cyclonic Storm 62–88 km/h, Severe Cyclonic Storm 89–117 km/h — Akvan weakened one step before landfall. Trap: the landfall point Gopalpur is in Odisha, though the coast described is 'North AP–South Odisha'.

🏛️ *AP State Election Commission notifies the third phase of gram panchayat elections; polling on 2 December*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: 73rd Amendment (1992) → Part IX; Art. 243K = State Election Commission for panchayat polls (Art. 243ZA for municipalities). The SEC is appointed by the Governor but removable only like a High Court judge. Trap: ECI (Art. 324) does NOT conduct panchayat elections.

🏛️ *AP issues G.O. to explore Small Modular Reactor (SMR) nuclear power as an alternative to thermal power*
🎯 Exam point: Concept: an SMR is a small nuclear reactor (IAEA: up to about 300 MW(e) per unit) built in factory modules — it fits old thermal sites because grid lines and land already exist. Link: Union Budget 2025-26 Nuclear Energy Mission for SMRs. Trap: SMR is fission, not fusion.

⚖️ *Supreme Court: prior Gram Sabha consent is MANDATORY for land acquisition in Scheduled Areas; Odisha mining acquisition quashed*
🎯 Exam point: Link chain: Fifth Schedule (Scheduled Areas — AP has them in ASR, Parvathipuram Manyam etc.) → PESA Act 1996 (Gram Sabha consulted before acquisition) → RFCTLARR 2013 Sec. 41(3) consent → this judgment. Remember: 80% (private) / 70% (PPP) consent; Gram Sabha consent extra in Scheduled Areas.

🌏 *ICJ rules Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya — first time the World Court has held a State responsible for genocide*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: ICJ (UN organ, disputes between STATES) ≠ ICC (Rome Statute, tries INDIVIDUALS) — both sit in The Hague. Enforcement of ICJ judgments rests with the UN Security Council (Art. 94, UN Charter), which has never used it.

💰 *Electronics exports up 44% to about $32 bn in April–September 2026-27; non-smartphone items grow faster than phones*
🎯 Exam point: Concept: diversification — growth now comes from components (PCBs, memory, storage, network gear), not only phone assembly. Links: PLI for Large-Scale Electronics (2020), Electronics Component Manufacturing Scheme (ECMS, 2025), India Semiconductor Mission. Trap: a share can fall even when value rises.

🩺 *Health Ministry drafts rules to bring antimicrobials under central 'new drug' oversight and a separate licence for bulk-drug sale*
🎯 Exam point: Link: both rules are made under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940; Central Licensing Authority = DCGI (CDSCO). AMR links: Red Line campaign, National Action Plan on AMR. Trap: antimicrobials include antibiotics, antivirals, antifungals and antiparasitics — not antibiotics alone.

🌏 *18th India–France Joint Working Group on Counter-Terrorism meets in New Delhi; India briefs France on its 'PRAHAAR' strategy*
🎯 Exam point: Static: FATF secretariat is in Paris (OECD); India–France strategic partnership since 1998; 'No Money for Terror' ministerial conference — India hosted the 3rd edition in New Delhi (2022). Trap: PRAHAAR is India's national CT strategy, not a military exercise.

🗓️ *57th International Film Festival of India (IFFI) opens in Goa; theme 'Stories Without Borders'*
🎯 Exam point: Static: IFFI began in 1952 (Bombay); Goa is its permanent home since 2004; organised by the Ministry of I&B (through NFDC) with the Goa government. Trap: Golden Peacock = IFFI; Golden Lotus (Swarna Kamal) = National Film Awards.
