🔑 *Answers — Daily Quiz* | 20 NOV 2026
1-(b) · 2-(d) · 3-(a) · 4-(c) · 5-(c) · 6-(d) · 7-(b) · 8-(a) · 9-(d) · 10-(c)

📖 Explanations:
1. (b) II and III are correct. I is the trap: Akvan had weakened from a Severe Cyclonic Storm to a Cyclonic Storm BEFORE landfall; 70–80 km/h fits the Cyclonic Storm class (62–88).
2. (d) II (Art. 243K) and III are correct. I is wrong: panchayat polls are run by the State Election Commission (Art. 243K), not the ECI (Art. 324 — Parliament, State legislatures, President, Vice-President).
3. (a) I and III are correct. II is the trap — the Court said the opposite: a mining lease cannot override third-party landowners' rights or the 2013 Act's safeguards.
4. (c) 73rd Amendment 1992 → PESA 1996 (extends Part IX to Fifth Schedule areas) → RFCTLARR 2013 → judgment 2026. Trap: PESA came AFTER the 73rd Amendment because Art. 243M left Scheduled Areas out until Parliament extended it.
5. (c) I and III are correct. II is the trap: The Gambia (West Africa), with no direct link to the crimes, filed the case in 2019 as a party to the Genocide Convention; Bangladesh only hosts the refugees.
6. (d) (d) is incorrect — that describes the International Criminal Court (ICC, Rome Statute 1998, tries individuals). The ICJ was set up by the UN Charter (1945) and hears cases between States. Both sit in The Hague — the classic trap.
7. (b) I and III are correct (non-smartphone +56%, smartphones +35%). II is the trap: smartphone exports ROSE (about $13 bn → $18 bn); the share fell only because other items grew even faster.
8. (a) All three are correct — the draft does both: central 'new drug' permission for antimicrobials (2019 Rules) and a separate bulk-drug sale licence (1945 Rules). Trap: thinking licensing stays only with the States.
9. (d) A-2 (PRAHAAR = National Counter Terrorism Policy and Strategy, briefed to France), B-1, C-4 (Golden Peacock; not Swarna Kamal of National Film Awards), D-3 (1948 Genocide Convention).
10. (c) Both true and linked: the 20 Nov G.O. targets retiring thermal units; small, modular reactors can reuse the land and grid already there — that is why such sites suit them.
