📰 *SULABHAM · DAILY CURRENT AFFAIRS* | 21 NOV 2026
Important for APPSC Group 1 & 2

🏛️ *AP fixes maximum retail prices for rice and stock limits for mills, wholesalers and retailers to check the price rise*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: stock limits are imposed under the Essential Commodities Act, 1955 — a Central law; States act with the Centre's consent. Trap: the limits are for traders, not for farmers storing their own crop.

🏛️ *SVANidhi Samman Awards 2026: Andhra Pradesh and four AP cities among winners for helping street vendors*
🎯 Exam point: Ask: which ministry runs PM SVANidhi (MoHUA, not MSME) and what 'SVANidhi se Samriddhi' does (links vendors to welfare schemes). Trap: the RuPay credit-card limit is ₹30,000.

🩺 *India crosses 1 crore HPV vaccine doses in under nine months; Andhra Pradesh among six States at 100% of target*
🎯 Exam point: Ask: age group, number of doses and the platform (U-WIN — the same digital platform used for routine immunisation). Trap: Telangana is at about 95%, not among the six States at 100%.

💰 *Core sector grows 10.9% in October 2026, a 13-month high, led by iron ore, coal and electricity*
🎯 Exam point: Ask: how many industries and which has the highest weight (electricity in the new series; refinery products had the highest weight in the old 2011-12 series). Trap: it is still called 'Eight Core' in many books.

🌏 *Nepal PM Balendra Shah on a two-day state visit to India (22–23 Nov); 10-point 'Samriddha Nepal, Samunnat Bharat' vision expected*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: Pancheshwar is on the Mahakali (Sharda) river under the 1996 Mahakali Treaty; Janakpur is linked to Sita (Ramayana circuit). Trap: an 'ICP' is an Integrated Check Post (land port), not an airport.

🌏 *'Operation Monsoon Thunder': NCB and US DEA bust a network that sent prescription psychotropic pills from India to US buyers*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: NCB works under the Union Home Ministry and enforces the NDPS Act, 1985. Trap: the partner was the DEA, not the FBI.

💰 *PM dedicates 'Nitish Setu', the six-lane Kachchi Dargah–Bidupur bridge over the Ganga in Bihar, about 9.75 km long*
🎯 Exam point: Compare: Mahatma Gandhi Setu (Patna–Hajipur) is the older Ganga bridge; Bhupen Hazarika (Dhola–Sadiya) bridge is on the Lohit. Trap: Raghopur is a river island (diara) on the route, not the end point.

🗓️ *IIT Kharagpur, India's first IIT (1951), celebrates its Platinum Jubilee — 75 years; PM attends*
🎯 Exam point: Ask: India's first IIT and its year (Kharagpur, 1951). Static: it began at the old Hijli Detention Camp and the Sarkar Committee recommended IITs. Trap: IIT Bombay (1958) and IIT Madras (1959) came later.
