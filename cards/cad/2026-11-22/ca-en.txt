📰 *SULABHAM · DAILY CURRENT AFFAIRS* | 22 NOV 2026
Important for APPSC Group 1 & 2

🏛️ *NIELIT opens a temporary Quantum & AI university campus at Acharya Nagarjuna University; permanent campus to come up in Amaravati*
🎯 Exam point: Link: NIELIT is under MeitY (not the Education Ministry). Trap: the temporary campus is at ANU, Guntur; the permanent one at Nidamarru, Amaravati.

🏛️ *Reliance commissions 'Asia's largest' beverage plant at Orvakal, Kurnool — within 12 months of the foundation stone*
🎯 Exam point: AP economy link: Orvakal (Kurnool) is a Rayalaseema industrial node; SIPB is chaired by the Chief Minister. Trap: the plant is in Kurnool district, not Nandyal or Anantapur.

🌏 *Nepal PM Balendra Shah arrives in Delhi; Janakpur Dham–Ayodhya weekly train to be flagged off; Nepal seeks $2 billion credit and grants*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: India–Nepal Treaty of Peace and Friendship (1950); Janakpur–Ayodhya is a Ramayana-circuit link. Trap: Janakpur is in Nepal (birthplace of Sita), not in Bihar.

🌏 *Jaishankar holds talks with Bulgaria's Foreign Minister; says the India–EU FTA will enter into force soon*
🎯 Exam point: IR link: Bulgaria is an EU member, so the India–EU FTA covers it. Trap: an FTA is negotiated with the EU as a bloc, not with each member country.

🌏 *Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla opens the BRICS Women's Leadership Summit at Banasthali Vidyapith, Rajasthan (till 23 Nov)*
🎯 Exam point: Polity link: the Constitution (106th Amendment) Act, 2023 (Nari Shakti Vandan) reserves one-third of Lok Sabha and Assembly seats for women. Trap: BRICS (bloc of countries) ≠ BRIC (India's biotech council, next item).

🌿 *Botswana-born cheetah KOMG 3 gives birth to four cubs in the wild at Kuno; India's cheetah count rises to 62*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: the cheetah was declared extinct in India in 1952 — Project Cheetah is the world's first intercontinental large-carnivore translocation. Trap: Botswana is the third source country, after Namibia and South Africa.

🔬 *BRIC aims to grow India's bioeconomy to $300 billion by 2030 and $1 trillion by 2047 — Jitendra Singh*
🎯 Exam point: Numbers trap: $300 billion is the 2030 target and $1 trillion the 2047 target — examiners swap them. Link: BioE3 policy (Biotechnology for Economy, Environment, Employment).

📋 *GeM signs MoU with the Tribal Indian Chamber of Commerce and Industry to bring tribal entrepreneurs into government buying*
🎯 Exam point: Link: Public Procurement Policy for MSEs reserves 4% of annual procurement for SC/ST-owned enterprises (within the 25% MSE target). Trap: GeM is under Commerce, not Finance.

💰 *SEBI proposes to drop the extra disclosure rules for offshore funds introduced in 2023*
🎯 Exam point: Concept: 'ultimate beneficial owner' = the natural person who finally owns or controls the money. Trap: this is only a proposal for public comments, not a new rule.
