🔑 *Answers — Daily Quiz* | 22 NOV 2026
1-(c) · 2-(a) · 3-(d) · 4-(a) · 5-(b) · 6-(d) · 7-(b) · 8-(c) · 9-(b) · 10-(c)

📖 Explanations:
1. (c) II and III are correct. I is the trap: NIELIT is an autonomous body under MeitY (Electronics & IT), with Deemed University status — not under the Education Ministry.
2. (a) ₹47 crore is for the four first-phase labs; ₹730.70 crore is the permanent campus; 8.5 acres at Nidamarru; about 8,250 learners (1,650 degree + 6,600 skill). Trap: swapping ₹47 cr and ₹730.70 cr.
3. (d) (d) is incorrect: the plant gives about 1,200 direct jobs (not 12,000). The other three are correct — Orvakal (Kurnool), 10 lines, foundation in Nov 2025, production in about 12 months.
4. (a) I and II are correct. III is the trap: Nepal said it can allow civilian flights, not military ones; the new air route is not yet agreed.
5. (b) I and III are correct. II is wrong: trade deals are with the EU as a bloc — Jaishankar said the India–EU FTA will enter into force soon; no agreement was signed with Bulgaria at these talks.
6. (d) Namibia (8, Sept 2022) → South Africa (12, Feb 2023) → Botswana (9, Feb 2026). KOMG 3, whose four cubs took the count to 62, came from Botswana.
7. (b) I and II are correct: 62 in all, 43 born in India (more than half). III is wrong: 3 live in Gandhi Sagar Wildlife Sanctuary, the rest in Kuno — both in Madhya Pradesh.
8. (c) A is true ($300 bn by 2030, $1 trillion by 2047). R is false: BRIC = Biotechnology Research and Innovation Council of India's Department of Biotechnology — not linked to BRICS.
9. (b) I and II are correct. III is the trap: GeM is under the Ministry of Commerce & Industry, not Finance.
10. (c) I and III are correct. II is wrong: it is only a consultation paper for public comments; SEBI regulations do not need Parliament's approval to be proposed.
