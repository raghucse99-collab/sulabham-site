Important for APPSC Group 1 & 2

🏛️ *AP: first council meetings in 13 corporations and 87 municipalities; Deputy Mayors and Vice-Chairpersons elected indirectly*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: the Constitution (Part IXA) leaves the method of electing the Mayor/Chairperson to the State law — so states differ. Trap: in AP now the Mayor is direct, but the Deputy Mayor is still indirect.

🏛️ *AP panchayat polls, phase 1: 758 sarpanches unopposed; voting for 3,191 sarpanch posts on 26 November*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: panchayat elections are run by the State Election Commission (Art 243K, 73rd Amendment), NOT by the Election Commission of India (Art 324). Panchayats = Part IX; Municipalities = Part IXA.

🌏 *India–Nepal: 8 MoUs, six projects and an economic package of about $1.62 billion as PM Balendra Shah meets PM Modi*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: Motihari–Amlekhgunj was South Asia's first cross-border petroleum pipeline; Nepal borders 5 Indian states (Uttarakhand, UP, Bihar, West Bengal, Sikkim). Trap: Dharchula (India, Uttarakhand) vs Darchula (Nepal).

💰 *MoSPI–RBI data users conference in Mumbai explains the new GDP, CPI and IIP series*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: CPI's new base is a calendar year (2024=100), but GDP and IIP use the financial year 2022-23. Link: RBI's inflation target is set on CPI (headline) — 4% ±2%.

📋 *Labour Ministry launches NCS ONE job portal, Occupational Shortage Index and e-Shram livelihood module*
🎯 Exam point: Link: e-Shram is the national database of unorganised workers (Labour Ministry). The four Labour Codes: Wages; Industrial Relations; Social Security; Occupational Safety, Health & Working Conditions. Trap: 'five protections', not three.

🛡️ *Amit Shah opens the earthquake-resistant campus of NDRF's 12th Battalion at Hollongi, Arunachal Pradesh*
🎯 Exam point: Static: NDRF was formed under the DM Act, 2005 and works under the Home Ministry; NDMA is chaired by the Prime Minister, SDMA by the Chief Minister. Trap: NDRF is not under the Defence Ministry.

🌿 *COP31 climate talks in Antalya end without a deal; fossil-fuel language blocks the main decision*
🎯 Exam point: Static: COP = Conference of Parties to the UNFCCC (1992 Rio Earth Summit); decisions need consensus. Trap: the 'transition away from fossil fuels' pledge came at COP28 Dubai, not COP31.

🏅 *Anupama Ramachandran retains the IBSF World Women's Snooker title at Indore — back-to-back world crowns*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: men's world champion here is Iran's Ali Gharahgojlou — Pankaj Advani (India's most decorated cueist) took bronze. The event also marked the centenary of the Billiards and Snooker Federation of India.
