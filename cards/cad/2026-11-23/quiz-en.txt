1-(c) · 2-(a) · 3-(d) · 4-(b) · 5-(a) · 6-(c) · 7-(b) · 8-(d) · 9-(a) · 10-(c)

📖 Explanations:
1. (c) I and III are correct. II is the trap: the number of Deputy Mayors (and Vice-Chairpersons) was REDUCED from two to one per body. Options (b) and (d) fail because they include II; (a) misses III.
2. (a) Part IX = Panchayats (73rd Amendment); Part IXA = Municipalities (74th). Art 243K = State Election Commission, which is running AP's panchayat polls; Art 243-I = State Finance Commission. Traps: swapping IX/IXA (b, d) or 243K/243-I (c, d).
3. (d) I and II are correct (3,949 − 758 = 3,191 posts go to poll on 26 Nov). III is wrong: phase 1 covers 26 districts — Visakhapatnam and Guntur have no panchayats in this phase. So (b) and (c) fail; (a) misses II.
4. (b) Lower Arun = 669-MW hydro project (foundation stone); Motihari–Amlekhgunj = petroleum pipeline (phase II dedicated); Dharchula–Darchula = new motorable bridge; Banbasa = new Integrated Check Post. (c) swaps the bridge and ICP; (a) and (d) misplace the hydro project.
5. (a) All three are correct as reported on 23 Nov. The trap is to doubt II — the South Asia Satellite (GSAT-9, launched 2017) is India's gift satellite for neighbours, and the new MoU uses it for Nepal's communication network.
6. (c) II is wrong: the IIP base moved FROM 2011-12 TO 2022-23 (same as GDP). I, III, IV are correct. (b) and (d) include the old base; (a) leaves out the true statement III.
7. (b) (b) is NOT correct: the module tracks FIVE protections — food, health, life insurance, accident/disability and pension. (a), (c) and (d) are correct (Labour Codes in force from 21 Nov 2025).
8. (d) IV is the trap: NDRF is under the Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA), not Defence. I, II, III are correct. (b) and (c) contain IV; (a) leaves out III, which is also true.
9. (a) Both true and linked: the COP31 President said there was no agreement on the text, so the decisions were not adopted — the EU and vulnerable nations wanted stronger fossil-fuel language, exporters resisted. Without consensus nothing passes. Trap (c): thinking a majority vote could have passed it.
10. (c) I and II are correct. III is the trap: the men's title went to Ali Gharahgojlou of Iran (5-1 over Tam Yun Fung); Advani won bronze. So (b) and (d) fail; (a) misses II.
