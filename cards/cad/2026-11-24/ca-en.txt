📰 *SULABHAM · DAILY CURRENT AFFAIRS* | 24 NOV 2026
Important for APPSC Group 1 & 2

🏛️ *ASEAN–India Maritime Conclave 2026 in Visakhapatnam: AP pitches its ports, shipbuilding and green-energy plans*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: ASEAN was formed in 1967 (Bangkok Declaration), Secretariat in Jakarta; India's 'Act East' policy (2014). Visakhapatnam = HQ of the Eastern Naval Command. Trap: know the north-to-south order of the ports — Mulapeta (Srikakulam) → Kakinada → Machilipatnam → Ramayapatnam → Dugarajapatnam.

🏛️ *Indo-French (AFD) mission studies Visakhapatnam Fishing Harbour for an 'eco-fishing port' makeover*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: CMFRI (Central Marine Fisheries Research Institute) and MPEDA (Marine Products Export Development Authority) are both in Kochi; Fishery Survey of India (FSI) HQ is Mumbai. Trap: do not write that a French-funded project was 'sanctioned' — only a study mission.

🏛️ *TTD and LIC launch 'Srivari Yatra Suraksha' life cover for Tirumala pilgrims; first ₹2 lakh payouts to 7 families*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: LIC was set up in 1956 (LIC Act) by nationalising life insurers; HQ Mumbai. Trap: ₹2 lakh is for NATURAL death and ₹10 lakh for ACCIDENTAL death — examiners swap them.

🏛️ *Buddhist tourism circuit to link Andhra Pradesh with Thailand: Lokesh's proposals to the Thai Ambassador*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: Acharya Nagarjuna — founder of the Madhyamika (Shunyavada) school of Mahayana Buddhism, linked with Nagarjunakonda (Palnadu) and the Amaravati stupa of the Satavahana period. Trap: Bodh Gaya (enlightenment) is in Bihar; Sarnath (first sermon) is in UP.

⚖️ *West Bengal Cabinet approves renaming the State 'Paschim Banga'; resolution in the Assembly on 9 December*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: under Art 3 the Bill needs the President's prior recommendation; the President refers it to the State Legislature, whose views are NOT binding; Parliament passes it by simple majority and, by Art 4, it is not an Art 368 amendment. Trap: the State cannot rename itself.

⚖️ *Supreme Court seeks responses on a PIL to reform the anti-defection law and stop 'strategic resignations'*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: the Speaker/Chairman decides disqualification under the Tenth Schedule (52nd Amendment, 1985); the 'merger' exception needs two-thirds of the legislature party. Trap: the 'split' (one-third) exception was deleted by the 91st Amendment (2003), not by a court.

📋 *Social Justice Ministry to launch SAMAVESH single-window portal and new e-Anudaan grant portal on 25 November*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: SMILE = livelihood support for transgender persons and people in begging; AVYAY = Atal Vayo Abhyuday Yojana for senior citizens; SHRESHTA = residential schooling for SC students; NAPDDR = drug demand reduction. Trap: e-Anudaan is for grants to NGOs/institutions, not direct cash to beneficiaries.

🔬 *India's space economy to grow from about $9 billion to $40–45 billion: Jitendra Singh at the India International Space Conclave*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: IN-SPACe (2020) authorises and promotes private space activity; NSIL (2019) is the commercial arm of ISRO under the Department of Space; Indian Space Policy 2023. Trap: ISpA is an industry body, not a government agency.

🗓️ *National Milk Day (26 Nov) to be held in Patna: Basic Animal Husbandry Statistics 2026 and a climate-resilient dairy roadmap to be released*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: Operation Flood began in 1970 under NDDB (set up 1965, Anand, Gujarat); India is the world's largest milk producer. Trap: World Milk Day is 1 June (FAO); National Milk Day is 26 Nov. Also 26 Nov = Constitution Day.

🌏 *PM Modi and German Chancellor Merz review the Strategic Partnership in a phone call, in the 75th year of India–Germany ties*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: Germany's capital is Berlin; India and Germany are G4 partners seeking permanent UNSC seats (with Brazil, Japan). Trap: the head of government in Germany is the Chancellor; the President is head of state.
