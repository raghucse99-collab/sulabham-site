🔑 *Answers — Daily Quiz* | 24 NOV 2026
1-(b) · 2-(d) · 3-(a) · 4-(c) · 5-(d) · 6-(a) · 7-(c) · 8-(b) · 9-(d) · 10-(a)

📖 Explanations:
1. (b) I and II are correct. III is the trap: the shipbuilding cluster (about ₹30,000 crore) is proposed at DUGARAJAPATNAM, not Krishnapatnam. (a) misses II; (c) and (d) include the wrong III.
2. (d) AFD = Agence Française de Développement; CMFRI = research institute in Kochi; FSI (Fishery Survey of India) = Mumbai; MPEDA = export development authority, also Kochi. Trap (a): both CMFRI and MPEDA are in Kochi — separate them by function (research vs export).
3. (a) I and II are correct. III is wrong: the 7 families were 4 from Tamil Nadu, 2 from Telangana and only 1 from AP — the scheme covers all pilgrims. (b) misses I; (c) and (d) include III.
4. (c) A is true (Lokesh's proposal to the Thai Ambassador, 23 Nov). R is false: Nagarjuna founded the MADHYAMIKA (Shunyavada) school of MAHAYANA Buddhism. Theravada is the older school followed in Thailand and Sri Lanka — that is the trap link.
5. (d) All three are correct. Art 3: Parliament alters names by law, Bill needs the President's recommendation, and the State Legislature's views are not binding. Art 4: such laws are not Art 368 amendments, so a simple majority is enough. Trap: thinking West Bengal's 9 Dec resolution itself changes the name.
6. (a) 52nd Amendment 1985 (Tenth Schedule) → Kihoto Hollohan 1992 (Speaker's decision open to judicial review) → 91st Amendment 2003 (split clause deleted) → PIL notice 23 Nov 2026. Trap (c): the 91st Amendment came AFTER Kihoto.
7. (c) AVYAY = Atal Vayo Abhyuday Yojana (seniors); SMILE = transgender persons and people in begging; SHRESHTA = residential education for SC students; NAPDDR = National Action Plan for Drug Demand Reduction. Trap (a): swapping AVYAY and SMILE.
8. (b) I and III are correct. II is the trap: ISpA is an industry association of private space companies that organised the conclave, not a government/statutory body (the government regulator-promoter is IN-SPACe).
9. (d) (d) is NOT correct: 1 June is WORLD Milk Day (FAO). National Milk Day is 26 Nov, Kurien's birth anniversary; the 2026 event is at Bapu Sabhagar, Patna, where BAHS 2026 will be released. (a)–(c) are correct.
10. (a) I and II are correct. III is wrong: Merz is the Federal CHANCELLOR (head of government); Germany's President is the head of state. (b) misses II; (c) and (d) include III.
