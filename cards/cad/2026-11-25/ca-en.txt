📰 *SULABHAM · DAILY CURRENT AFFAIRS* | 25 NOV 2026
Important for APPSC Group 1 & 2

🏛️ *AP's 24th SIPB clears 17 projects worth ₹88,625 crore with 54,419 jobs; Kia expands with ₹19,000 crore*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: Kia's first Indian plant is at Penukonda (old Anantapur, now Sri Sathya Sai district). Trap: SIPB is chaired by the Chief Minister, not the Industries Minister; don't mix SIPB (State) with the Centre's DPIIT.

🏛️ *AP: polling in 23 urban local bodies and phase-1 gram panchayats on 26 Nov; Cabinet meeting moved to 27 Nov*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: the SEC conducts panchayat polls under Article 243K and municipal polls under Article 243ZA (73rd & 74th Amendments, 1992). Trap: the Election Commission of India does NOT conduct local body polls.

🏛️ *Illegal sand mining in the Pennar at Proddatur damages a drinking-water pipeline; CM orders strict action*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: the Penna rises in the Nandi Hills (Chikkaballapur, Karnataka), flows through Rayalaseema and enters the Bay of Bengal in Nellore district. Sand is a 'minor mineral' — States make the rules for it under the MMDR Act, 1957.

💰 *Cabinet approves ₹11,000 crore scheme to build construction and infrastructure equipment in India*
🎯 Exam point: Concept: 'sales-linked' incentive is paid only on EXTRA sales over a base year — same idea as the PLI schemes. Trap: the incentive rate FALLS (13% → 8%), it does not rise; and the testing grant is VGF, not a loan.

📋 *Cabinet continues two India Post schemes for 2026-31: Postal Operations (₹1,141 cr) and Estates Management (₹1,491 cr)*
🎯 Exam point: Concept: a Central SECTOR scheme is fully funded and run by the Centre; a Centrally SPONSORED scheme is shared with States. Trap: the bigger outlay is Estates Management (₹1,491 cr), not Postal Operations. Department of Posts is under the Ministry of Communications.

⚖️ *Supreme Court reserves verdict on commuting a death sentence because a mercy petition has been pending since 2012*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: only the President (Art 72) can PARDON a death sentence; the Governor (Art 161) can commute or remit it but cannot pardon it. The President acts on the advice of the Union Council of Ministers.

📊 *Asia Power Index 2026: India stays 3rd with its best-ever score of 40.5 and remains a 'major power'*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: Japan (38.6) narrowly missed 'major power'; India is 3rd, not 2nd. Remember the publisher — Lowy Institute is Australian (not Japanese or American).

🌏 *India–EU free trade agreement to be signed in December, may come into force by mid-2027: EU Ambassador*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: India–EU FTA talks (formally the Broad-based Trade and Investment Agreement) began in 2007, stalled in 2013 and were relaunched in 2022. The EU has 27 member countries. Trap: signing ≠ entry into force — the European Parliament must still approve.

🔬 *China's Shenzhou-24 docks with Tiangong carrying Pakistan's first astronaut — the station's first foreign visitor*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: Tiangong is China's own station — not part of the ISS (USA, Russia, Europe, Japan, Canada). India–China–Pakistan space links are a frequent IR + S&T combination question.
