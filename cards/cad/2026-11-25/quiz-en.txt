📖 Explanations:
1. (a) (a). Kia's expansion is in Sri Sathya Sai district (its plant is at Penukonda); Anakapalli gets the data centre, tyre and Saint-Gobain projects. Trap: III mixes up two districts.
2. (d) (d). Polling 26 Nov, counting 30 Nov, panchayat phases 26 Nov/29 Nov/2 Dec, Cabinet moved to 27 Nov. Trap (c): 27 Nov is also the re-poll day, but counting is on 30 Nov.
3. (c) (c). SEC (Art 243ZA, 74th Amendment) conducts the whole process including counting; ECI has no role. AP's indirect elections of heads are on 5–6 Dec. Trap: II — ECI conducts only Parliament, State legislature, President and Vice-President polls.
4. (c) (c). The Penna is an east-flowing river: Nandi Hills → Rayalaseema → Bay of Bengal in Nellore district. Trap: II — 'Pennar' sounds like the west-flowing Periyar of Kerala.
5. (b) (b) is wrong: the sales-linked incentive is graded DOWN from 13% to 8%, so firms are pushed to become competitive early. (a), (c), (d) are correct.
6. (a) (a). The ₹280 crore new-construction part builds 190 departmental buildings on vacant plots precisely to replace rented spaces. Trap: don't confuse it with the ₹1,141 crore Postal Operations scheme (mail, parcels, philately).
7. (c) (c). Only the President can pardon a death sentence; the Governor can commute/remit but not pardon it. III is the Shatrughan Chauhan (2014) principle, now argued in the Rajoana case. Trap: I.
8. (b) (b). Japan scored 38.6, below India's 40.5, and narrowly missed the 'major power' mark of 40. India's best measure is diplomatic influence (70.3), weakest defence networks (18.6). Trap: III.
9. (d) (d). 2007 begin → 2013 stall → 2022 relaunch → Dec 2026 signing (expected); entry into force may follow by mid-2027 after the European Parliament's scrutiny. Trap (a): relaunch cannot come before the stall.
10. (c) (c). Shenzhou-24 went to China's own Tiangong station, not the ISS; China is not an ISS partner. Pakistan's SUPARCO and China's CMSA signed the deal in Feb 2025. Trap: II.
