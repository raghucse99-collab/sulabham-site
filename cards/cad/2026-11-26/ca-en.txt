📰 *SULABHAM · DAILY CURRENT AFFAIRS* | 26 NOV 2026
Important for APPSC Group 1 & 2

🏛️ *AP votes in phase-1 gram panchayat polls and 23 urban local bodies on 26 Nov*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: Art. 243K (SEC for panchayats) and Art. 243ZA (SEC for municipalities) — added by the 73rd and 74th Amendments (1992). Trap: ECI (Art. 324) does NOT conduct local body polls.

🏛️ *AP High Court bars recording, screenshots and live streaming of hearings without its permission*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: High Courts are 'courts of record' under Art. 215 (Supreme Court: Art. 129) and can punish contempt. AP High Court started at Amaravati on 1 Jan 2019. Trap: this is not a ban on reporting.

💰 *Six brownfield urea plants worth about ₹54,000 crore approved under NIPU-2026*
🎯 Exam point: Concept: 'brownfield' = new unit at an existing site; 'greenfield' = on fresh land. Trap: Uttar Pradesh gets two plants; none of the six is in Andhra Pradesh.

💰 *Cabinet clears ₹4,288 crore second container terminal at Kamarajar Port (Ennore) on PPP mode*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: Kamarajar (Ennore) is India's first corporatised major port and is owned by Chennai Port. It lies near Pulicat Lake on the AP–TN border. Trap: Major Ports are under the Union; minor ports (like AP's) are under states.

💰 *Cabinet approves ₹2,014 crore greenfield airport at Doloo near Silchar, Assam*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: Barak river valley of south Assam; Silchar is its main town. Trap: 'greenfield' = new site (Doloo), unlike the 'brownfield' urea plants in today's news.

💰 *RBI forex reserves report: reserves rise to $737.78 billion by end-September 2026*
🎯 Exam point: Static: Forex reserves have 4 parts — FCA, Gold, SDRs and Reserve Tranche Position in the IMF. RBI manages them under the RBI Act, 1934. Trap: SDR is an IMF reserve asset, not a currency.

📋 *MSME registrations on Udyam portals cross 10 crore*
🎯 Exam point: Order of MSME registration systems: EM-II (2006) → Udyog Aadhaar Memorandum (2015) → Udyam (2020) → Udyam Assist (2023). Trap: MSMED Act is of 2006.

🛡️ *India–Nepal Army exercise SURYAKIRAN XX begins at Saljhandi, Nepal (25 Nov–8 Dec)*
🎯 Exam point: Match-the-pair favourite: Suryakiran = India–Nepal; Hand-in-Hand = India–China; Mitra Shakti = India–Sri Lanka; Sampriti = India–Bangladesh. Trap: don't confuse with the IAF 'Surya Kiran' aerobatic team.

🗓️ *Kashi Tamil Sangamam 5.0 from 28 Nov to 12 Dec, on the theme of Thirukkural*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: Thirukkural was written by Thiruvalluvar — 1,330 couplets in three parts (virtue, wealth, love). Trap: Kashi = Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh.
