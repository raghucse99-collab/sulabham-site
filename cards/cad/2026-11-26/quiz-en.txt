📖 Explanations:
1. (b) (b). The SEC conducts local polls (Art. 243K/243ZA); the 4 corporations were Srikakulam, Kakinada, Rajamahendravaram, Mangalagiri-Tadepalli. Trap: only village counting was same-day; ULB results are on 30 Nov.
2. (a) (a). 324 = ECI; 243K = SEC (panchayats, Part IX); 243ZA = SEC (municipalities, Part IXA); 243E = 5-year term of panchayats. Trap: option (b) swaps 243K and 243ZA.
3. (c) (c). The order is not a ban on reporting — media may report hearings, observations and judgments; only unauthorised recordings/clips are barred. (a), (b), (d) are correct.
4. (a) (a). Art. 215 makes HCs courts of record with contempt powers (SC: Art. 129). Trap: the separate AP High Court started at Amaravati on 1 Jan 2019, not in 2014 (the year of bifurcation).
5. (c) (c). Six brownfield plants of 12.7 lakh t each; UP gets Shahjahanpur and Jagdishpur. Trap: the southern plant is at Chennai (Tamil Nadu), not in AP.
6. (d) (d). DBFOT, 40 years, 2.0 million TEU are correct. Trap: Kamarajar (Ennore) is a Major Port under the Union, owned by Chennai Port — not a state minor port.
7. (a) (a). Doloo (Cachar) airport and the Kamarajar terminal are correctly matched. Trap: the airport will be built by the Airports Authority of India; it is the port terminal that is on PPP.
8. (d) (d). Reserves: $691.11 bn → $737.78 bn; 140.39 t of 880.52 t gold is with BoE/BIS. Trap: gold's share FELL (16.70% → about 14.38%) as gold prices fell ~9%.
9. (c) (c). EM-II (2006) → Udyog Aadhaar (2015) → Udyam portal (1 Jul 2020) → Udyam Assist (11 Jan 2023). Trap: (b) puts Udyam Assist before the Udyam portal.
10. (b) (b). Suryakiran (XX began 25 Nov at Saljhandi) is with Nepal; Hand-in-Hand is with China. Trap: Mitra Shakti is with Sri Lanka — India–Bangladesh is Sampriti.
