📰 *SULABHAM · DAILY CURRENT AFFAIRS* | 27 NOV 2026
Important for APPSC Group 1 & 2

🏛️ *AP Cabinet clears Kia Plant-2 at Penukonda, MSME Right to Business Bill and Amaravati works*
🎯 Exam point: Match the project with its place (Kia–Penukonda, titanium–Srikakulam, float glass–Achyutapuram). Trap: Kia's FIRST AP plant is also at Penukonda — Plant-2 is an expansion, not a new location.

🏛️ *Rushikonda buildings in Visakhapatnam to be used for tourism under PPP*
🎯 Exam point: Concept link: PPP models (BOT, O&M, lease) and CRZ rules under the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986. Trap: in an O&M lease the government keeps ownership — the asset is not sold.

🏛️ *Polavaram: PPA CEO checks quality of the earth-cum-rock-fill (ECRF) dam*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: Polavaram (Indira Sagar) on the Godavari was declared a national project under Section 90 of the AP Reorganisation Act, 2014. Trap: the PPA supervises — the State builds the project.

⚖️ *Union Cabinet clears fresh women's reservation–delimitation bill; Parliament to sit from 30 Nov*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: Art. 81 (Lok Sabha size), Art. 82 (readjustment after census), Art. 368 (special majority: two-thirds of members present and voting + majority of total membership). Trap: adjourned sine die ≠ prorogued.

🌏 *Colombo Security Conclave adopts 2026–27 roadmap at 8th NSA meeting in the Maldives*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: CSC is an Indian Ocean security grouping, separate from SAARC, BIMSTEC and IORA. Trap: Australia and Malaysia were only guests, not members.

🛡️ *GRSE delivers Vindhyagiri, the 7th and last Project 17A stealth frigate, to the Navy*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: P17A ships were built at two yards — Mazagon Dock (Mumbai) built four, GRSE (Kolkata) three. Trap: 'Vindhyagiri' here is a hill range in Karnataka, not the Vindhya Range of central India.

💰 *Record foodgrain output of 378.5 million tonnes in 2025-26; kharif 2026-27 first estimate 167.1 MT*
🎯 Exam point: Concept: estimates are issued by the Agriculture Ministry (DES) in stages — 1st/2nd/3rd advance estimates, then final. Trap: India still imports pulses and edible oils — hence the National Pulses and Oilseeds Missions.

🏆 *India Pavilion wins the Visitors' Lion for Best National Participation at the 61st Venice Biennale*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: the Visitors' Lion is chosen by public vote, unlike the jury's Golden Lion. Venice is in Italy; the Biennale is held every two years ('biennale').

🗓️ *27 Nov: birth anniversary of G.V. Mavalankar, first Speaker of the Lok Sabha*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: the Speaker is elected under Art. 93. Trap: the first Deputy Speaker M.A. Ayyangar became the 2nd Speaker; Neelam Sanjiva Reddy of AP was the 4th Speaker.
