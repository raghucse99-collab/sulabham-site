📰 *SULABHAM · DAILY CURRENT AFFAIRS* | 28 NOV 2026
Important for APPSC Group 1 & 2

🏛️ *AP gram panchayat polls: phase 2 on 29 Nov — 1,171 sarpanches already unopposed*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: panchayat elections are run by the State Election Commission (Art. 243K, 73rd Amendment 1992), NOT by the Election Commission of India. Trap: the Upa-Sarpanch is chosen by the elected ward members, not directly by voters.

🏛️ *AP seeks ₹4,851 crore from the Centre for a cyclone-proof power network*
🎯 Exam point: Link to disaster management: AP's 974-km coastline (second-longest in India after Gujarat) makes it cyclone-prone; 'resilient infrastructure' = build back better. Trap: the ₹4,851 crore is only a PROPOSAL — not yet sanctioned.

🏛️ *Gandikota to become South India's adventure sports hub — MoU with NIMAS*
🎯 Exam point: AP geography link: Gandikota — the 'Grand Canyon of India' — is a gorge cut by the Penna (Pennar) river through the Erramala hills in YSR Kadapa district. Trap: it is on the Penna, not the Krishna or Tungabhadra.

🌿 *Northeast winds bring rain to AP; a low-pressure system may form in the Bay of Bengal*
🎯 Exam point: Static link: Oct–Dec is the northeast (retreating) monsoon — the main rain season for south coastal AP and Rayalaseema. IMD stages: low → depression → deep depression → cyclonic storm. Trap: Yanam lies inside AP's East Godavari region but belongs to the UT of Puducherry.

⚖️ *Constitution of India released in all 22 Eighth Schedule languages for the first time*
🎯 Exam point: Memory: 14 + 1 + 3 + 4 = 22. Constitution Day is observed since 2015 (adoption on 26 Nov 1949). Trap: English is NOT in the Eighth Schedule; official language provisions are Articles 343–351 (Part XVII).

💰 *RBI eases daily CRR rule: banks need keep only 90% of CRR on any day (was 99%)*
🎯 Exam point: Trap: this is NOT a CRR cut — the 3% rate is the same; only the daily flexibility changed. CRR earns no interest and is kept with the RBI; SLR is kept by banks themselves in cash, gold or G-secs.

🌏 *India hosts the 25th IORA Council of Ministers in New Delhi*
🎯 Exam point: Memory: theme initials = I-O-R-A. Trap: IORA's secretariat is in Mauritius (not India), and MAHASAGAR (2025) is the upgraded form of SAGAR (2015).

🌏 *India to host the ITU Plenipotentiary Conference (PP-30) in 2030*
🎯 Exam point: Static: ITU (founded 1865) is the UN specialised agency for ICT and the oldest UN agency; HQ Geneva. Trap: the conference just held was in Doha — India hosts the NEXT one (2030), not this one.

💰 *Sivok–Rangpo rail line: longest tunnel (T-10, 5.3 km) breaks through; all 14 tunnels dug*
🎯 Exam point: Map link: the line follows the Teesta valley from Sivok (near Siliguri, West Bengal) to Rangpo (Sikkim). Trap: Pakyong district is in Sikkim; Darjeeling and Kalimpong are in West Bengal.

🩺 *WHO: India unlikely to meet the 2026 measles–rubella elimination target, despite big gains*
🎯 Exam point: Static: measles and rubella are viral diseases; rubella in pregnancy causes Congenital Rubella Syndrome. The MR vaccine is given free under the Universal Immunisation Programme (UIP). Trap: high coverage ≠ elimination — elimination needs 3 years of zero local transmission.
