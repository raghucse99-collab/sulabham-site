📖 Explanations:
1. (b) (b). I: panchayat polls are run by the SEC (Art. 243K), not the ECI. III: 1,171 unopposed, 3,803 went to poll. II is the trap — the Upa-Sarpanch is chosen by the elected ward members on poll day, not directly by voters.
2. (d) (d). 1967 (21st Amd) – Sindhi; 1992 (71st) – Konkani, Manipuri, Nepali; 2003 (92nd) – Bodo, Dogri, Maithili, Santali. 14+1+3+4 = 22. Trap (a): swapping the 1992 and 2003 groups.
3. (c) (c) is incorrect — English is NOT in the Eighth Schedule (it is used under Art. 343(2)/Art. 348). The VP released the editions at Vigyan Bhawan on 26 Nov; the Legislative Department organised it.
4. (a) (a). Only the daily floor changed (99% → 90%, from the fortnight starting 1 Dec); the fortnightly average must be full. I is the trap — CRR stays at 3%; nothing was cut.
5. (d) (d). All three are correct: HQ Ebene (Mauritius), founded 7 March 1997, 23 members + 12 dialogue partners, India Chair 2025–27 (Jaishankar chaired the 25th CoM in New Delhi). Trap: assuming the secretariat is in India because India is Chair.
6. (b) (b). PP-26 was held in Doha (Qatar); India hosts PP-30 in 2030; WTSA-28 goes to Samarkand (Uzbekistan); ITU HQ is Geneva. Trap (a): thinking India hosted the 2026 conference.
7. (c) (c). I and III are correct (all 14 tunnels now excavated; 23 bridges). II is wrong — Pakyong is in Sikkim; Darjeeling and Kalimpong are the West Bengal districts.
8. (c) (c). A is true (WHO: new targets — interrupt by 2029, verify by 2032). R is false — coverage is 98% (dose 1) and 95% (dose 2). The gap is in remote pockets, hesitancy and the rule of 3 straight years without local spread.
9. (a) (a). Cyclonic circulation over the south Andaman Sea (29 Nov) → low-pressure area (30 Nov) → depression (by 2 Dec). A depression is stronger than a low; next stages would be deep depression and cyclonic storm.
10. (b) (b). I: Gandikota (YSR Kadapa) is the Penna gorge. III: Centre 90 : State 10, over 2027-28 to 2029-30. II is the trap — NIMAS is under the Ministry of Defence, even though AP's Tourism Minister attended the signing.
