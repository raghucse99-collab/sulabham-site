AP DSC-2026 (APTRT) · SGT · Part III: Educational Psychology (16 questions, 8 marks) · Updated 9 Nov 2026

Educational Psychology is now taught as four day-wise lessons that cover all four units of the official SCERT syllabus. Every topic has the 9-step explanation, real photos or diagrams, a 5-question tap-to-check quiz, and each day ends with a Notebook page.

Day-wise lessons

Day Unit Topics
Day 1: Development of the Child Unit 1 Growth, development, maturation; principles, factors, stages; Piaget, Vygotsky, Kohlberg, Erikson, Chomsky, Carl Rogers
Day 2: Individual Differences Unit 2 Inter/intra differences; theories of intelligence; IQ and tests (CAVD); levels of intellectual disability; EQ (Goleman); creativity; aptitude, attitude, interest, habit
Day 3: Learning Unit 3 Thorndike, Pavlov, Skinner, Kohler, Bandura, Bruner; factors; Gagné's phases and types; Bloom/Krathwohl/Dave domains; curves; transfer; assessment
Day 4: Memory, Motivation, Personality, Special Needs Units 3–4 Memory and forgetting; motivation and Maslow; personality theories; Freud; assessment; mental health and stress; defence mechanisms; inclusive education; hindrances to learning

Test yourself: after Day 4, take the Week 1-2 model test (40 questions), written on the pattern of the 2025 paper.
