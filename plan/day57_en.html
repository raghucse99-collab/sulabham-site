HISTORY · Indian culture: architecture, painting, dance, music, theatre, crafts and heritage

Understand it first

What is it? Culture is a people's whole way of life passed on through generations: beliefs, arts, language, food, festivals, crafts and knowledge. Exams ask it in three ways: (1) identify a form from its features (e.g. 'tribhanga posture' → Odissi), (2) match a form or monument with a State, dynasty or patron, and (3) heritage lists (UNESCO, GI tags, Sangeet Natak Akademi). The Ministry of Culture, Sangeet Natak Akademi (1952), Lalit Kala Akademi (1954), Sahitya Akademi (1954) and the Archaeological Survey of India (1861, Alexander Cunningham) protect and promote it. Art 29 protects the culture of every section; Art 49 asks the State to protect monuments of national importance; Art 51A(f) makes it a duty to value and preserve our composite culture.

1. Architecture and painting

  • Temple styles: Nagara (north; curved shikhara; sub-styles: Odisha – Konark, Lingaraj; Khajuraho – Chandellas), Dravida (south; pyramidal vimana, tall gopurams, enclosed courtyard; Pallava → Chola: Brihadeeswara, Thanjavur by Rajaraja I), Vesara (Deccan mix: Chalukya, Hoysala). Buddhist: stupa (Sanchi, Amaravati, Nagarjunakonda), chaitya (prayer hall; Karle) and vihara (monastery; Ajanta). Indo-Islamic: true arch and dome, pietra dura (Taj Mahal), jaali; Qutb Minar, Gol Gumbaz, Charminar.
  • Painting: Bhimbetka rock shelters (prehistoric; UNESCO); Ajanta murals (mostly Gupta–Vakataka era; Bodhisattva Padmapani); Lepakshi ceiling murals (Vijayanagara); miniatures: Mughal (Akbar's atelier; *Hamzanama*), Rajasthani/Rajput (Mewar, Kishangarh – 'Bani Thani'), Pahari (Basohli, Kangra); Deccani; Company painting (British era). Folk/regional: Madhubani (Bihar), Warli (Maharashtra tribes), Pattachitra (Odisha), Gond (MP), Tanjore (gold foil), Cheriyal scrolls (Telangana) and Kalamkari (AP).
Bodhisattva Padmapani, Ajanta Cave 1: a masterpiece of ancient Indian mural painting. (Photo: Wikimedia Commons, free licence)

2. Dance, music and theatre

The eight classical dances recognised by the Sangeet Natak Akademi
AreaKey points
Classical danceBased on Bharata's Natyashastra; elements nritta (pure dance), nritya (expressive), natya (drama); eight recognised by the Sangeet Natak Akademi: Bharatanatyam (TN), Kuchipudi (AP; Siddhendra Yogi; *Bhama Kalapam*; dancing on the rim of a brass plate in Tarangam), Kathakali and Mohiniyattam (Kerala), Kathak (UP), Odissi (Odisha; tribhanga), Manipuri (Raas Lila), Sattriya (Assam; Sankardeva; added 2000); the Ministry of Culture also lists Chhau
MusicHindustani (north; Persian influence; forms dhrupad, khayal, thumri; gharanas) and Carnatic (south; kriti-based; Purandara Dasa – 'father of Carnatic music'); the Carnatic Trinity: Tyagaraja (Telugu kritis; Tiruvaiyaru), Muthuswami Dikshitar, Syama Sastri; Annamacharya (Tallapaka; ~32,000 sankirtanas on Lord Venkateswara), Kshetrayya (padams), Ramadasu (Bhadrachalam); instruments: veena (Bobbili), mridangam, nadaswaram
Theatre and folk artsKutiyattam (Sanskrit theatre, Kerala; UNESCO), Yakshagana (Karnataka), Jatra (Bengal), Tamasha (Maharashtra), Bhavai (Gujarat); AP: Burrakatha, Harikatha, Veedhi Natakam, Tholu Bommalata (leather shadow puppets), Chindu Yakshaganam, tribal Dhimsa; Surabhi family theatre

3. Crafts, heritage lists and Andhra Pradesh

  • UNESCO lists: India has 45 World Heritage Sites (latest: Maratha Military Landscapes, 2025; Sarnath, 2026) and 16 Intangible Cultural Heritage elements, including Vedic chanting, Ramlila, Kutiyattam, Yoga (2016), Kumbh Mela (2017), Durga Puja in Kolkata (2021), Garba (2023) and Deepavali (Dec 2025). Telugu-region sites: Ramappa temple (Telangana, 2021); Lepakshi and others on AP's tentative list.
  • Andhra Pradesh's crafts and GI tags: Kalamkari – Srikalahasti (free-hand pen style) and Machilipatnam/Pedana (block print); Kondapalli and Etikoppaka toys; Bobbili veena; handlooms: Uppada Jamdani, Mangalagiri, Venkatagiri, Dharmavaram silk, Ponduru Khadi; Budithi bell-metal; Araku coffee; architecture: Amaravati stupa, Lepakshi (hanging pillar, monolithic Nandi), Srisailam, Draksharama, Ahobilam; festivals: Sankranti (kodi pandalu, Haridasu), Ugadi, Godavari and Krishna Pushkaralu (every 12 years).

Other angles asked in exams

Other angles asked: (1) Mudras (hand gestures) and rasas (nine emotions: navarasa) come from the Natyashastra. (2) Kathakali's make-up types: pacha (green, noble), kathi (villain), thadi (beard), minukku (women, sages). (3) Raga–tala: raga = melody framework; tala = rhythm cycle. (4) Pietra dura reached its height under Shah Jahan. (5) The Ashtadiggajas and Annamacharya link Telugu literature with music. (6) Bhakti saints and music: Meera, Surdas, Tulsidas (Hindustani); Purandara Dasa, Annamacharya (Carnatic). (7) Lalit Kala Akademi handles visual arts; Sahitya Akademi literature (24 languages). (8) Project Mausam links Indian Ocean cultures; Ek Bharat Shreshtha Bharat (2015) pairs States for cultural exchange.

Exam trap

Art 29, 49, 51A(f); Sangeet Natak 1952, Lalit Kala 1954, Sahitya 1954, ASI 1861 (Cunningham); Nagara (shikhara; Konark, Khajuraho), Dravida (vimana, gopuram; Brihadeeswara – Rajaraja I), Vesara (Chalukya, Hoysala); stupa, chaitya, vihara; Indo-Islamic arch, dome, pietra dura; Bhimbetka; Ajanta (Padmapani); Lepakshi murals; Mughal, Rajput (Bani Thani), Pahari (Kangra); Madhubani, Warli, Pattachitra, Gond, Tanjore, Cheriyal, Kalamkari; Natyashastra; 8 SNA dances: Bharatanatyam, Kuchipudi (Siddhendra Yogi; Bhama Kalapam; Tarangam), Kathakali, Mohiniyattam, Kathak, Odissi (tribhanga), Manipuri, Sattriya (2000); Chhau (Ministry); Hindustani (dhrupad, khayal, thumri) vs Carnatic (Purandara Dasa); Trinity: Tyagaraja, Dikshitar, Syama Sastri; Annamacharya ~32,000; Kshetrayya; Ramadasu; Kutiyattam, Yakshagana, Jatra, Tamasha, Bhavai; AP: Burrakatha, Tholu Bommalata, Veedhi Natakam, Dhimsa, Surabhi; UNESCO: 45 sites (Maratha 2025, Sarnath 2026); 16 ICH (Yoga 2016, Kumbh 2017, Durga Puja 2021, Garba 2023, Deepavali 2025); Ramappa 2021; AP GI: Kalamkari (Srikalahasti, Machilipatnam), Kondapalli, Etikoppaka, Bobbili veena, Uppada, Mangalagiri, Venkatagiri, Dharmavaram, Ponduru, Budithi, Araku coffee; Pushkaralu every 12 yrs.
