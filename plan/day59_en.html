Understand it first

How to use today (Day 59, revision phase): (1) Read the four master sheets below: they put the latest numbers (Economic Survey 2025-26, Union Budget 2026-27, the new GDP and CPI series, RBI's October 2026 policy, the 16th Finance Commission, AP Budget 2026-27) next to the concepts from Days 41–53. Tick what you remember; re-open Day notes only for unticked points. (2) Answer the 10 current-trend questions. (3) Take the 30-question Economy test 4 at the end (60 minutes). (4) One month of current affairs. Full-length 150-question mocks start from Day 61.

Other angles asked in exams

How APPSC now asks Economy (trend from recent Group 1 and Group 2 papers): (1) Long statement sets (3–4 statements, 'which are correct') built on the latest Survey and Budget; (2) AP-specific numbers: AP Budget, GSDP growth, sector shares, flagship schemes; (3) concept + current fact pairs: e.g. 'what is a debt anchor' with 'what did Budget 2026-27 adopt'; (4) match and how-many-pairs on schemes, committees and institutions; (5) assertion–reason on cause and effect (repo rate → rupee, base year → growth). Learn each number with its year and its source, because options often change only the year.
