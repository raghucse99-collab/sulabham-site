Understand it first

How to use today (Day 60, revision phase): (1) Read the three master sheets (Science and Technology, Environment, Society). Each puts the 2025–26 current-affairs facts beside the concepts from earlier days, because APPSC now joins the two in one question. (2) Answer the 10 current-trend questions. (3) Sit Mini Mock 1: 50 questions from all subjects in 50 minutes, in one sitting, as a dress rehearsal for the full-length 150-question mocks from Day 61. (4) One month of current affairs.

Other angles asked in exams

How these subjects are asked now: Science questions are application-based (which lens, which vitamin, why ice floats) rather than definitions; space and technology questions combine a mission with its feature (NISAR → radar → sees through clouds); Environment questions use conventions with their subject and year, COP outcomes city-wise, and protected areas with their State; Society questions use constitutional articles, Acts with years, and new schemes (Census 2027, VB-G RAM G). Learn every item as a pair: name + feature, place + State, Act + year.

MINI MOCK 1 · 50 questions from all subjects (50 minutes)

Understand it first

Exam conditions: one sitting, 50 minutes, no notes; mark answers on paper first, then tap to check. +1 correct, −1/3 wrong. Target: 35+. Subject mix as in the real paper: History 8, Polity 8, Geography 6, Economy 5, Science and Technology 10, Environment 8, Society 5. About two-thirds are statement, match, pair and assertion questions; about one in five uses 2025–26 developments. Strategy: first pass, answer what you are sure of (about 30 minutes); second pass, eliminate options in the rest; leave a question blank only if you cannot remove even two options.
