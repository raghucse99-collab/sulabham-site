Reasoning (Days 1–6)

  • Number series: check differences, then second differences, squares and cubes (n² ± 1), ×2 + 1 patterns and alternate series.
  • Letter series and odd man out: write A = 1 … Z = 26 and the reverse (A ↔ Z = 27); look for the skip pattern; odd one out by a property (prime, square, vowel position).
  • Coding–decoding: letter shift (+1, −1), reverse alphabet, position numbers, word-to-word substitution.
  • Blood relations: draw a family tree with + (male) and − (female); 'only daughter of my mother' means the speaker herself (if female).
  • Counting figures: for a triangle with n lines from the apex to the base, triangles = n(n + 1)/2 where n is the number of base segments.
  • Statement and assumption: an assumption is what the speaker takes for granted; it must be implicit, not a conclusion.
